Whose Land, Whose Water? · adapted version

When a government makes promises to Indigenous peoples, who decides what happens to the land and the water? Eight lessons you can do alone: SEGPA, CAP, CFA and Bac pro.

Road map

Eight lessons of 55 minutes, each timed for 52 minutes. Every lesson has the same steps, a stopping point at minute 44, an 8-minute emergency exit, a written record, a moment when you speak, and homework.

  1. Lesson 1. Look Before You Judge 52 min

    I can name the three Indigenous peoples and say that land can be kin, not only a resource.

  2. Lesson 2. The White Paper and the Red Paper 52 min

    I can say what the government was going to do in 1969, and how First Nations answered.

  3. Lesson 3. Rivers into Reservoirs 52 min

    I can say why a village had to move after a dam was built.

  4. Lesson 4. Dams Today 52 min

    I can give one fact about Muskrat Falls and one fact about Site C.

  5. Lesson 5. Pipelines on Unceded Land 52 min

    I can say that consultation is not the same as consent.

  6. Lesson 6. Promises Kept, Promises Broken 52 min

    I can use two official figures, with their date and source, to judge a promise.

  7. Lesson 7. The Grizzlies 52 min

    I can say what the film is about and why a team can become a family.

  8. Lesson 8. Final Task: A Citizens’ Hearing and an Open Letter 52 min

    I can present one promise, one fact and one request, even if I work alone.

  • Written record. Each lesson ends with a short written record for your route.
  • Participation. You speak at least once, even if you work alone: you say the speaking frame aloud.
  • Homework for next lesson. Each lesson page states the homework.

Stopping point. Minute 44, after the speaking frame.

Emergency exit · 8 min. A condensed ending inside the 52 minutes still leaves you with the written record, one spoken sentence, and the homework.

How this version works

This is not the same text in a larger font. Texts are shorter. Instructions come one step at a time. Each exercise starts with a corrected worked example. You check your work with a visible self-check key. You can use the page alone, without an AESH, even if you are absent from class.

Dyslexia support does not mean a lower language level. Your route sets the amount of help and the length of the text, not a ceiling written into an official programme.

RouteWho it is forLanguage aim
SSEGPA (collège)A1 towards A2. This is a teaching route, not an official ceiling.
CCAPA2, the examination target.
CFAA training centre (CFA is a setting, not a CEFR level)Follow route C or route P, then do the CFA task instead of Exercise 3.
PBac proSupported B1, towards B1+ in LVA / B1 in LVB.

For bac pro, the vocational English programme is still BO spécial n° 5 of 11 April 2019 (arrêté of 3 April 2019). The official level of the standard sequence remains Première générale, LVA B1+, LVB B1.

How to do an exercise

  1. Read the short text for your route. Use Listen if you want. You do not need an internet search.
  2. Read the worked example. It is already corrected.
  3. Do the task.
  4. Self-check with the answer key. Correct one mistake if you need to.
  5. Say one sentence aloud.

No partner, AESH, printer or recording is required. Videos on the standard pages are optional and are not needed here: the short text is enough.
