Appendix A. CFA pathway: hair and beauty

For: apprentices preparing the CAP Métiers de la coiffure, the BP Coiffure, the CAP Esthétique, cosmétique, parfumerie, the BP Esthétique, cosmétique, parfumerie, and students of the bacs pro Métiers de la coiffure and Esthétique cosmétique parfumerie.
How to use it: after Lessons 1, 2 and 3. One lesson of 55 minutes (planned over 52). Lessons 7 and 8 complete the portrait.
Debatable question: Is a “Dolly look” a costume or a real service you can sell?

For the teacher: timing (52 minutes)
Step Time Activity
1 0-4 Recap
2 4-12 Expanding your vocabulary: hair and beauty
3 12-18 The teacher’s point: questions to a client, will
4 18-26 Exercises
5 26-44 Role-play with a client (every apprentice plays both roles)
6 44-50 Written task: a service description
7 50-52 Homework

Stop point (minute 44). Every apprentice has played the professional.
Emergency exit (8 minutes). Each apprentice asks the client five questions (consultation only), then writes the price and time line of the service description.

Step 1. Recap

  1. Three words for hair and three for make-up from Lesson 2. 2. Make it polite: I want more volume. 3. What colour was Dolly’s eyeshadow in the Warhol portrait?

    Answer key

    Answer key: 1 wig, curls, volume; foundation, eyeshadow, false lashes · 2 Could you add more volume, please? · 3 lilac.

Step 2. Expanding your vocabulary

Hair

Word Meaning Example
a wig / a hairpiece false hair for the whole head / for part of it Dolly wore a wig on stage.
extensions hair added to make it longer or thicker Extensions add volume.
styling giving a shape to the hair Wig styling takes time.
a blow-dry drying and styling with a hairdryer and a brush I’ll start with a blow-dry.
volume the impression of a lot of hair We need more volume on top.
curls / rollers / a curling iron round locks / tools to make curls Hot rollers give big curls.
bleaching making hair lighter with a product Platinum blonde needs bleaching.
highlights lighter strands of hair Golden highlights look warm.
roots the part of the hair near the scalp Your roots are darker.
a toner a product that corrects the shade A toner removes yellow tones.
hairspray a spray that keeps the style in place Use hairspray for the stage.

Beauty

Word Meaning Example
foundation cream that evens the skin colour Choose a foundation for your skin type.
concealer product that hides dark circles or spots A little concealer under the eyes.
contouring shaping the face with darker and lighter shades Contouring makes the cheekbones stand out.
highlighter a shiny product for the top of the cheeks Highlighter catches the stage lights.
eyeshadow / eyeliner colour on the eyelids / a line along the lashes Lilac eyeshadow, black eyeliner.
false lashes / lash glue artificial eyelashes / the glue for them False lashes need a patch test.
nail extensions artificial long nails Dolly wore long acrylic nails.
stage make-up strong make-up for lights and cameras Stage make-up must be bright.
a patch test testing a product on a small area of skin first We do a patch test 48 hours before.
skin type dry, oily, combination or sensitive skin What is your skin type?

Words to sell a service: It’s ideal for... · It includes... · It will last... · I’d recommend... · Please note that...

Exercise 1. Hair or beauty? Sort: rollers, concealer, toner, false lashes, highlights, contouring, hairspray, foundation.

Answer key

Answer key: Hair: rollers, toner, highlights, hairspray · Beauty: concealer, false lashes, contouring, foundation.

Exercise 2. Fill in the gaps: patch test · skin type · roots · toner
Before the lashes, we need a (1) __. What is your (2) _? Your (3) are darker, so we’ll use a (4) ___ after bleaching.

Answer key

Answer key: 1 patch test · 2 skin type · 3 roots · 4 toner.

Flashcards (model). a patch test · test cutané · We do a patch test 48 hours before. / It will take about + time (red) · It will take about an hour.

Step 3. The teacher’s point

Teacher script. Play the professional with one apprentice as the client. Ask five questions. The group notes the questions, then classifies them yes/no or wh-.

Point Example
Yes/no questions Do you have any allergies? Is it for a special occasion? Have you ever worn a wig?
Wh- questions What look would you like? How long do you have? When is the concert?
Offers and advice Would you like me to add volume? I’d recommend a patch test. It might look better if we use a lighter shade.
will: time and result It will take about an hour. Your curls will last all night.

Quick check. Make a question: you / allergies / have → Do you have any allergies?

Step 4. Exercises

Exercise 3. The steps of a Dolly look. Put in order: a. add false lashes · b. apply foundation · c. put on the wig and add hairspray · d. welcome the client and do the consultation · e. apply eyeshadow and lipstick

Answer key

Answer key: d, b, e, a, c (c before b is accepted if justified: some salons do the hair first).

Exercise 4. Questions for the client. Write the questions: 1 occasion? 2 allergies? 3 skin type? 4 wig or own hair? 5 time available?

Answer key

Answer key: 1 What is the occasion? 2 Do you have any allergies? 3 What is your skin type? 4 Would you like a wig or would you rather use your own hair? 5 How much time do you have?

Exercise 5. Transform with will. 1 The service / take / ninety minutes. 2 The curls / last / all night.

Answer key

Answer key: 1 The service will take ninety minutes. 2 The curls will last all night.

Step 5. Role-play with a client

Situation. A client comes to your salon. She is going to a Dolly Parton tribute concert on Saturday night and wants “a Dolly look”: big hair and stage make-up.

Role card A · The client
1. Explain the occasion and what you want.
2. Answer the questions (hair, skin, allergies).
3. Ask about the price and the time.
4. Accept or refuse a suggestion politely.

Role card B · The professional
1. Welcome the client and ask about the occasion.
2. Ask about allergies and skin or hair type; suggest a patch test.
3. Suggest a service: wig or styling, stage make-up, lashes.
4. Give the price and the time, and book the appointment.

Help: Hello, can I help you? · Do you have allergies? · It’s ... euros. · I want... · How much is it?
Core: Would you like me to...? · What about false lashes? · It will take about... · I’d rather...
Going further: I’d recommend a patch test before... · With your skin type, I’d suggest... · Would it be possible to...?

Professional words: appointment, consultation, patch test, allergy, skin type, hair type, price list, booking, aftercare.

Model dialogue (read it after your own performance)
Professional: Good morning! How can I help you?
Client: I’m going to a Dolly Parton tribute concert. I’d like a Dolly look!
Professional: Great idea. Would you like me to style your hair or would you rather wear a wig?
Client: A wig, please. Something big and platinum.
Professional: Perfect. For the make-up, do you have any allergies? I’d recommend a patch test for the lash glue.
Client: No allergies. How much is it and how long will it take?
Professional: The wig styling and stage make-up package is 75 euros and it takes about an hour and a half. Shall I book you for Saturday at four?
Client: Yes, please. I love the idea!

Apprentices can also play Scene 2 (hairdresser) or Scene 3 (make-up artist) of Lesson 3.

Step 6. Written task: a service description (60 to 80 words)

Name of the service, what it includes, who it is for, the time, the price, one precaution.
Help frame: The ... package includes ... It is perfect for ... It takes ... It costs ... Please note: ...
Model answer: The Dolly Look: stage hair and make-up package. Ready for a tribute night? Our team styles a platinum wig with big soft curls and lots of volume, then creates a bright stage make-up: contouring, lilac eyeshadow, false lashes and red lips. Perfect for concerts, parties and photo shoots. Time: about 90 minutes. Price: 75 euros. Please note: a patch test is required 48 hours before for the lash glue.

Assessment grid (CFA)

Criterion Not yet Nearly Reached Beyond
Interaction with the client I read my card short answers I answer and ask questions I advise, negotiate, book
Questions and offers none Can I / I want Do you have...? / Would you like me to...? I’d recommend / With your skin type, I’d suggest
Professional vocabulary fewer than 3 words 3 to 5 6 to 9 10 or more, precisely
Health and safety (patch test, allergies) not mentioned mentioned explained explained with the time (48 h)
Language: vocabulary reused and syntax many errors errors in questions questions and will correct varied and correct

Self-correction grid (CFA). Same lines; columns: What I did / What I missed / My improved version. Rewrite your service description following the grid.

Homework

  1. Learn ten words from each vocabulary table (flashcards).
  2. Write a second service description (for example “Rhinestone nails” or “Big hair blow-dry”).
  3. Record yourself welcoming a client (one minute).

Passing the torch. Dolly said her style was “a country girl’s idea of glam”. Behind it, there were professionals: wig makers, hairdressers, make-up artists. Which part of her look would you like to recreate in your salon?
