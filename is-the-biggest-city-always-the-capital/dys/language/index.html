WordMeaning

Locating precisely: not far from, at the far end of, halfway between, on the banks of, off the coast of, about 160 km from.

2. Lexicon check

Complete with: compromise, from scratch, seat, rivals, crowded, harbour.

Sydney and Melbourne were ______, so Australia chose a ______: a new capital. Abuja was built ______ because Lagos was too ______. Wellington was chosen for its central position and its ______. Pretoria is the ______ of the South African government.

Answer key

rivals, compromise, from scratch, crowded, harbour, seat

3. Grammar: complex comparatives

StructureExample
twice as big as / X times as big asHouston is more than twice as big as Austin.
by far the biggestToronto is by far the biggest city in Canada.
nowhere near as big asAlbany is nowhere near as big as New York City.
much / far smaller thanJuneau is much smaller than Anchorage.
the more…, the more…The bigger the city, the more expensive the housing.

Exercise 1

  1. Los Angeles (3.9 million) / Sacramento (525,000): times as big as
  2. Toronto / the other Canadian cities: by far
  3. Abuja / Lagos: nowhere near as… as
  4. The bigger the capital / the higher the rents

Answer key

  1. Los Angeles is more than seven times as big as Sacramento.
  2. Toronto is by far the biggest city in Canada.
  3. Abuja is nowhere near as big as Lagos.
  4. The bigger the capital, the higher the rents.

4. Grammar: the passive

Form: be + past participle. Use it when the result matters more than the person: Canberra was named in 1913. The Union Buildings were completed in 1913. New Delhi was inaugurated in 1931.

Exercise 2

  1. Queen Victoria chose Ottawa in 1857.
  2. Two British architects planned New Delhi.
  3. They discovered gold near Johannesburg in 1886.
  4. A decree created the Federal Capital Territory in 1976.

Answer key

  1. Ottawa was chosen (by Queen Victoria) in 1857.
  2. New Delhi was planned by two British architects.
  3. Gold was discovered near Johannesburg in 1886.
  4. The Federal Capital Territory was created (by a decree) in 1976.

5. Grammar: used to, whose and connectors

Used to + base verb: a past situation that is no longer true. Auckland used to be the capital.

Whose: possession in a relative clause. Canberra is a city whose name probably comes from an Aboriginal word.

Connectors: moreover, besides (adding); as a result, consequently (consequence); whereas, however, yet (contrast).

Exercise 3

  1. Calcutta ______ the capital of British India.
  2. Johannesburg is a city ______ wealth comes from gold.
  3. Lagos was crowded. ______, the government decided to build Abuja.
  4. Toronto is the biggest city, ______ Ottawa is the capital.

Answer key

  1. used to be
  2. whose
  3. As a result / Consequently
  4. whereas

6. Pronunciation

The -ate ending: /eɪt/ in many verbs (to relocate, to debate); /ɪt/ in many nouns and adjectives (the Senate, climate, a candidate).

Stress: CANberra (on 12 March 1913, Lady Denman said: “the accent is on the Can”), WELlington, aBUja, OTtawa, HOUSton. Silent letters: Illinoi(s), Arkansa(s).
