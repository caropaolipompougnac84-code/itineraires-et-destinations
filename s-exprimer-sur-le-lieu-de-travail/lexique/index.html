Building your vocabulary · Expanding your vocabulary

Banques lexicales par métier

Pour l'enseignant

Ces listes complètent les rubriques Building your vocabulary et Expanding your vocabulary des leçons. Elles sont destinées à des onglets sur le site. L'élève n'apprend pas tout. Il choisit, avec l'enseignant, les mots de son lieu de PFMP, et il les réemploie dans des phrases.

Cœur commun, tous métiers

workshop or workplace, workbench, cardboard box, window, tutor, colleague, PFMP student, apprentice, customer, visitor, order, task, problem, solution, safety, gloves, safety shoes, locker, delivery, team, to check, to prepare, to repair, to explain, to welcome, to apologise, at the moment, immediately, over there, I'm afraid, I'm sorry, Could you... please?

Maintenance et industriel

screwdriver, spanner, pliers, spare part, engine, cable, switch, leak, machine, to switch on, to switch off, to replace, to assemble, to measure, a fault, a check, It's broken, The machine stopped.

Bâtiment

helmet, drill, level, tape measure, plan, brick, load, site, scaffolding, delivery, to cut, to build, to carry, to measure, site supervisor, The delivery is late, Don't walk under the scaffolding.

Vente et accueil

customer, counter, shelf, stock, receipt, exchange, refund, size, to serve, to recommend, to pack, to exchange, How can I help you?, Would you like..., I'm sorry about the delay, We can exchange it.

Restauration

kitchen, worktop, tray, knife, order, allergen, hygiene, starter, dish, to cook, to serve, to clean, to wash your hands, table number, I ordered..., We can prepare a new dish.
