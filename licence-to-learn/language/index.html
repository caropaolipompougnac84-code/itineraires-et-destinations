Language study

The simple past, dates, obligation and prohibition, and the vocabulary of the sequence.

The simple past

Regular verbs add -ed (work, worked; play, played; star, starred). Irregular verbs must be learnt (be, was / were; grow, grew; become, became; win, won).

We write He was born on 2 June 1990. We say He was born on the second of June, nineteen ninety.

Must, have to, mustn’t, don’t have to

FormMeaningExample
must / have toobligationYou must be discreet. An MI6 officer has to travel.
mustn’tprohibitionYou mustn’t post photos of your job.
don’t have to / doesn’t have tono obligationYou don’t have to drive an Aston Martin. An MI5 officer doesn’t have to live abroad.

Mustn’t is not the same as don’t have to.
