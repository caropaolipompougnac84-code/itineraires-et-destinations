Model cards

These model cards follow the method on the flashcards page. Students make their own cards; they do not copy a finished set as the only work of the lesson.

Frontobservation or interpretation?
Back

An observation describes what is visible. An interpretation suggests a possible meaning: “The boy might feel lonely.”

Frontstewardship
Back

Care for a place and its future.

Frontrepeal
Back

Officially cancel a law. The White Paper proposed repealing the Indian Act.

FrontCitizens Plus
Back

The Red Paper, 1970: citizenship together with treaty and Indigenous rights.

Frontreservoir
Back

An artificial lake storing water. The project turned rivers into reservoirs.

Frontdischarge
Back

The volume of water passing a point over time, in cubic metres per second. Not speed.

Frontprediction or measurement?
Back

“Levels could increase” is a prediction. “The laboratory recorded this concentration” is a measurement.

Frontconsultation
Back

Meaningful discussion and consideration of concerns.

Frontconsent
Back

Agreement. Consultation and consent are different concepts.

Frontstake
Back

An ownership share. An offer of a stake is not a completed transfer.

Frontlift an advisory
Back

Officially end it. 158 advisories were lifted since November 2015 (snapshot of 10 September 2026).

Frontfailed to
Back

The government failed to meet the March 2021 deadline.

Frontagency
Back

The ability to act and influence events.

FrontPlaying together…
Back

The -ing form as a subject: “Playing together can strengthen relationships.”

Frontlimitation
Back

Something the evidence does not establish: “The figures concern only these systems.”

FrontWe recommend that…
Back

We recommend that the government provide clear, dated updates.

Figures on the cards are dated; check them before use.
