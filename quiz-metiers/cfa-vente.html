Teacher key: sentences at work
  1. What ________ are you? Medium or large? (size)
  2. The ________ is over there, on the left. (fitting room)
  3. Do you have a ________? You get ten per cent off. (loyalty card)
  4. Would you like to pay by card or in ________? (cash)
  5. You can have a ________ if you keep the receipt. (refund)
  6. It is a ________: two for the price of one. (special offer)

In steps 1, 2 and 3, the right answer is the line in the word list.
