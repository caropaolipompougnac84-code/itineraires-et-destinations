Lesson 4. Fiction vs reality

In the films the boss is M; in real life the boss of MI6 is C. Real intelligence work is often done with brains, not guns.

Road map

Lesson of 55 minutes, timed for 52 minutes. Six lessons of 55 minutes, each timed for 52 minutes. Lesson 6 is the final-task interview.

  1. Oral game: M, Q and C 8 min
  2. Read “Q becomes C”, then true or false 14 min
  3. Silent Courier, and a question 8 min
  4. Bletchley Park: brains, not guns 8 min
  5. Debate: is James Bond a good image of a real spy? 14 min
  • Written record. Copy: the film boss is M; the real boss of MI6 is C; only the chief’s name is public. Add one sentence from the debate.
  • Participation. Everyone speaks at least once: each student answers one M / Q / C prompt, or later gives one debate sentence.
  • Homework for next lesson. Write six to eight lines: “In my opinion, James Bond is / is not a good image of a real spy because…” Use two arguments.

Stopping point. After Silent Courier. If time is short, show the Bletchley photograph, take one debate sentence from each student, and set the written homework.

Emergency exit · 8 min. Dictate the three facts (M, C, names are secret). Each student says one debate sentence. Write the homework on the board.

Recap. Recite the mnemonic. Where is Thames House? Where is Vauxhall Cross?

For the teacher. The M / Q / C game works well orally. Insist on the fact that agents’ names are secret: only the chief is known. The debate can be run in two camps, “Bond is realistic” against “Bond is pure fiction”.

In the films The boss of MI6 is called M. The man with the gadgets is Q.
In real life The boss of MI6 is called C. MI6 also has a real Q.

Read. Q becomes C

In the Bond films, Q is the man who invents the gadgets. In real life, MI6 also has a “Q”: the director of technology and innovation.

In 2025, the real Q became C. Her name is Blaise Metreweli. She is the first woman to lead MI6 in its history. In the films, a woman, played by Judi Dench, was already the boss of MI6. Real life caught up with fiction.

The boss of MI5 is called the Director General. He is Sir Ken McCallum. The heads of the two services are the only members whose names are public. These facts were still current on the official sites of MI6 and MI5 in November 2026.

True or false?

  1. In real life, the head of MI6 is called M.
  2. Blaise Metreweli is the first woman at the head of MI6.
  3. Before, she was “Q”.
  4. The names of all MI6 agents are public.

Answer key

  1. False. The real chief is called C.
  2. True.
  3. True.
  4. False. Only the chief’s name is public.

Real spies today

In 2025, MI6 opened an online portal called Silent Courier. People can send secret information safely. MI6 explains how on its own YouTube channel.

Are real spies always in action like Bond? Or do they also work behind a screen?

Debate

Is James Bond a good image of a real spy?

I think… / In my opinion… / Real spies don’t… / In the films, Bond always… / I agree with… / I disagree because…

Written record and homework. Copy the three facts about M, C and public names, plus one debate sentence. Homework: six to eight lines with two arguments.
