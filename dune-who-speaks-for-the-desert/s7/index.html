Lesson 7. The Hero Trap

Step of the problématique: Je trouve le sens. Is the hero the voice of the people, or the voice that replaces them?

Timing (55 minutes, timed on 52)

Stopping point

After stage 4. The rehearsal and the draft can be finished at home.

Emergency exit (8 minutes)

Q7, Q8, and each student says the tip of the spiral in one sentence.

Stage 1. The last talks

The last two-minute mid-sequence talks, with one follow-up question each.

Stage 2. Listening: a portrait made of opposites (Q7)

Listen to Q7, read aloud twice. Note every pair of opposites. Then look at the written lines.

"warrior and mystic, ogre and saint, the fox and the innocent, chivalrous, ruthless, less than a god, more than a man"

Q7. Book III, a late epigraph by the Princess Irulan. The full epigraph is about five lines: check your edition.

B1 pathway

Classify the words of Q7 into "positive" and "negative", then say which side wins for you, in two sentences.

B2 pathway

Comment on the effect of the antitheses in four to five sentences.

Text intelligence. Q7 is a portrait made of opposites (oxymoron, antithesis): the historian cannot decide what Paul is.

Stage 3. Reading Q8: the threat

"The power to destroy a thing is the absolute control over it."

Q8. Book III, final chapter: Paul faces the Emperor and the Guild. Read the threat to the spice, and the Guild's reaction, in your edition.

Q8 shows the liberator using the language of total control, and of the destruction of the very resource that defined the planet. Link this with Q9: the leader understands consequences and uses them as a weapon. Has the hero become the new owner of the spice?

Teacher's note on Q8

Some sources give an earlier formulation, in Paul's thoughts: "He who can destroy a thing has the real control of it." Check which wording your edition uses, and where (spoken threat, or inner thought, or both). Use your edition with the class. Do not write the film line "The spice must flow" as if it belonged to this chapter: it does not belong to the novel.

Key, a direction for Q7 and Q8

B1, pairs: warrior / mystic; ogre / saint; the fox / the innocent; chivalrous / ruthless; less than a god / more than a man. Which side "wins" is a reasoned choice, not a single answer. B2: the antitheses refuse a simple portrait; Irulan's official history cannot close the question it opens, and that doubt is already a comment on power. Q8: the hero now speaks as a controller of the resource; the liberator uses destruction as a form of ownership.

Stage 4. Spiral presentations

Each group presents its spiral, from turn 1 to the tip, one minute per student. The tip is a sentence the group has reached, not a sentence given in advance. A possible kind of tip (for the teacher, not to be written on the board first): "A people can be saved by the story that enslaves it."

Stage 5. Council rehearsal

Practise questions for Lesson 8. Language for speech: Let me begin by... / I would like to stress that... / To put it simply... / I am convinced that... / To conclude...

Optional bridge, if the class knows the Orwell sequence (Terminale): the leader as a manufactured figure. Big Brother may not even exist; Paul exists, but his legend was written before him (Q4, Q7). Does it matter whether the leader is real, if the story is what people follow? A short question, not a second lesson.

Grammar. Comparison

as ... as, more ... than, the most, less than. Example (already corrected): Paul / powerful / the Emperor → Paul becomes more powerful than the Emperor. Notice Q7: "less than a god, more than a man".

Exercise 6.

  1. the Fremen / independent / before
  2. a legend / dangerous / an army
  3. Kynes's plan / slow / Paul's war
Key, exercise 6

a) The Fremen are not as independent as before / less independent than before (both are arguable). b) A legend can be more dangerous than an army. c) Kynes's plan is slower than Paul's war. Accept any reasoned order: the content is debatable, the structure must be correct.

Expanding your vocabulary

A hero, heroism, a trap, to be trapped, an antithesis, to blackmail, a threat, a leader, a follower, to obey, charisma.

Written trace and homework

The class tip is copied and signed by every group. Begin a draft of the final written statement (Lesson 8).

Homework. Finish the draft of the final statement. Rehearse the briefing with a timer (3 to 4 minutes). Record it once.
