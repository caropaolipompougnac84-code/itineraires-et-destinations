Lesson 3. Whose Planet? Empire and the Desert People

Step of the problématique: Je repère, then Je comprends. Who owns Arrakis: the Emperor who gives it, the family who rules it, or the people who live on it?

Timing (55 minutes, timed on 52)

Stopping point

After stage 3. The exposés can open the next lesson if time is short.

Emergency exit (8 minutes)

The dialogue, a short explanation of Q4, and one sentence from each student.

Stage 1. Flashcard duel

In pairs, test each other on the six flashcards of Lessons 1 and 2. One minute each, then swap.

Stage 2. A dialogue, written for the lesson

Two students read the dialogue aloud (the teacher may read it first). Then listen for: what the knife is; what the housekeeper is waiting for; what Jessica already knows.

Dialogue, written for the lesson (not a quotation from the novel)

This paraphrase is for listening and speaking. The exact scene is to be read in your edition (Book I, the crysknife in Arrakeen). Do not present these lines as Herbert's wording.

Mapes. My lady, this blade is sacred to my people. It is a crysknife.

Jessica. I know what it is. I have been taught something of the desert.

Mapes. Then you know we have been waiting. The legends speak of a mother and a son from the outer world.

Jessica (quietly, to herself): I know this legend. It was planted here, long ago, so that a woman of my order would be safe. I can use it, and perhaps I must.

Mapes. The signs have begun. Will you take the knife?

Stage 3. Reading Q4: the Missionaria Protectiva

The Missionaria Protectiva is, in the novel, the Bene Gesserit order that plants protective legends on worlds such as Arrakis, so that a sister of the order can later be received and protected. In the Arrakeen scene, Jessica realises that the housekeeper's words echo a prophecy, and she decides to use it.

The exact wording of the scene is to be read from the teacher's edition (Book I, the chapter in which Shadout Mapes shows Jessica the crysknife). What matters here is the idea: the religion of the people who live on Arrakis has been partly written by outsiders. This is the core of the problématique.

B1 pathway

Match each sentence to a speaker: the Emperor, the Duke, a Fremen, a Bene Gesserit.

  1. "I give this planet as a fief, and I can give it to someone else."
  2. "I am sent to rule this land and to keep the spice flowing into the empire's hands."
  3. "This is our desert. We have lived here for generations."
  4. "We planted a legend here, long ago, so that one of us would be safe."

Careful: "the spice flowing" in sentence 2 is ordinary English, not a film quotation, and not a line from the novel.

B2 pathway

Explain, with although and whereas, why the Fremen both believe and are manipulated.

Key, Q4 pathways

B1: 1 the Emperor; 2 the Duke (or a governor); 3 a Fremen; 4 a Bene Gesserit. B2, a possible direction: although the Fremen have a real faith and a real knowledge of the desert, the legend they wait for was partly planted by the Bene Gesserit, whereas the offworld families claim the planet as a fief. Belief and manipulation can occupy the same sentence.

Teacher's note on Q4

Quote the exact wording of the Missionaria Protectiva scene from your edition only. Do not invent a sentence and attribute it to Herbert. The key idea for the class: Jessica knows the legend was planted, and she chooses to use it. The religion of the "colonised" people has been partly written by outsiders.

A desert people has its own knowledge of water

Look at these two images of a foggara (a qanat): a traditional way of catching and sharing water in an arid land. Peoples of arid regions have their own knowledge. The Fremen are not "primitive", and the offworlders' view of them is a colonial view. Ask: who calls whom "primitive" in the novel, and why?

Stage 4. Who owns Arrakis?

In threes, one minute each, three positions: the Emperor who gives the planet, the family who rules it, the people who live on it. Every student speaks.

Grammar. Concession

Although, even though, whereas, however. Example (already corrected): The Fremen are strong. They are used by outsiders. → Although the Fremen are strong, they are used by outsiders.

Exercise 3.

  1. Jessica is a foreigner. The Fremen respect her.
  2. The legend helps Jessica. It was invented by her order.
  3. The Duke wants peace. The Emperor wants him dead.
Key, exercise 3

a) Although Jessica is a foreigner, the Fremen respect her. b) Even though the legend was invented by her order, it helps Jessica. (Or: The legend helps Jessica, although it was invented by her order.) c) The Duke wants peace, whereas the Emperor wants him dead. Accept however with a new sentence.

Expanding your vocabulary

To colonise, a coloniser, the colonised, to own, ownership, to rule, a native people, to exploit, a legend, to plant (an idea), to manipulate.

Words for nuance: partly, to some extent, on the one hand, on the other hand.

Written trace and homework

Complete: "The Missionaria Protectiva shows that the religion of Arrakis was partly written by outsiders. This means that..." (about 60 words). On the spiral, close Je repère and open Je comprends.

Homework. Record 45 seconds: "Who owns Arrakis? My view."
