Lesson 5. Pipelines on Unceded Land

Who speaks for a nation when a pipeline crosses the land?

Recap · 0 to 4 min

What is unceded land? Who objected to Site C?

Self-check

Land never given up by treaty. Treaty 8 nations, such as West Moberly.

4 to 8 min

Today’s goal

I can say that consultation is not the same as consent, and that one nation can be divided.

WordMeaning
consentsaying yes, with full information
to consultto ask people before deciding
a band councilan elected council under the Indian Act
a hereditary chiefa leader of the traditional system

Route S · SEGPA

I can: say who said yes and who said no to Coastal GasLink.

Coastal GasLink is a gas pipeline in British Columbia. It crosses Wet’suwet’en land. That land is unceded. Most band councils said yes. Most hereditary chiefs said no. They say they hold the title to the land. Trans Mountain is an oil pipeline. In 2018 a court said Canada had not consulted properly. In 2024 the new pipeline started. Consultation means asking. Consent means saying yes. They are not the same.

Route C · CAP

I can: explain the difference between consultation and consent with one example.

Twenty band councils signed agreements for Coastal GasLink, including five of the six Wet’suwet’en band councils. Most hereditary chiefs refused: they hold title to 22,000 km² of unceded territory, as the Supreme Court recognised in 1997. Police arrested protesters in 2019 and 2020. The pipeline was mechanically complete in November 2023. For Trans Mountain, the Federal Court of Appeal quashed the first approval in 2018 because officials ‘simply took notes’. In September 2026 Ottawa offered a 15 per cent stake; one leader said ‘consent is not for sale’. That offer was still in negotiation on 4 November 2026.

Route P · Bac pro

I can: use although or whereas to show a divided community.

The conflict is also about who may speak for a nation: an elected band council, created under the Indian Act for a reserve, or hereditary chiefs of a house and clan system that existed before contact with Europeans. Although twenty councils signed, most hereditary chiefs said no. Whereas some First Nations want a stake in Trans Mountain, others want no pipeline at all. The government has to respect court orders; many people say it ought to obtain consent, not only consultation.

Exercise 1

Choose: band council or hereditary chief.

Worked example (already corrected). Worked answer: Most hereditary chiefs of the Wet’suwet’en said no.

Your turn. Most ________ signed agreements with the company.

Self-check

Most band councils signed agreements with the company.

Exercise 2

Consultation or consent?

Worked example (already corrected). Worked answer: Asking people for their opinion is consultation.

Your turn. Saying yes, so that a project can go ahead, is ________.

Self-check

Saying yes, so that a project can go ahead, is consent.

Exercise 3

Write one sentence with although or whereas.

Worked example (already corrected). Worked answer: Although twenty band councils signed, most hereditary chiefs said no.

Your turn. Write your sentence about Coastal GasLink or Trans Mountain.

Self-check

Possible sentence: Some nations want a stake, whereas others say consent is not for sale.

CFA task · instead of Exercise 3

If you are in a training centre, do this task instead of Exercise 3. You may follow route C or route P for the reading.

Worked example (already corrected). A trainee prepares a meeting note: ‘We consulted the elected council. We do not yet have the consent of the hereditary chiefs.’

Your turn. Write three lines for a site meeting: who was asked, who has not agreed, and what should happen next.

Self-check

Possible answer: We have consulted the band council. The hereditary chiefs have not given consent. We ought to keep talking before any work starts.

Speaking frame · 40 to 44 min

Say this aloud, and change the words in brackets if you need to:

On unceded land, [band councils] and [hereditary chiefs] can disagree. Consultation is not the same as [consent].

Written record · 44 to 48 min

Route S. Most band councils said yes to Coastal GasLink; most hereditary chiefs said no. Consultation is not consent.

Route C. The 2018 court decision on Trans Mountain showed that taking notes is not meaningful consultation. Consent means a real yes.

Route P. Although councils signed, hereditary chiefs held title to unceded land. A stake is not automatically consent.

Progress record · 48 to 50 min

  • I read the short text for my route.
  • I did two exercises and checked the self-check key.
  • I said one sentence aloud.
  • I copied the written record.

Homework. Make a two-column table: two arguments for and two against one pipeline, with a source if you can.

Emergency exit · 8 min

  • 0 to 2. Say this sentence: “Consultation means asking; consent means a real yes.”
  • 2 to 5. Copy the written record for your route.
  • 5 to 7. Write down the homework.
  • 7 to 8. Check one self-check answer.
