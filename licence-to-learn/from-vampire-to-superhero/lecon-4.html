Leçon 4 · Évaluation de l'EOI · 1 h

Action! The Casting Call

Play the interview, convince a sceptical director, evaluate and vote as a jury.

Déroulé

  1. 40 minMise en scènePassage des binômes devant la classe, ou en îlots filmés sur tablette. Le bureau du professeur devient le bureau du réalisateur à Hollywood.
  2. 15 minRôle du public (médiation)Grille “Would you hire him? Yes / No, Why?” et vote à main levée après chaque passage.

1. Action!

The teacher's desk is now the director's office in Hollywood. Each pair plays the interview for three to four minutes. You can also film it on a tablet in small groups.

2. The jury

While two students play, you are the jury. Fill in the grid, then vote with your hand.

PairDid he answer every question?Did he use examples from his career?Was he confident?Did he use soft skills words?Would you hire him? Yes / No, Why?
      
      
      
      
      

Étude de la langue

Vocabulary

  • an interview: un entretien
  • a director: un réalisateur
  • a jury: un jury
  • to convince: convaincre
  • to hire: embaucher
  • eye contact: le regard
  • to hesitate: hésiter
  • confident: sûr de soi
  • a strength / a weakness: une qualité / un point faible

Grammaire, pas à pas

Étape 1 · Poser une question polie

Could you tell me about your experience? Would you like to add something?

Étape 2 · La question au present perfect

Have you ever worked under pressure? What have you learned from your failures?

Étape 3 · Répondre avec nuance

Although I…, I… / It's true that…, however… / Instead of…, I…

Figures de style

La répétition
Répéter une idée pour convaincre : I can do it. I want to do it. I will do it.
L'opposition
Retourner l'étiquette : You see a vampire. I see an actor who has played everything.

Useful expressions

  • Ouvrir l'entretien: Good morning, thank you for meeting me. / Please, sit down.
  • Gagner du temps: That's a good question. / Let me think…
  • Conclure: Thank you for your time. / I hope to hear from you soon.
