Version adaptée dys · Lesson 7

Lesson 7. Customers and tutors

Aménagements de la Lesson 7

  • Questions de CO dans l'ordre du script, avec une banque : Mrs Grant, 4702, Friday, ready, this morning, switch the machine on.
  • Formules polies présentées en deux colonnes courtes (client / tuteur), à copier telles quelles, sans l'exercice de transformation si celui-ci est trop lourd : le proposer en défi facultatif.
  • Oral au tuteur : cadre à trous de cinq phrases, lu puis dit.
  • Compte rendu de PFMP : cadre A2 à trous, déjà structuré en cinq phrases à compléter. Volume visé : 60 mots. L'enseignant peut accepter une dictée à l'adulte ou un enregistrement oral transcrit, si l'écrit est un obstacle majeur, tout en gardant une trace écrite minimale.
  • Pour la préparation de la tâche finale : une fiche unique recto, six cases, mots-clés déjà proposés à choisir et à entourer.

Pour l'enseignant. Parler à un client, parler à son tuteur, rédiger le compte rendu de PFMP, préparer la tâche finale.

Durée. 55 minutes.

Activités langagières. compréhension de l'oral, expression orale en continu, expression écrite.

Objectifs. À la fin de la leçon, l'élève peut : comprendre un échange avec un client ; employer des demandes polies ; expliquer une tâche ou un produit à un client ; rendre compte oralement à un tuteur ; rédiger un court compte rendu de PFMP en anglais, qui sera la partie écrite de la tâche finale.

Langue de la leçon. Formules polies : Could you... ?, Would you mind... ?, I'd like to..., Of course, Please wait a moment, I'll check. Réemploi du past simple pour le bilan. Réemploi de tout le lexique des leçons 1 à 6.

Phonologie. Intonation polie, légèrement montante, sur Could you give me the order number, please? I'd like to (/aɪd laɪk tə/).

Amorce (4 min)

Pour l'enseignant

L'œuvre au tableau. Faire imaginer l'arrivée d'un visiteur, comme un plan rapproché qui prépare la tâche finale.

Pour les élèves

Look at the painting. A customer comes into this school workshop. A young person walks towards the visitor, in front of the workbenches and the cardboard boxes. What is the first thing the young person says? What is the first question the customer asks? Say two lines with your partner.

Activité 1. Listen: talking to a customer (12 min). Compréhension de l'oral.

Script enseignant

Student: Good afternoon. Welcome to the workshop. How can I help you?

Customer: Good afternoon. I'd like to check my order, please. My name is Mrs Grant.

Student: Of course. Could you give me the order number, please?

Customer: Yes, it's 4702. I need the parts for Friday. Would you mind checking if they are ready?

Student: Not at all. Please wait a moment. I'll check with my tutor. (a short pause)

Student: Thank you for waiting. Your order is ready. We packed it this morning. I can bring the box to the counter now.

Customer: That's great, thank you. Can you also explain how to switch the machine on? I don't want to make a mistake.

Student: Yes, of course. First, you have to check the cable. Then, you can switch it on, here. You mustn't open this part when the machine is on. If you have a problem, you should call us.

Customer: Thank you very much. Have a good day.

Student: You're welcome. Have a good day too.

Pour les élèves, questions

Listen twice and answer.

  1. What does Mrs Grant want?
  2. What is the order number?
  3. When does she need the parts?
  4. Is the order ready?
  5. When did they pack the box?
  6. What does the student explain at the end?
  7. Find two polite phrases used by the student, and one used by the customer.
  8. (B1) Complete the safety warning the student gives about the machine.

Corrigé

  1. She wants to check her order. / She'd like to check her order.
  2. 4702.
  3. On Friday. / She needs the parts for Friday.
  4. Yes, it is ready.
  5. This morning.
  6. How to switch the machine on. / How to use the machine safely.
  7. Student (deux parmi) : How can I help you? Could you give me the order number, please? Please wait a moment. I'll check with my tutor. Thank you for waiting. Customer : I'd like to check my order, please. Would you mind checking if they are ready?
  8. First you have to check the cable. Then you can switch it on. You mustn't open this part when the machine is on. If you have a problem, you should call us.

Activité 2. Polite forms, for a customer and for a tutor (10 min).

Pour les élèves

Building your vocabulary: polite requests and explanations

To a customer

  • Good afternoon. Welcome. How can I help you?
  • I'd like to... / Could you give me..., please?
  • Would you mind waiting a moment? / Please wait a moment. I'll check.
  • Thank you for waiting. / I'm sorry about the delay.
  • Let me explain. First... Then... You have to... You mustn't...
  • You're welcome. Have a good day.

To a tutor

  • Excuse me, could I ask you a question?
  • Could you show me how to... ?
  • This morning I checked... / I finished... / I had a problem with...
  • What should I do next?
  • Thank you for your help.

Exercise. Make these requests more polite.

  1. Give me the order number.
  2. Wait.
  3. Check this for me.
  4. Show me the machine.
  5. I want to see my order.

Corrigé

  1. Could you give me the order number, please?
  2. Please wait a moment. / Would you mind waiting a moment?
  3. Could you check this for me, please? / Would you mind checking this for me?
  4. Could you show me the machine, please?
  5. I'd like to see my order, please.

Activité 3. Talk to your tutor about your PFMP (12 min). Expression orale en continu.

Pour les élèves

Your tutor asks you about your work. Speak in continuous English for one minute (A2) or about two minutes (B1). Use this plan:

  1. Greet your tutor.
  2. Say where you work and who you work with.
  3. Say what you do every day (present simple) and what you did this morning or yesterday (past simple).
  4. Mention one problem and the solution.
  5. Say what you learned, or what you would like to learn next.

Useful sentences

  • I work in... My tutor is...
  • Every day, I check... / I prepare... / I help...
  • Yesterday, I... This morning, I...
  • There was a problem: ... We solved it. We...
  • I learned how to... I'd like to learn how to...

Practise with your partner: one person is the student, the other is the tutor and asks two questions. Then change roles.

Corrigé

Exemple A2 :

Good morning, Mr Baker. I work in the workshop, with Karim and the team. Every day, I prepare cardboard boxes and I check orders. This morning, I packed order 4702. There was a problem: one box arrived damaged. We replaced it. I learned how to check an order carefully. I'd like to learn how to talk to customers.

Exemple B1 :

Good morning, Ms Clark. I work on the site, with my tutor and two colleagues. Every day I check deliveries, I prepare materials, and I help with measurements. Yesterday I measured a wall and I wrote the numbers on the plan. There was a problem in the morning: the delivery arrived late, and one box was damaged. I told my tutor, we put the box aside, and we called the supplier. I learned how to report a problem clearly. Next, I'd like to learn how to explain a task to a new colleague, in English too.

Activité 4. Write your PFMP report (12 min). Expression écrite. Partie écrite de la tâche finale.

Pour l'enseignant

Cette production est évaluée (grille, activité EE). Les élèves terminent si besoin à la maison, au propre, pour la leçon 8. Rappeler le volume, le plan, et l'interdiction d'écrire un document administratif français : c'est un texte en anglais, à la première personne, professionnel et lisible.

Pour les élèves

Final written task: a short PFMP report in English

Write about your PFMP (a real PFMP, or a realistic one in your trade).

A2: 60 to 80 words. B1: 120 to 150 words.

Use this plan:

  1. Where you work, and who your tutor is.
  2. Your main tasks (present simple).
  3. What you did on one particular day (past simple).
  4. A problem and the solution.
  5. What you learned, and what you want to improve.

Write a first version now. Check: verbs, polite tone, clear facts. You will hand in a clean version at Lesson 8 (or at the written evaluation if your teacher organises a separate time).

Corrigé

Exemple A2 (environ 75 mots) :

I work in a workshop at Martin & Co. My tutor is Mr Baker. Every day, I prepare orders, I pack cardboard boxes, and I clean my workbench. Yesterday, I checked order 4702 and I packed it in the morning. There was a problem: one box arrived damaged. I told my tutor, and we replaced the box. I learned how to check an order and how to report a problem. I want to improve my English with customers.

Exemple B1 (environ 140 mots) :

I am doing my PFMP at Martin & Co, in the workshop, and my tutor is Ms Clark. Every day I prepare orders, I check parts, I pack cardboard boxes, and I help colleagues at the workbench. I also welcome visitors when the tutor asks me to. Last Tuesday, I prepared four orders in the morning and I helped Karim with a delivery in the afternoon. There was a problem with order 4821: the box arrived damaged, and two parts were broken. I checked the number, I told my tutor, and we replaced the parts. We delivered the new box in the afternoon, and I apologised to the customer. I learned how to report a problem clearly and how to speak politely to a customer. I want to improve two things: my technical vocabulary, and my confidence when I explain a safety rule in English.

Préparer la tâche finale, critères de réussite (5 min).

Pour l'enseignant

Projeter les critères (les mêmes que la grille orale). Distribuer le plan de la tâche finale (section 6). Rassurer : toute la langue a déjà été travaillée.

Pour les élèves

Next lesson is the final task: Welcome to the workshop.

You will welcome a visitor, in pairs. You will:

  • greet and introduce yourself,
  • say what is happening in the workshop,
  • explain a task,
  • give a safety rule,
  • report a problem and a solution,
  • answer the visitor's questions.

You may bring key-word notes. A2 students may keep a word bank. Learn your useful phrases tonight. Bring your PFMP report, clean and complete.

Trace pour le cahier.

Les formules polies de l'activité 2, plus le plan du compte rendu.

Pour les élèves

Homework, Lesson 7

  1. Finish a clean version of your PFMP report.
  2. Make these sentences polite. a. I want to see the manager. b. Give me a new box. c. Wait here.
  3. Prepare key-word notes (not a full text) for the final oral task, following the six-point plan.

Corrigé

  1. (Production personnelle, selon les modèles A2 et B1 ci-dessus.)
  2. a. I'd like to see the manager, please. b. Could you give me a new box, please? c. Would you mind waiting here? / Please wait here a moment.
  3. (Notes personnelles. Vérifier que les six points sont présents, sous forme de mots-clés seulement.)
