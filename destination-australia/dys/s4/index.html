Lesson 4. A café job: pay, tax and RSA

What questions must you ask before you accept a café job?

Road map

Lesson of 55 minutes, timed for 52 minutes. 8 lessons.

  1. Recap (marked /3) 5 min
  2. A café job in Melbourne: tasks and honesty 12 min
  3. Serving alcohol: RSA 8 min
  4. Pay, casual loading and tax withholding 15 min
  5. Five questions before you accept an offer, record and homework 12 min
  • Written record. The idea of an award and a casual rate, the 15% WHM withholding on the first AUD 45,000 (when it applies), and why an RSA depends on the state and the role.
  • Participation. Everyone speaks at least once: one café task you could talk about in an interview, or one question about pay or shifts.
  • Homework for next lesson. Write five questions to ask an employer about pay, shifts, safety, accommodation and, if relevant, specified work. Learn the new flashcards: award, casual loading, RSA.

Stopping point. End of this lesson.

Emergency exit · 8 min. If time runs short at this stopping point, condense the end of the lesson: dictate a short written record (two sentences); a quick round so that everyone speaks at least once (one sentence each); write the homework on the board.

Recap · marked /3
  1. List the Big Four.
  2. Where do you apply for a TFN, and should you pay an intermediary?
  3. What should you do if you are unsure about an item at the airport?

Answer key

  1. SIM or eSIM, bank account, TFN, private insurance.
  2. On the official ATO page, at no cost; no, do not pay an intermediary.
  3. Ask an officer and declare items when required.

A café job in Melbourne

You may take orders, prepare coffee, use the till, clear tables and work with a team. Explain the experience you actually have. Ask about training, shifts, pay, breaks and the applicable award.

Serving alcohol: RSA

Check the Responsible Service of Alcohol requirements in the state where you will work. A Victorian RSA may be needed for a role involving alcohol service. Do not assume that one certificate is accepted in every state or that every café role requires it.

Pay and tax

From the first full pay period starting on or after 1 July 2026, the National Minimum Wage is AUD 26.44 per hour, or AUD 33.05 for a casual employee including the 25% casual loading, where the adult National Minimum Wage applies. Awards, agreements, age and classifications can change the applicable rate. Weekend penalties are not a single universal percentage: use Fair Work’s pay tools.

For WHM withholding, a registered employer generally withholds 15% on the first AUD 45,000. Higher rates apply above that amount. Tax residency and treaty rules can affect the final assessment, including for French citizens. Do not treat a withholding rate as a guarantee of your final tax bill.

Check: Fair Work, minimum wage from 1 July 2026 · ATO, Working Holiday Maker tax table.

Your task. Before accepting an offer, prepare five questions about pay, shifts, safety, accommodation and specified-work eligibility.

Answer key · examples

“Which award and classification apply?” “What is my casual hourly rate?” “What are the weekend rates?” “Are accommodation deductions explained in writing?” “Does this postcode and role count as specified work?”

Exit ticket
  1. What is casual loading, in one sentence?
  2. Is a Victorian RSA valid in every Australian state?

Answer key

  1. Extra pay (here 25% on the adult National Minimum Wage, when that rate applies) for a casual employee, instead of paid leave.
  2. No. Check the rules in the state where you will work.

Homework
