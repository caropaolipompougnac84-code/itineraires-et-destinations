Lesson 2. Listen to Bond

Goal: I can identify the situation and report one detail.

Road map

Lesson of 55 minutes, timed for 52 minutes. Lesson 2 of 6. Minutes 52 to 55: no new task. Working at home and need longer? Split the lesson into two blocks: this is a classroom structure, not a speed test.

  1. Review one card 0 to 5
  2. Learn four words and predict 5 to 13
  3. First listening 13 to 18
  4. Second listening and Exercise 1 18 to 26
  5. Short replay and checking 26 to 32
  6. Grammar Exercise 2 32 to 38
  7. Prepare oral account 38 to 44
  8. Each student speaks 44 to 47
  9. Homework 47 to 48
  10. Written record 48 to 52
  • Written record. Complete the written record: Bond returns; MI6 faces a threat; a trailer gives clues, not the whole story.
  • Participation. Everyone speaks at least once: say two, four or more sentences, according to your route.
  • Homework for next lesson. make a return card; Route C adds threat; Route P writes a short account with because.

Stopping point. Stopping point: minute 44. Stop the main exercises. Keep the written record and the homework, even if an exercise is unfinished.

Emergency exit · 8 min. If time runs short at this stopping point, this eight-minute ending replaces unfinished extension work inside the 52 minutes; it is not added to them.
  • 44 to 47: three minutes of speaking;
  • 47 to 48: one minute for homework;
  • 48 to 52: four minutes for the written record.

Use the same Sony Pictures official Skyfall trailer as the standard lesson: official trailer on YouTube. No film still and no full transcript are reproduced here.

Word help

  • return: come back;
  • threat: danger;
  • attack: violent action;
  • presumed dead: believed to have died.
Catch-up text

Independent catch-up text:

Bond was believed to be dead.

MI6 is attacked.

Bond returns.

M is under pressure.

The trailer does not explain the whole story.

This text provides access to the lesson if the video is unavailable. It is reading support, not evidence that listening comprehension has been assessed.

Exercise 1

Listen for the situation

Worked example. A person comes back after leaving. The person returns. (This example is practised before the video.)
Now you.

Now listen, or use the catch-up text if the video is unavailable.

  1. Is the situation routine or serious?
  2. Does Bond return?
  3. Do we learn the whole story?

Route C: add one detail you heard. Route P: explain whether your detail was heard, seen or inferred. Check the heard-detail answer with the teacher’s verified extract notes. Do not mark a visually noticed detail as a listening error, but label it accurately.

Self-check

  1. Serious.
  2. Yes.
  3. No.
Exercise 2

Grammar

Worked example. Now: Bond speaks. Happening in the scene: Bond is speaking.
Now you.
  1. Bond speaking.
  2. The characters discussing a problem.
  3. The trailer creating tension.

Route P extension. Corrected example: Bond returns. Past: Bond returned. Now: M reacts.

Self-check

  1. is
  2. are
  3. is

Extension: M reacted.

Exercise 3

Build an oral account

Worked example. This is a trailer. It presents a crisis.
Now you.

Complete:

This is a .

Bond .

The situation is .

We do not know .

Self-check

This is a trailer. Bond returns. The situation is serious. We do not know the whole story.

Speaking

  • S: two short sentences.
  • C: four sentences.
  • P: add one heard detail and one uncertainty.

If alone, speak after reading the catch-up text. Label the work “reading-based oral account”, not “completed listening assessment”.

Homework

Make a return card.

Route C: add threat.

Route P: write a short account with because.

Written record

Bond .

MI6 faces a .

A trailer gives clues, not the whole .

Answer key

returns; threat; story.
