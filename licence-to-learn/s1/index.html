Lesson 1. Who will be the next 007?

News, two services, three actors, and an argued vote. Nothing is official yet.

Road map

Lesson of 55 minutes, timed for 52 minutes. Six lessons of 55 minutes, each timed for 52 minutes. Lesson 6 is the final-task interview.

  1. News and a few useful words 8 min
  2. Warm-up quiz, spoken 8 min
  3. MI5 or MI6: look, then true or false 10 min
  4. The three biographies and the simple past 10 min
  5. Exercises 1 to 3 8 min
  6. An argued vote and a class tally 8 min
  • Written record. Copy one sentence about MI5, one sentence about MI6, and one sentence that says, in the simple past, a fact about the actor you voted for.
  • Participation. Everyone speaks at least once: each student answers one warm-up question, or later gives one argument in the vote.
  • Homework for next lesson. Learn how to say the three dates in full. Write four flashcards (screen test, rumour, a spy, headquarters).

Stopping point. After the biographies and the simple past box. If time is short, do only exercise 1, then a one-sentence vote.

Emergency exit · 8 min. Dictate the two sentences about MI5 and MI6. Each student names one actor and says “I vote for … because…”. Write the homework on the board.

Warm-up quiz

  1. When did the last Bond film, No Time to Die, come out?
  2. Who played Bond in it?
  3. Which Daniel Craig film is often called one of the best Bond films?

Your teacher’s favourite: Skyfall (2012). You will study it in lesson 2 and lesson 5.

Answer key

  1. In 2021.
  2. Daniel Craig (his fifth and last film in the role).
  3. Skyfall (2012), often named by teachers and critics; other answers can be discussed.

MI5 and MI6: who does what?

James Bond is a fictional MI6 agent.

True or false?

  1. MI5 works abroad.
  2. MI6 is the Secret Intelligence Service.
  3. James Bond is a real agent.
  4. MI5 protects the UK at home.

Answer key

  1. False. MI5 works at home, inside the UK.
  2. True.
  3. False. He is a fictional character.
  4. True.

The finalists, according to reports

Grammar: the simple past

Regular verbs: play, played · work, worked · star, starred.

Irregular verbs: be, was · grow, grew · become, became · win, won.

Time markers: in 2017 · two years ago · when he was young · then · last year · since 2019 (present perfect) · for ten years.

Dates: we write He was born on 2 June 1990. We say He was born on the second of June, nineteen ninety.

Exercise 1. Put the verbs in the simple past

  1. Jack Lowden (grow) ______ up in Scotland.
  2. Callum Turner (work) ______ as a model.
  3. Josh O’Connor (win) ______ an Emmy Award.
  4. Jack Lowden (play) ______ a soldier in Dunkirk.
  5. Josh O’Connor (become) ______ famous in The Crown.

Answer key

  1. grew
  2. worked
  3. won
  4. played
  5. became

Exercise 2. Say the dates

Write the dates in words, then say them aloud.

  1. 2 June 1990
  2. 15 February 1990
  3. 20 May 1990

Answer key

  1. the second of June, nineteen ninety
  2. the fifteenth of February, nineteen ninety
  3. the twentieth of May, nineteen ninety

Exercise 3. Timeline

Put these events in order on a timeline, then tell the story with first, then, after that, in…:

  1. Josh O’Connor played Prince Charles.
  2. Jack Lowden was born.
  3. Dunkirk came out.
  4. Fantastic Beasts: The Crimes of Grindelwald came out.

Answer key

B (1990), C (2017), D (2018), A (2019).

Your argued vote

Step 1. Vote for one actor. Step 2. Give at least three arguments.

To…Sentence starters
give an opinionI think… · In my opinion… · I’m convinced that…
give reasonsbecause… · since… · thanks to his role in…
compareHe is more … than… · He looks as … as…
add or contrastMoreover… · However… · Although…
concludeThat’s why… · To sum up…

Model answer. In my opinion, Jack Lowden should be the next Bond because he already played an MI5 agent in Slow Horses. Moreover, he is more serious than the other actors. However, Callum Turner looks as elegant as Daniel Craig. To sum up, I’m convinced that Jack Lowden is the best choice.
