3. His early life

Fact file · Robert Pattinson, the early years

  • Born on 13 May 1986, in Barnes, London.
  • Two older sisters.
  • Started the piano and the guitar at four.
  • Worked as a model from the age of twelve, for about four years.
  • Joined the Barnes Theatre Company at thirteen, because he was shy.
  • Sang his own songs in London pubs as a teenager, sometimes as “Bobby Dupea”.

4. Lesson summary

Lesson summary · model

Robert Pattinson was born in London in 1986. He started modelling at twelve and played the piano and the guitar before he became an actor. His drama teacher didn't believe in him, but he never stopped.

Étude de la langue

Vocabulary

  • to be born: naître
  • a model: un mannequin
  • to start / to begin: commencer
  • shy: timide
  • a stage name: un nom de scène
  • a teen idol: une idole des adolescents
  • a label: une étiquette
  • a trap: un piège
  • to audition: passer une audition
  • fame: la célébrité

Grammaire, pas à pas

Étape 1 · Le prétérit des verbes réguliers

On ajoute -ed à la base verbale, à toutes les personnes. He started modelling at twelve. He played the guitar. Attention à l'orthographe : stop donne stopped, study donne studied, love donne loved.

Étape 2 · La négation et la question

On utilise l'auxiliaire did et la base verbale : He didn't study drama. Did he play the piano? Yes, he did.

Étape 3 · Les repères de temps

Le prétérit raconte un moment terminé et daté : in 1986, at twelve, in 2005, three years later, when he was four.

Figures de style

L'allitération
La répétition d'un même son au début des mots attire l'oreille : the Twilight Trap, Blurred face, big break.
La métaphore
On parle d'une chose avec l'image d'une autre : a label (une étiquette collée sur quelqu'un), a trap (un piège dont on doit sortir).

Useful expressions

  • Deviner: I think it's… / It might be… / It could be…
  • Être sûr: I'm sure it's… / It can't be…
  • Hésiter: I'm not sure, but… / Maybe…
