Title: Man at Work, Bratislavia.jpg
Subject: Čumil (also called Man at Work), bronze statue of a sewer worker leaning out of a manhole, Bratislava.
Sculptor and work: Čumil, Viktor Hulík, 1997, Bratislava.
Photograph author: John, Tucson, Arizona (Flickr user “John” / 55849279@N00).

Freedom of panorama: the statue stands permanently in a public street in Slovakia; sections 37 and 41 of Slovak copyright law allow this photograph (Commons:CRT/Slovakia#Freedom of panorama).
Verification: the photograph clearly shows the bronze figure leaning out of an open manhole, with a road sign “MAN AT WORK” and a warning triangle depicting the same figure, on a paved street in Bratislava. The worker wears a soft cap, not a safety helmet, a jacket without a high-visibility vest, and no gloves.
