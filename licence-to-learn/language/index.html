Language

Grammar and vocabulary recycled across the six lessons. The headings below follow the usual site wording: Building your vocabulary, then the structures practised in class.

In real intelligence terminology, an officer works for a service, while an agent can be a person recruited to provide information. The everyday word “spy” is less precise.

Lesson 1. Past simple, present perfect, possibility and comparison

Use the past simple with a finished past date. Use the present perfect to connect earlier reporting with an unfinished current discussion. Use might or could for possibility, not certainty.

Examples

Lowden played a fighter pilot. Several actors have been linked to Bond during the continuing casting discussion. Another actor might get the role. A confirmed announcement is more reliable than a rumour.

Lesson 2. Present continuous, past simple and linkers

Present forms can describe the film as a work or an image on screen. Past forms can retell events. Choose one viewpoint and remain consistent. Link ideas with because, although or however.

Examples

Bond is speaking to M. Bond returned. Although M is under pressure, she uses humour. The trailer gives clues. However, it does not explain everything.

Lesson 3. Present simple, questions, whereas and who

MI5 protects UK national security, whereas MI6 focuses on foreign intelligence. An agent is a person who provides information or assistance.

Examples

What does MI5 protect? Where do officers work? Does an agent provide information?

Lesson 4. Comparatives, too/enough and qualified claims

Evidence is more useful than a stereotype. “Some officers” is less absolute than “all officers”. This statement is too general to be reliable. We do not have enough evidence to confirm it. Screen heroes are not necessarily good employees.

Lesson 6. Questions, past evidence and clarification

Which service would you choose? I would like to join MI6. I am patient. For example, I checked the sources yesterday. Could you repeat the question, please? Could I have a moment to think? What training would I receive?
