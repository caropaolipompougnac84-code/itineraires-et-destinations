Key question: How does Ruby feel?
What you need to say

In this lesson, you need to talk about feelings.

Ruby is brave. I think she is scared. She was alone.

Feelings

WordMeaningExample
brave you do something difficultRuby is brave.
scared you are afraidPeople shout. Maybe Ruby is scared.
alone nobody is with youRuby was alone in her class.
happy you smile, you feel goodRuby is happy with her teacher.
sad you are not happyI am sad when my friend is ill.
Mrs Henry, the teacher

On the first day, Ruby sat in the office all day.

Many white parents took their children out of the school.

Many teachers said no to Ruby. One teacher said yes: Mrs Barbara Henry.

Mrs Henry came from Boston.

Ruby was alone in her class, all year. Mrs Henry taught Ruby alone.

Ruby could not go to the playground.

Ruby said: « Mrs Henry became my best friend. »

brave : courageux, courageuse · scared : qui a peur · alone : seul, seule · happy : heureux, heureuse · the playground : la cour de récréation

Point prof · talking about feelings
How does she feel?
She is brave. She feels happy.
In the past
She was alone. She was scared.
My opinion
I think Ruby is brave.
Why? because
Ruby is brave because she goes to school.

Quick check. Complete: I … Ruby is brave.

Answer keyI think Ruby is brave.
Note pour l'enseignante

Faits vérifiés : le premier jour, Ruby est restée toute la journée dans le bureau du directeur (récit de Ruby Bridges, Guideposts) ; les parents blancs ont retiré leurs enfants ; Barbara Henry, venue de Boston, lui a fait classe seule toute l'année ; Ruby n'avait pas le droit d'aller à la cantine ni dans la cour ; « Mrs Henry became my best friend » (interview Scholastic, 21 octobre 2010). Pour aller plus loin en classe, l'interview filmée de Ruby Bridges (chaîne Scholastic) est listée sur la page des sources, à montrer avec les sous-titres anglais si vous le souhaitez.

Exercises

1. Choose the feelingEntryrecognition

  1. Ruby walks to the school. A lot of people shout. She is (brave / happy).
  2. Ruby is the only child in her class. She is (alone / angry).
  3. Mrs Henry is her best friend. Ruby is (happy / scared).
Answer key
  1. brave
  2. alone
  3. happy

2. Mrs Henry: gap-fillEntryguided

3. I think…Corepractice

Write two sentences.

Sentence starters

I think Ruby is… because…

I think Mrs Henry is… because…

Answer key

Example: I think Ruby is brave because she goes to school. I think Mrs Henry is nice because she teaches Ruby.

4. Say it!Corespeaking

Point to Ruby in the painting. Say: She is… She was… I think…

Answer key

Example: She is brave. She was alone in her class. I think she is very strong.

Stretch · A2

Mrs Henry was brave too. Why?

Example answer

Many teachers said no. She said yes. She taught Ruby every day.

Homework
  1. Learn five feelings: brave, scared, alone, happy, sad.
  2. Write one sentence: I think Ruby is… because…
