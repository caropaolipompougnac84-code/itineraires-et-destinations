Lesson 7. True or Tale? The inheritance rumour

Debatable question: Why do we want to believe a rumour, even when there is no evidence?
By the end of this lesson, I can: say if a piece of information is a verified fact, an unverified claim or a fake, explain why, and quote my sources.

For the teacher: timing (52 minutes)
Step Time Activity
1 0-4 Recap and flashcard duel
2 4-8 The question of the day: “the claim”
3 8-18 Documents 1 to 3 (jigsaw reading in groups of three)
4 18-24 Expanding your vocabulary: fake news
5 24-30 The teacher’s point: modals of deduction, talking about sources
6 30-38 Exercises
7 38-47 Speaking task: “The newsroom” (mediation)
8 47-50 Written record
9 50-52 Homework

Stop point (minute 47). Every group has given its verdict aloud.

Emergency exit (8 minutes). Read only Document 2. Each student classifies one sentence of Exercise 2 aloud with must, might or can’t, then the class writes the verdict together.

Teacher’s note. The fake video is never shown in class: we describe it. Showing it would spread it.

Step 1. Recap

  1. Report: “I accept.” (Dolly) 2. Two songs Dolly and Miley sang together. 3. What does an heir receive?

    Answer key

    Answer key: 1 Dolly said that she accepted. · 2 Jolene, Rainbowland, Wrecking Ball · 3 money, property or rights from a person who dies.

Step 2. The question of the day

Write on the board: “Dolly Parton left her whole fortune to Miley Cyrus.”

Ask: Have you seen this? Do you believe it? Why would people like this story? Students vote: true / false / we don’t know.

Transition. Let’s check like journalists: who said it, what is the original source, what do reliable media say?

Step 3. Documents (jigsaw: each student of a group reads one document, then explains it to the other two)

Document 1. The viral video

On 2 September 2026, a YouTube account called @Celebritynews.m posted a video. In it, “Miley Cyrus” seems to explain that her godmother left her entire fortune to her. (We describe the video; we do not show it.)

Document 2. The fact-checkers

According to Lead Stories (9 September 2026), an AI detection tool, Hive Moderation, gave the video a score of 99%: it is very likely AI-generated. The voice is a monotone copy of Miley’s voice, without natural changes in emotion or rhythm, and her lips do not always match the words. Read the fact-check: Lead Stories.

Another claim said that Dolly left the rights to Jolene to Miley. The site meaww found no credible evidence for it: meaww fact-check.

Document 3. What we know

  • No will has been made public (Ridley Law, October 2026).
  • According to court documents reported by The Tennessean (24 September 2026), Dolly created a trust in 1997, the D.P. Dean Trust, to manage her property after her death. A trust is private, so the public does not know who receives what.
  • In 2020, Dolly told Billboard: “I would not want to leave that mess to somebody else.”
  • Miley’s real tribute on X (26 August 2026) said nothing about money: “I will always love her and want to make her proud.” (quoted by Lead Stories)
  • Dolly’s sister, Stella Parton, asked fans to stop sharing fake content: “There is a tremendous amount of AI and endless garbage being posted everyday” (NBC News, September 2026).

Jigsaw task. Each student explains his or her document in French or English (mediation) in one minute, then the group fills in the table.

Who posted it? Original source? What reliable media say What is really known

Answer key

Answer key: an unknown account, @Celebritynews.m · none: no document, no official statement · Lead Stories: very likely AI-generated; meaww: no evidence for Jolene · a private trust from 1997; no will made public; Miley’s tribute says nothing about money.

Step 4. Expanding your vocabulary: fake news

Word Meaning Example
a rumour information that spreads but is not checked The inheritance story is a rumour.
a fake / a hoax something false made to trick people The video is a fake.
a deepfake a video where AI copies a real person’s face or voice Deepfakes can copy famous voices.
AI-generated made by artificial intelligence The voice is AI-generated.
a fact-check an article that checks a claim Lead Stories published a fact-check.
a claim something people say is true The claim has no source.
evidence (uncountable) facts that prove something There is no evidence of the inheritance.
a will a legal document saying who gets your property No will was made public.
a trust a legal structure that manages property, often privately Her property is managed by a trust.
an inheritance what you receive when someone dies The inheritance is private.
reliable / unreliable that you can / cannot trust Is this source reliable?
clickbait content made to attract clicks Emotional hashtags are typical of clickbait.

Words to talk about sources: According to... · ... reports that... · It is not confirmed. · There is no evidence that... · It has been proved false.

Exercises on vocabulary

Exercise G1. Fill in the gaps: evidence · fact-check · reliable · deepfake · will
The video is a (1) __. A (2) _ by Lead Stories shows it. There is no (3) that Miley inherited everything, and no (4) was made public. Always use (5) ___ sources.

Answer key

Answer key: 1 deepfake · 2 fact-check · 3 evidence · 4 will · 5 reliable.

Exercise G2. Correct the mistake. 1 There are no evidences. 2 According to me, it’s fake.

Answer key

Answer key: 1 There is no evidence (uncountable). 2 In my opinion, it’s fake. (According to is for a source, not for yourself.)

Flashcards

Front Back
evidence (blue) preuves (indénombrable) · There is no evidence that she inherited everything.
It must / might / can’t be... (red) quasi sûr / possible / impossible · It can’t be her real voice.
According to + source (green) selon · According to Lead Stories, the video is fake.

Step 5. The teacher’s point

Teacher script. Draw three columns: Verified fact / Unverified claim / Fake. Read each sentence of Exercise 2 and ask: How sure are we? Students answer with must, might or can’t before placing the sentence.

Point Meaning Example
must + base verb almost sure it is true The video must be fake: the lips don’t match.
might / may / could possible The post might be a joke.
can’t almost sure it is not true It can’t be Miley’s real voice: it is too monotone.
must have + past participle (going further) deduction about the past Someone must have used AI.
Sources According to... · There is no evidence that... · It is not confirmed.
Fact, claim, fake verified fact = proved by reliable sources; unverified claim = we don’t know; fake = proved false

Quick check. The trust is private, so the claim (must / might / can’t) be true, but nobody can prove it. → might.

Step 6. Exercises

Exercise 1. Signs of an AI video. Tick the warning signs mentioned by Lead Stories.
a monotone voice · lips that don’t match the words · an official account · natural emotion in the voice · no reliable media report the story · a link to a court document

Answer key

Answer key: monotone voice, lips that don’t match, no reliable media. (No official account, no natural emotion, no court document.)

Exercise 2. Fact, claim or fake?
1. Dolly was Miley’s honorary godmother. 2. Dolly left Jolene to Miley. 3. The video shows the real Miley talking about her inheritance. 4. Dolly died on 25 August 2026. 5. Dolly’s will was published. 6. Dolly and Miley sang Jolene together.

Answer key

Answer key: 1 verified fact · 2 unverified claim (no evidence) · 3 fake (very likely AI-generated, according to Lead Stories) · 4 verified fact · 5 false: no will has been made public · 6 verified fact.

Exercise 3. How sure are you?
1. The voice is robotic. It ... be Miley’s real voice. 2. No journalist reported it. The story ... be fake. 3. The trust is private. Miley ... be one of the people who inherit, but we can’t know.

Answer key

Answer key: 1 can’t · 2 must · 3 might.

Exercise 4. The method. Put the questions in a logical order: a. What does the official account say? b. Who posted it? c. Where else is it reported? d. What is the original source?

Answer key

Answer key: b, d, c, a (other logical orders are accepted if justified).

Exercise 5. Transform with There is no evidence that... or According to...
1. Nobody can prove that Miley inherited the fortune. 2. Lead Stories says the video is AI-generated.

Answer key

Answer key: 1 There is no evidence that Miley inherited the fortune. 2 According to Lead Stories, the video is AI-generated.

Exercise 6. Write a fact-check (80 words): the claim, your verdict, three pieces of evidence, your sources.
Model answer: Claim: Dolly Parton left her whole fortune to Miley Cyrus. Verdict: unverified, and the viral video is fake. Evidence: 1. According to Lead Stories, the video is very likely AI-generated (99%). 2. No will has been made public: Dolly’s property is managed by a private trust created in 1997. 3. Miley’s real tribute on X says nothing about money. Sources: Lead Stories, The Tennessean, meaww.

Help box. Frame: Claim: ... Verdict: ... According to..., the video... There is no evidence that... Miley’s real message...
Going further. Use impersonal structures: It is said that... · It is claimed that... · Miley is said to have inherited..., but there is no evidence.

Step 7. Speaking task: “The newsroom”

Groups of three journalists. Each group prepares a 1-minute TV news flash: “True or Tale?”

  1. Presenter 1: the claim and where it appeared.
  2. Presenter 2: the evidence (two facts, with According to...).
  3. Presenter 3: the verdict with must, might, can’t, and an answer to the question of the day: Why do people want to believe it?

Possible answer for presenter 3: The video can’t be real: the voice is robotic. People might want to believe it because it is a beautiful story: a famous godmother and a famous goddaughter. But a good story is not evidence.

Debate (if time allows). Is it acceptable to use someone’s death to get views online? Each student says one sentence with I believe... because....

Step 8. Written record

Lesson 7. True or Tale?
Claim: “Dolly left everything to Miley.” Verdict: unverified. The viral video is very likely AI-generated (Lead Stories). No will has been made public; Dolly’s property is managed by a private trust (1997).
Method: Who posted it? What is the original source? Where else is it reported? What does the official account say?
Grammar. must = almost sure; might = possible; can’t = almost sure it is not true. According to... / There is no evidence that...

Step 9. Homework

  1. Check one viral post of the week with the four questions of the method (five sentences).
  2. Make five flashcards about fake news.
  3. Help box: record a 30-second verdict on your post with must, might or can’t.

Passing the torch. Being a goddaughter gives no automatic right to an inheritance. What we can verify is another heritage: songs, advice, values. Which heritage matters more?
