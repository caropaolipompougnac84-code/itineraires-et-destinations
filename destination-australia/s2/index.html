A Short History of Australia

  • At least 65,000 years ago: Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples live on the continent. It is one of the oldest living cultures in the world.
  • 1770: James Cook lands at Botany Bay and claims the east coast for Britain.
  • 26 January 1788: the First Fleet arrives in Sydney Cove with convicts. Britain starts a penal colony.
  • 1850s: gold rushes. Thousands of migrants arrive from Europe and China.
  • 1 January 1901: Federation. Six colonies become the Commonwealth of Australia. The "White Australia" policy limits non-European immigration.
  • 1915: Gallipoli. The ANZAC soldiers become a national symbol (Anzac Day, 25 April).
  • 1910s to 1970s: the "Stolen Generations". Many Aboriginal children are taken from their families.
  • 1967: a referendum allows Aboriginal people to be counted in the census.
  • 1970s: end of the White Australia policy; multicultural Australia begins.
  • 1992: the Mabo decision. The High Court rejects the idea of "terra nullius" (land belonging to no one).
  • 13 February 2008: Prime Minister Kevin Rudd says "Sorry" to the Stolen Generations.
  • 2023: Australians vote "No" to a referendum on an Indigenous "Voice to Parliament".

Reading CE

On 26 January every year, many Australians celebrate Australia Day with barbecues, fireworks and flags. But for many Aboriginal people, this date marks the beginning of invasion, the loss of their land and the destruction of their families. Some call it "Invasion Day" or "Survival Day". Every year, there is a debate: should Australia change the date? Today, at public events, speakers often begin with an "Acknowledgement of Country": they recognise the traditional owners of the land where the event takes place. For a young visitor, understanding this debate is a way to respect the people who have lived there for more than 65,000 years.

  1. ★ What happens on 26 January? Give two examples.
  2. ★ Find two other names for this day.
  3. ★ What is an "Acknowledgement of Country"?
  4. ★★ Why is the date controversial? Explain in your own words.
  5. ★★ Do you think Australia should change the date? Give one argument for and one against.
