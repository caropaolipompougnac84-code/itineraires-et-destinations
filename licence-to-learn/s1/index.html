Lesson 1. Who will be the next James Bond?

Students can explain what is known and what remains uncertain; describe relevant experience; compare possible casting choices; and defend a preference without presenting it as a fact.

Road map

Lesson of 55 minutes, timed for 52 minutes. Six lessons of 55 minutes, each timed for 52 minutes. The remaining three minutes allow for arrival, equipment and departure. Lesson 6 is the final-task interview.

  1. Pair starter: what should a new Bond actor bring to the role? 0 to 5
  2. Read Document A; complete the news grid 5 to 12
  3. Read the profiles; complete the candidate grid 12 to 21
  4. Grammar workshop and correction 21 to 31
  5. Prepare an evidence-based preference 31 to 35
  6. Paired casting debate, then a few sampled contributions 35 to 44
  7. Every student gives a final qualified vote 44 to 47
  8. Set homework 47 to 48
  9. Complete the written record 48 to 52
  • Written record. Complete the five-sentence written record on rumour, reporting, preference and uncertainty.
  • Participation. Everyone speaks at least once: each student speaks for 30 to 40 seconds, then answers a partner question.
  • Homework for next lesson. three flashcards (successor, screen test, rumour) and 70 to 90 words recommending an actor.

Stopping point. Stopping point: minute 44.

Emergency exit · 8 min. If time runs short at this stopping point, this eight-minute ending replaces unfinished extension work inside the 52 minutes; it is not added to them.
  • 44 to 47: each student gives a choice and one reason to a partner;
  • 47 to 48: homework;
  • 48 to 52: complete the written record.

Student document A. The casting desk

Original classroom adaptation of current reporting:

Daniel Craig’s successor has attracted intense media attention. Callum Turner, Jack Lowden and Josh O’Connor have all appeared in reporting about the casting process. More recent coverage has also linked other actors to the role.

However, a reported screen test is not an official casting announcement. A headline may repeat an unnamed source, and different reports may contradict one another.

The official Bond website has announced Denis Villeneuve as director and Steven Knight as writer of the next film. The sources checked for this lesson do not establish an officially announced new Bond actor.

Our classroom question is therefore not “Who has definitely won?” It is “Who would we choose, and what evidence supports our choice?”

These facts were checked on 11 November 2026.

Teacher update rule. Date the news box on the day it is used. If an official actor announcement appears, replace the uncertainty paragraph and change the debate to: was this a convincing choice, and what kind of Bond could this actor create? Do not use betting odds as evidence of a casting decision.

Student document B. Three actors discussed in the reporting

These short profiles describe career experience, not a confirmed shortlist.

Jack Lowden

Jack Lowden trained at the Royal Conservatoire of Scotland. He developed his career on stage and won an Olivier Award for his performance in Ghosts. He played a fighter pilot in Dunkirk and River Cartwright in Slow Horses. His work gives students examples of theatre, historical drama and screen espionage.

Callum Turner

Callum Turner began his career as a model before moving into acting. He played Theseus Scamander in the Fantastic Beasts films and appeared in The Capture. He also played a rower in The Boys in the Boat and a pilot in Masters of the Air. His career includes thrillers, fantasy and historical drama.

Josh O’Connor

Josh O’Connor trained at Bristol Old Vic Theatre School. He played Prince Charles in The Crown and won an Emmy for the role. His films include God’s Own Country and Challengers. Reports have linked him to Bond, but recent claims about his position in the casting process conflict.

  2. Reported.
  3. Opinion.
  4. Not established.

Building your vocabulary and grammar workshop

Exercise 1. Finished past events

Complete in the past simple. Verbs: begin, play, train, win.

  1. Lowden a fighter pilot.
  2. Turner his career as a model.
  3. O’Connor an Emmy.
  4. O’Connor at Bristol Old Vic Theatre School.

Answer key

  1. played
  2. began
  3. won
  4. trained

Exercise 2. Past simple or present perfect?

Choose the correct form.

  1. Turner played / has played a rower in The Boys in the Boat in 2023.
  2. Several actors were linked / have been linked to Bond during the continuing casting discussion.
  3. O’Connor won / has won his Emmy in 2021.

Answer key

  1. played
  2. have been linked
  3. won

Rule. Use the past simple with a finished past date. Use the present perfect here to connect earlier reporting with an unfinished current discussion.

Exercise 3. Possibility is not certainty

Rewrite using might or could.

  1. Perhaps Lowden will play Bond.
  2. Perhaps Turner will create a more vulnerable Bond.
  3. Perhaps another actor will get the role.

Possible answers

  1. Lowden might play Bond.
  2. Turner could create a more vulnerable Bond.
  3. Another actor might get the role.

Exercise 4. Compare responsibly

Complete.

  1. This argument is convincing than an argument based only on appearance.
  2. A confirmed announcement is reliable than a rumour.
  3. An actor’s previous role does not prove that they are the choice.

Answer key

  1. more
  2. more
  3. best

Protected oral intervention

Each student speaks for 30 to 40 seconds.

I would choose .

In , the actor played .

This experience could be useful because .

However, .

My choice is a preference, not a prediction.

Partner question: which detail supports your opinion?

Debate rule: discuss performance, interpretation and relevant experience. Do not rank candidates by attractiveness or repeat stereotypes as facts.

  • □ I gave a complete spoken answer.
  • □ I listened to my partner.
  • □ I asked or answered a question.

Homework

Compulsory:

  1. Make three flashcards: successor, screen test, rumour.
  2. Write 70 to 90 words recommending an actor.
  3. Include one past-simple sentence, one comparison and one might/could sentence.

Extension: compare two headlines and explain which one expresses uncertainty more clearly.

Written record

A casting is not an official announcement.

Lowden, Turner and O’Connor have been to the role in media reporting.

I would choose because .

This actor might .

However, we cannot conclude that .

Model record

A casting rumour is not an official announcement. Lowden, Turner and O’Connor have been linked to the role in media reporting. I would choose Lowden because he has experience in espionage drama. He might create a thoughtful Bond. However, we cannot conclude that he has won the role.
