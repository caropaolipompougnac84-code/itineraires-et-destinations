Lesson 7. 1997 and 2020: speaking to the nation

How does the institution learn to show emotion, and to use memory?

Road map

Lesson of 55 minutes, timed for 52 minutes. 8 lessons.

  1. Recap (marked /3) 5 min
  2. The 1997 broadcast: “Queen and grandmother” 14 min
  3. The 2020 broadcast: anaphora and wartime memory 14 min
  4. Mediation in French, 100 words 14 min
  5. Exit ticket and homework 5 min
  • Written record. Notes on the two speeches (double identity; anaphora and memory) and the draft of the mediation.
  • Participation. Everyone speaks at least once: one sentence on 1997 or on 2020, so that everyone has spoken at least once.
  • Homework for next lesson. Finish the mediation if needed, and watch the official trailer of The Crown once, noting three images that look like history.

Stopping point. End of this lesson.

Emergency exit · 8 min. If time runs short at this stopping point, condense the end of the lesson: dictate a short written record (two sentences); a quick round so that everyone speaks at least once (one sentence each); write the homework on the board.

Recap · marked /3
  1. What is litotes in the 1992 speech?
  2. Why does the Queen quote a correspondent?
  3. How does the 1992 speech adapt dignified power to a media age?

Answer key

  1. “Not … with undiluted pleasure”: an understatement that admits a crisis without a display of feeling.
  2. To create distance: the judgement is borrowed, then accepted.
  3. By accepting that institutions must expect criticism, and by speaking at all.
“What I say to you now, as your Queen and as a grandmother, I say from my heart.” Elizabeth II, broadcast after the death of Diana, Princess of Wales, 5 September 1997. Full text: Royal Family website.
“We will be with our friends again; we will be with our families again; we will meet again.” Elizabeth II, broadcast during the Covid-19 pandemic, 5 April 2020. Full text: Royal Family website.
  • 1997. The public criticised her silence after Diana’s death. How does the double identity “Queen and grandmother” answer that criticism?
  • 2020. Anaphora, and the echo of Vera Lynn’s wartime song “We’ll Meet Again”. Why recall the Second World War?
  • Mediation in French, 100 words, for a French newspaper: why were these two short speeches so important? This is a mediation task, clearly labelled: write in French, for a French reader, and do not translate the speeches word for word.

Answer key · key points

In 1997 the Queen is forced to show emotion, which she usually avoided: the institution adapts under pressure. The double identity answers the charge of coldness without abandoning the role of Queen. In 2020 she uses collective memory to unite the country; the monarch becomes the voice of national continuity. Anaphora and the wartime song make the promise feel shared and familiar.

Exit ticket
  1. What does “Queen and grandmother” do that “Queen” alone would not do?
  2. Name the device in the 2020 ending and its effect.

Answer key

  1. It shows feeling and family, answering the charge of silence and coldness, while she still speaks as Queen.
  2. Anaphora (“we will… we will… we will…”), plus the echo of a wartime song: a shared promise of reunion.

Homework

Finish the mediation if needed, and prepare lesson 8: watch the official trailer of The Crown once (link on the next page) and note three images that look like history. We will ask which of them are invented or compressed.
