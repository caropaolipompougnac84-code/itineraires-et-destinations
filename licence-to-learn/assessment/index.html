Assessment

The final task is the job interview in lesson 6, timed for 52 minutes. The Spy Code from lesson 5 can be marked as written work.

Oral grid for the interview (out of 20)

CriteriaPoints
Understanding and answering the questions/5
Correct use of must, mustn’t, have to/5
Vocabulary from the sequence/4
Fluency and pronunciation/4
Interview attitude (eye contact, politeness)/2

Everyone should speak at least once, as recruiter and as candidate. If the oral has to be organised outside the 52-minute lesson, keep the same grid.

Written marking grid (out of 20)

Use this grid for a piece of written work in the sequence (the Spy Code, the debate paragraph, or the short account of the interview). The mark stays between 0 and 20: never below 0, never above 20.

Base: 10 points if the task is met.

Bonuses (up to plus 10)Points
Clear plan, followed+2
Introduction+1
Conclusion+1
Linking words+1
Figures of speech+1
Integrated quotations+2
General knowledge+2
Points taken off (up to minus 11)Points
Grammar: more than 5 errorsminus 3
Vocabulary: more than 5 errorsminus 5
Phrasing or reasoning: more than 3 errorsminus 3

Worked example: task met (10); plan, introduction and quotations (plus 5); grammar (minus 3). Total: 12 / 20.

This written grid comes from the earlier baccalauréat professionnel edition of the sequence. It can be used as it stands, or replaced by the usual mark scheme of the class. That is a decision for the teacher.
