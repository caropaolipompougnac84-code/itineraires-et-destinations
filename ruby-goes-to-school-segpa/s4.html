Lesson 4: Final task, my postcard to Ruby

English · SEGPA · A1 towards A2

HomeLesson 1Lesson 2Lesson 3Lesson 4: Final taskRelax timePlayDys versionWorksheetSources
Recap: what do you remember?
  1. Give two feelings in English.
  2. Who was Mrs Henry?
  3. Complete: I … Ruby is brave.
Answer key
  1. For example: brave, happy.
  2. Ruby's teacher. She came from Boston.
  3. I think Ruby is brave.
Key question: What do you want to tell Ruby?
What you need to say

Write a postcard to Ruby: three or four sentences. Then read it aloud to the class.

Model postcard

Dear Ruby,

My name is Léa. I am twelve.

In the painting, you are wearing a white dress.

In 1960, you were six. You were very brave.

From,
Léa

This postcard was written for this sequence. Léa is not a real student.

My postcard

1. Complete the postcardEntryguided

Word bank: white · six · brave · happy · dress · shoes

Dear Ruby,

My name is  . I am  .

In the painting, you are wearing a white  .

In 1960, you were  . You were  .

From,
 

Answer key

Example: My name is Tom. I am eleven. In the painting, you are wearing a white dress. In 1960, you were six. You were brave. From, Tom

2. Add one sentenceCoreproduction

Add one more sentence to your postcard.

Sentence starters

I think you are…

Mrs Henry was…

I like the painting because…

Answer key

Example: I think you are very strong. I like the painting because your dress is beautiful.

3. Read it aloudCorespeaking

Click Listen on the model. Then read your postcard to a partner, slowly and clearly. Then read it to the class.

Assessment grid

I can…Points
start with Dear Ruby and end with From + my name/ 2
say who I am (name, age)/ 2
describe Ruby in the painting (clothes, colours)/ 2
use the past: you were…/ 2
read my postcard aloud, clearly/ 2
Total/ 10
Note pour l'enseignante

Tâche finale : production écrite guidée (trois ou quatre phrases) puis lecture à voix haute, ce qui correspond au genre « carte postale » cité dans les stratégies A1+ du programme de 6e. Le modèle est rédigé pour la séquence et signalé comme tel. Les élèves peuvent recopier leur carte au propre sur le recto vierge de la fiche imprimable et dessiner un détail du tableau (chaussures, robe, brassard).

Stretch · A2

Write two more sentences about Mrs Henry or about the marshals.

Example answer

Mrs Henry was your best friend. Four marshals walked with you.

Relax time

Relax time: Happy Families! Take a break and play the card game with the words of the sequence: clothes, colours, feelings, people, school things, places and the past.

Homework
  1. Copy your postcard neatly on the worksheet.
  2. Read it aloud twice at home.
