Final task

Lesson outline

Lesson of 55 minutes, timed for 52 minutes. Lesson 7 sur 8.

  • 8 min · Remind students of the criteria and the aim of the task
  • 15 min · Preparation, with the documents already studied
  • 20 min · Performance or writing
  • 9 min · Retour bref et trace écrite
  • Written record. The plan of the task in three points.
  • Participation. everyone speaks at least once: one sentence each.
  • Homework for next lesson. Finish the task at home if it is not completed.

Stopping point. End of the final task. Next time: the summative assessment.

Emergency exit · 8 min. If time runs short at this stopping point, we condense: we dictate the plan of the task in three points ; a round of the class, one sentence each (everyone has spoken at least once) ; finish the task at home; the instruction is written on the board.

Write an argued essay of 300 words: Is the London Underground only a means of transport, or a work of art? Use at least three quotes (The Times, Pick, Pevsner, the Beck statement) and two images. Then present it orally for five minutes.
