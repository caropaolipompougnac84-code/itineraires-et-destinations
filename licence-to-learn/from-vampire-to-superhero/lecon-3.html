1. The casting call

CASTING CALL

Wanted: a new Batman

Gotham Lights Pictures is looking for an actor to play Bruce Wayne, the man behind the mask, in a dark detective film.

Requirements

  • must be dark: you can play a hero with secrets and pain
  • athletic: long action scenes, a heavy costume, night shoots
  • serious: no jokes, no smile, a quiet and intense hero
  • versatile: you have played very different roles
  • able to work under pressure: millions of fans are watching

We offer

  • the role of a lifetime
  • a six-month shoot in London and Chicago
  • a team of world-class professionals

How to apply

Send a short video presentation and a list of your past roles. Shortlisted candidates will meet the director for an interview.

Tâche finalePO · PE

Student A · The director

Ideas of trick questions:

Student B · Robert or his agent

Ideas of arguments:

Grammaire, pas à pas

Étape 1 · La forme du present perfect

have / has + participe passé : He has played many roles. He has worked with famous directors. He has been Batman.

Étape 2 · Bilan ou date ?

Le present perfect fait un bilan sans date : He has played a vampire. Le prétérit donne un moment daté : In 2008, he played a vampire. Avec ever, never, already : bilan. Avec in 2008, last year, ago : prétérit.

Étape 3 · Depuis quand ?

since + point de départ, for + durée : He has been an actor since 2004, for more than twenty years.

Étape 4 · Exprimer le contraste

although + phrase : Although he played a vampire, he is versatile. however en début de phrase, suivi d'une virgule. instead of + verbe en -ing : Instead of choosing easy films, he took risks. whereas oppose deux faits.

Figures de style

Le rythme ternaire
Trois éléments donnent de la force : dark, athletic, serious.
La question rhétorique
Une question qui n'attend pas de réponse et fait réfléchir : Is he the right person for the job?
L'hyperbole
L'exagération typique des annonces : the role of a lifetime, millions of fans.

Useful expressions

  • Donner son avis: In my opinion… / I'm convinced that…
  • Nuancer: That's true, but… / On the other hand…
  • Illustrer: For example… / Let me give you an example…
