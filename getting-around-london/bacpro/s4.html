Session 4: The lines and what they serve

Part 4. Which line will take you there?

Objectives

  • Read a network
  • Give directions

Warm-up

Name a London place you would like to visit.

Activities

  1. Which line goes to Heathrow Airport?
  2. You are at Paddington. How can you get to Westminster?
  3. Plan a day: three places, three lines.
  4. Find a station on the Bakerloo line where you can change to the Victoria line.

Sentence starters

  • Take the... line to...
  • Change at...
  • Get off at...
  • It takes about... minutes.

Role play

A tourist at King's Cross St Pancras asks the way to Camden Town. Use the Northern line, which serves both stations.

Gap-fill worksheet

Word bank: Piccadilly, red, Bank, Circus
  1. The ______ line serves Heathrow Airport.
  2. The Central line is ______ on the map.
  3. The Waterloo & City line links Waterloo and ______.
  4. Bakerloo and Victoria meet at Oxford ______.
Answer key
  1. Piccadilly
  2. red
  3. Bank
  4. Circus

Homework

Write directions from Baker Street to Canary Wharf.
