Money Matters

The Australian dollar (AUD)

In November 2026, €1 = about AUD 1.62, so AUD 1 = about €0.62. The Banque de France rate on 13 November 2026 was 1.6288. We keep "about 1.62" for the classroom calculations. Ask students to check the rate of the day (Banque de France, or their bank): it moves every day.

Minimum wage (from 1 July 2026): AUD 26.44 an hour, or AUD 1,004.90 a week for 38 hours. Casual workers get at least 25% more (about AUD 33 an hour), because they have no paid holidays. (Fair Work Ombudsman; casual loading: Fair Work Commission)

Life is expensive, especially rent in Sydney and Melbourne. Many backpackers share a room in a hostel or a flat ("share house").

Working holiday makers are taxed at 15% on the first AUD 45,000 (income year 2026-27). (Australian Taxation Office)

Exercise 4: plan your budget CE

  1. ★ Convert into euros: the visa (AUD 840) and the savings you need (AUD 5,000).
  2. ★ You work 30 hours a week as a casual at AUD 33 an hour. How much do you earn in a week, before tax?
  3. ★ Your hostel costs AUD 250 a week, food AUD 120, transport AUD 50. How much is left?
  4. ★★ Write a short budget for your first month in Australia, with your savings, your costs and your first salary. Is AUD 5,000 enough?

Useful words: wage, salary, payslip, rent, bond (deposit), tax, to save, to earn, to afford, exchange rate, bank transfer, casual job, full-time, part-time.

Corrigé (pour le professeur)

1. 840 ÷ 1,62 ≈ 520 € ; 5 000 ÷ 1,62 ≈ 3 090 €. 2. 30 × 33 = 990 AUD. 3. 990 − 420 = 570 AUD (avant impôts). 4. Réponse personnelle : valoriser la prise en compte du logement, de la caution (bond), du temps avant le premier salaire.
