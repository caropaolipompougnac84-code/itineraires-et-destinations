Lesson 1. Choose the next screen Bond

Goal: I can choose an actor and give a reason.

Road map

Lesson of 55 minutes, timed for 52 minutes. Lesson 1 of 6. Minutes 52 to 55: no new task. Working at home and need longer? Split the lesson into two blocks: this is a classroom structure, not a speed test.

  1. Read goal and word help 0 to 5
  2. Read text 5 to 13
  3. Exercise 1 13 to 21
  4. Grammar Exercise 2 21 to 29
  5. Exercise 3 29 to 36
  6. Speaking and flashcard 36 to 44
  7. Final spoken choice 44 to 47
  8. Homework 47 to 48
  9. Written record 48 to 52
  • Written record. Complete the short written record on rumour, choice and possibility.
  • Participation. Everyone speaks at least once: say your choice aloud, according to your route.
  • Homework for next lesson. make one played card; Route C adds won; Route P writes three casting sentences, including might.

Stopping point. Stopping point: minute 44. Stop the main exercises. Keep the written record and the homework, even if an exercise is unfinished.

Emergency exit · 8 min. If time runs short at this stopping point, this eight-minute ending replaces unfinished extension work inside the 52 minutes; it is not added to them.
  • 44 to 47: three minutes of speaking;
  • 47 to 48: one minute for homework;
  • 48 to 52: four minutes for the written record.
Short text

Newspapers have discussed several actors.

Jack Lowden played a pilot in Dunkirk.

He also appeared in Slow Horses.

Callum Turner appeared in The Capture.

He also played a pilot in Masters of the Air.

Josh O’Connor played Prince Charles in The Crown.

He won an Emmy.

These roles are facts about their careers.

A Bond casting rumour is different.

It is not an official announcement.

These facts were checked on 11 November 2026.

Word help

  • rumour: unconfirmed information;
  • role: a character an actor plays;
  • choose: decide which one you prefer;
  • might: perhaps.
Exercise 1

Find the actor

Worked example. Who appeared in Slow Horses?
Jack Lowden.
Now you.
  1. Who played Prince Charles?
  2. Who appeared in The Capture?
  3. Who played a pilot in Dunkirk?

Route S: names only. Route C: full sentences. Route P: add a possible reason for choosing one actor.

Self-check

  1. Josh O’Connor.
  2. Callum Turner.
  3. Jack Lowden.
Exercise 2

Grammar

Worked example. Today: He plays.
Past: He played.
Now you.

Choose:

  1. Lowden play / played a pilot.
  2. O’Connor win / won an Emmy.
  3. Turner appear / appeared in The Capture.

Extension example. Perhaps he will get the role. He might get the role. Now rewrite: perhaps another actor will get the role.

Self-check

  1. played
  2. won
  3. appeared

Extension: Another actor might get the role.

Exercise 3

Give a reason

Worked example. I choose Lowden because he has experience in a spy drama.
Now you.

Complete: I choose because .

Self-check

  • I named an actor.
  • I used because.
  • My reason matches the text.

Speaking

  • S: “I choose .”
  • C: “I choose because .”
  • P: add “However, this is my preference, not a confirmed result.”

Homework

Make one played card.

Route C: add won.

Route P: write three casting sentences, including might.

Written record

A rumour is not a confirmed .

I choose because .

Another actor might .

Answer key

fact; personal answer; get the role.
