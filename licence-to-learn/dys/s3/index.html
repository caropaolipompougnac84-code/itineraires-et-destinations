Lesson 3. MI5 or MI6?

I can say which service stays at home and which works abroad.

Road map

Lesson of 55 minutes, timed for 52 minutes. Lesson 3 of 6. Minutes 52 to 55: no new task. Working at home and need longer? Split the lesson into two blocks: this is a classroom structure, not a speed test.

  1. Say one sentence from the previous lesson (Lesson 2: Bond is fictional and works for MI6) 4 min (minutes 0 to 4)
  2. Read the goal and the word box 4 min (minutes 4 to 8)
  3. Read or listen to your route’s text 8 min (minutes 8 to 16)
  4. Exercise 1, then check it 8 min (minutes 16 to 24)
  5. Pause: look away from the text or move 2 min (minutes 24 to 26)
  6. Exercise 2, then check it 8 min (minutes 26 to 34)
  7. Exercise 3, then check it 6 min (minutes 34 to 40)
  8. Say your final answer aloud with the speaking frame 4 min (minutes 40 to 44)
  9. Written record 4 min (minutes 44 to 48)
  10. Progress record 2 min (minutes 48 to 50)
  11. Read and write down the homework 2 min (minutes 50 to 52)
  • Written record. One sentence for your route (see below), copied or typed.
  • Participation. Say at least one answer aloud. In class, use a quiet voice; at home, say it to yourself. You do not need to record yourself.
  • Homework for next lesson. Make two flashcards: abroad and headquarters. Say your written record twice.

Stopping point. Minute 44: stop the main exercises. Keep the written record and the homework, even if an exercise is unfinished.

Emergency exit · 8 min. If only eight working minutes remain, this replaces the unfinished plan; it is not added to it.
  1. Minutes 0 to 2: say the emergency sentence of the lesson aloud.
  2. Minutes 2 to 5: complete the short written record.
  3. Minutes 5 to 7: write down the homework.
  4. Minute 7 to 8: check one answer with the key. Do not rush through all the unfinished exercises.

Choose your route: Route S · SEGPA · Route C · CAP · Route P · Bac pro · CFA. Do only your route.

Route S · SEGPA

Short text

The United Kingdom has two famous services: MI5 and MI6.

MI5 works at home, inside the UK.

It protects the country.

Its headquarters is Thames House, in London.

MI6 works abroad, outside the UK.

Its headquarters is at Vauxhall Cross, in London.

A useful sentence: MI5 stays at home, MI6 travels abroad.

Exercise S1

Find information

Worked example. Question: Where does MI5 work? Answer: At home, inside the UK.

Now you.
1. Where does MI6 work?
2. What is the name of the MI5 building?

Self-check

1. Abroad, outside the UK.
2. Thames House.

Exercise S2

Complete

Worked example. “MI5 stays at home, MI6 travels abroad.”

Now you.
1. MI5 works .
2. MI6 works .

Self-check

1. at home.
2. abroad.

Exercise S3

Write and speak

Worked example. “MI5 stays at home, MI6 travels abroad.”

Now you.
Copy the mnemonic and say it aloud: “MI5 stays at home, MI6 travels abroad.”

Self-check

“MI5 stays at home, MI6 travels abroad.”

Route C · CAP

Short text

MI5 is the Security Service. It works inside the UK. It protects the country against threats at home. Its headquarters is Thames House.

MI6 is the Secret Intelligence Service. It works outside the UK. Its officers collect information in other countries. Its headquarters is at Vauxhall Cross.

Both services were created in 1909.

A French comparison can help: MI5 is close to the DGSI, and MI6 is close to the DGSE.

Mnemonic: MI5 stays at home, MI6 travels abroad.

Exercise C1

MI5, MI6 or both?

Worked example. “It works abroad.” Answer: MI6.

Now you.
1. It protects the UK at home.
2. Its headquarters is at Vauxhall Cross.
3. It was created in 1909.

Self-check

1. MI5.
2. MI6.
3. Both.

Exercise C2

Match

Worked example. “Thames House: MI5.”

Now you.
1. DGSI is the French equivalent of…
2. DGSE is the French equivalent of…

Self-check

1. MI5.
2. MI6.

Exercise C3

Write and speak

Worked example. “MI5 stays at home, MI6 travels abroad.”

Now you.
Write two sentences: one about MI5 and one about MI6. Then say the mnemonic aloud.

Self-check

Possible answer: “MI5 works at home, at Thames House. MI6 works abroad; its headquarters is at Vauxhall Cross.” Plus the mnemonic.

Route P · Bac pro

Short text

The United Kingdom has two main civilian intelligence services, created in 1909.

MI5, the Security Service, works on British soil. It deals with threats at home, including terrorism and foreign spies in the UK.

MI6, the Secret Intelligence Service, collects information outside the country.

The two headquarters are both in London, however their missions are not the same.

The letters “MI” mean “Military Intelligence”, a historical name.

A comparison with the DGSI and the DGSE can help, however no two services are identical.

Exercise P1

Identify the main point

Worked example. “The two buildings are in London, so the two missions are the same.” This is not the main point.

Now you.
Choose the best answer.
A. MI5 and MI6 have the same mission because both buildings are in London.
B. The two services have different missions: one at home, one abroad.
C. MI6 was created much later than MI5.

Self-check

B.

Exercise P2

Qualify

Worked example. “MI6 never works with information from the UK.” Improved: “MI6’s mission is abroad, but the two services can cooperate.”

Now you.
Improve:
1. “The DGSI and MI5 are exactly the same service.”
2. “Vauxhall Cross proves that Bond’s stories are all true.”

Self-check

Possible answers:
1. “The DGSI is a useful French comparison, however the services are not identical.”
2. “The building is real; Bond’s stories remain fiction.”

Exercise P3

Explain

Worked example. “Two services can share a city and still have different missions.”

Now you.
Write 50 to 70 words. Use however. Mention Thames House and Vauxhall Cross.

Self-check

Your answer should state the two missions clearly and use however.

CFA pathway

CAP apprentice: use Route C. Bac pro apprentice: use Route P. Do Exercises 1 and 2 of your route, then do this task instead of Exercise 3. Keep the same written record and homework.

CFA task

Choose the right service for the task

Worked example. “A problem inside the country: the service that stays at home.”

Now you.
Read these two tasks. Say which service is closer, and why.
1. Protect people against a threat inside the UK.
2. Collect information in another country.
Starter: “This task is closer to because…”

Self-check

1. MI5, because it works at home.
2. MI6, because it works abroad.

Written record

S: “MI5 stays at home, MI6 travels abroad.”

C: “MI5 works at home, at Thames House; MI6 works abroad, from Vauxhall Cross.”

P: “Both headquarters are in London, however MI5 works at home and MI6 works abroad.”

Speaking frame. “MI5 stays at , MI6 travels .”

Emergency sentence. “MI5 stays at home, MI6 travels abroad.”

Homework

Make two flashcards: abroad and headquarters. Say your written record twice.

  • I checked my answers.
  • I said at least one answer aloud.
  • I kept my written record.
