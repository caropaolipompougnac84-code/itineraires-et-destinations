How US Elections Work: from the Primaries to the Midterms

Première baccalauréat professionnel. Contextes du programme : vie quotidienne, personnelle, sociale et civique. Thème : histoire, institutions, élections. Niveau B1, with an entry level.

For the student who cannot come to class, or who is in hospital. Work through the lesson in order. The answer keys are available in this version: try first, then open them to check. Send your work to your teacher.

Levels: entry, core, stretch, oral

Entry (guided B1): guided tasks with word banks and sentence starters. Core (B1): the class version. Stretch (B1+): longer explanations and comparisons. Oral: a speaking version of each lesson.

1. Who votes? Who governs?Label the diagram with three words; describe the drawing with five given sentences to complete.Describe the drawing in six sentences with there is, there are.Explain in a short paragraph why the right to vote was won step by step.Present the 1867 drawing in pairs in one minute.
2. Two parties, two animalsComplete the table with the words given.Describe one cartoon in five sentences.Compare the two cartoons with comparatives and whereas.Present your invented party symbol in thirty seconds.
3. The road to the White House: primaries and conventionsPut five picture cards of the timeline in order.Explain the steps with first, then, after that, finally.Compare a primary and a caucus in a short paragraph.Present your banner in thirty seconds.
4. Election Day and the Electoral CollegeComplete the diagram with the numbers given.Calculate the electors of three states and explain.Explain why a candidate can win the votes of the people but lose in the Electoral College.Explain the diagram to a partner in one minute.
5. Swing states: where the election is wonRead the bar chart and answer three yes or no questions.Write the fact file and present it.Explain in a paragraph why a candidate can lose with more votes.Present your swing state in one minute.
6. Congress: the House and the SenateComplete the table with the numbers given.Explain the thirds diagram in four sentences.Write a paragraph on the 1889 cartoon: who really governs?Explain how a bill becomes a law in one minute.
