Lesson 5. Regional work and the White Card

Does every farm job count towards a second visa, and what must you check on a building site?

Road map

Lesson of 55 minutes, timed for 52 minutes. 8 lessons.

  1. Recap (marked /3) 5 min
  2. Regional work: places to investigate, and what the photographs show 12 min
  3. Specified work for a second or third visa 16 min
  4. Construction and the White Card 10 min
  5. Questions, written record and homework 9 min
  • Written record. Specified work is not any farm job: a second 417 visa normally requires three months (at least 88 calendar days of eligible full-time-equivalent work). A White Card is required for construction work.
  • Participation. Everyone speaks at least once: one reason why a farm job might not count, or one safety question to ask on a building site.
  • Homework for next lesson. On the official specified-work page, note one rule you must check (industry, postcode or counting). Give the link. Add flashcards: specified work, 88 calendar days, White Card.

Stopping point. End of this lesson.

Emergency exit · 8 min. If time runs short at this stopping point, condense the end of the lesson: dictate a short written record (two sentences); a quick round so that everyone speaks at least once (one sentence each); write the homework on the board.

Recap · marked /3
  1. What is casual loading?
  2. What does RSA stand for, and is one certificate enough for every state?
  3. Give one question to ask about pay before you accept a job.

Answer key

  1. Extra pay for a casual employee, instead of paid leave (25% on the adult National Minimum Wage, when that rate applies).
  2. Responsible Service of Alcohol; no, check the state where you will work.
  3. Accept: “Which award applies?”, “What is my casual hourly rate?”, “What are the weekend rates?”

Regional work and fruit picking

Bundaberg, Mildura and Shepparton are places to investigate for agricultural work. The photographs show Fleurbaix in Queensland, not those three locations.

For a French passport holder, a second subclass 417 visa normally requires three months of eligible specified work, counted under Home Affairs rules as at least 88 calendar days of full-time-equivalent work. A third visa normally requires six months during the second visa. These are different requirements. Not every farm job, location or day automatically qualifies. Keep payslips and records, and check the eligible industry, postcode and counting rules before accepting work.

Home Affairs, specified-work conditions. Confirm the current rules before you rely on them.

Construction: White Card

General construction induction training and a White Card are required for construction work. Check the recognised training and state rules. Wear the required protective equipment. Ask about site induction and safe working practices; do not trade safety for a higher wage.

Exit ticket
  1. How long is the usual specified-work requirement for a second subclass 417 visa?
  2. Does every farm job automatically count?

Answer key

  1. Three months, normally at least 88 calendar days of eligible full-time-equivalent work, under Home Affairs rules. Recheck the official page.
  2. No. The industry, postcode and counting rules must match.

Homework

On the official specified-work page, write down one rule you must check and the link. Add flashcards: specified work, 88 calendar days, White Card.
