Lesson 9. Final task and summative assessment

Question of the sequence: Is a legacy what a person leaves behind, or what other people decide to make of it?

For the teacher: timing (52 minutes)
Step Time Activity
1 0-3 Recap and instructions
2 3-10 Final rehearsal in groups
3 10-34 Final task: group performances (about 6 min per group, 4 groups; other groups in the next hour if needed)
4 34-49 Written assessment: reading (Part C) and writing (Part D)
5 49-52 Homework: self-correction

Stop point (minute 34). All groups have performed (or the remaining groups are scheduled at the start of the next hour).

Emergency exit (8 minutes). If the class is large, Parts C and D are done at home as a timed task (30 minutes), and the lesson is used only for the performances.

Step 1. Recap

  1. Give one goal of the Imagination Library. 2. Make it passive: They send a book every month. 3. Name the three backstage professionals.

    Answer key

    Answer key: 1 so that children can love reading · 2 A book is sent every month. · 3 the dresser, the hairdresser, the make-up artist.

Final task: Backstage at the tribute concert

Student sheet

Your group (four students) prepares a tribute concert for Dolly Parton. You must:

  1. Present Dolly (2 minutes, two students): her childhood, her look, 9 to 5, Dollywood, her generosity. Past simple, used to, one comparative.
  2. Play one backstage scene (2-3 minutes, two students): dresser, hairdresser or make-up artist, with the role cards of Lesson 3.
  3. True or Tale? (1 minute, whole group): tell the audience about the inheritance rumour and explain why it is not verified.
  4. Conclusion (30 seconds, one student): answer the question of the sequence.

Requirements: at least three past forms, one reported sentence, two modals of deduction, five words of the Dolly look, one purpose sentence, one source named for every fact (“according to...”).

Checklist before you perform
- [ ] Every member speaks at least 1 minute 30.
- [ ] We don’t read: we use cards with key words only.
- [ ] We have named at least three sources.
- [ ] We use at least ten words from our flashcards.

Part C · Reading: fact-check article (5 points)

Fact-check: did Miley inherit Dolly’s millions?
After Dolly Parton’s death in August 2026, a video spread online. In it, Miley Cyrus seems to say that her godmother left her entire fortune to her. Fact-checkers at Lead Stories analysed the clip with a detection tool: it is very likely AI-generated. The voice is monotone and the lips do not match the words. According to court documents, Dolly’s property is managed by a private trust created in 1997, so the names of the people who inherit are not public. In 2020, Dolly said she had organised everything so that nobody would have to deal with a “mess”. Miley’s only public message after the death was a tribute about love, grief and music. Experts say: before you share, check who posted it and whether reliable media report it.
(Text written for this assessment from the sources of Lesson 7.)

  1. True or false? Fact-checkers think the video is real. Justify.
  2. Why are the names of the people who inherit not public?
  3. What did Dolly say in 2020?
  4. What was Miley’s only public message about?
  5. Give the two pieces of advice from the experts.
Answer key

Answer key. 1 False: it is very likely AI-generated (1). 2 Because the property is managed by a private trust (1). 3 That she had organised everything so that nobody would have to deal with a mess (1). 4 Love, grief and music: it was a tribute (1). 5 Check who posted it; check whether reliable media report it (1).

Part D · Writing (5 points)

Choose one subject (100 words):
1. Write a tribute to Dolly Parton for your school website.
2. Describe one of Dolly’s stage looks and give your opinion.
3. Answer the question of the sequence: Is a legacy what a person leaves behind, or what other people make of it?

Criterion 0 1 2
Content: relevant, organised, facts correct off topic partly relevant relevant and organised
Language: syntax (tenses, passive, purpose, modals) many errors some errors mostly correct, varied
Language: vocabulary of the sequence fewer than 5 words 5 words or more

Model for subject 2. For the show, Dolly wore a sparkly pink rhinestone suit with high white boots. Her big platinum wig had soft curls and a lot of volume. Her make-up was bright: lilac eyeshadow, false lashes and scarlet lipstick. I think her look was glamorous and a bit over the top, but it was her own style. As she joked, it cost a lot of money to look that cheap! Although she looked artificial, she never forgot the coat of rags of her childhood.

Model for subject 3. I think a legacy is both. On the one hand, Dolly left real things: songs, a park that employs thousands of people, and a library that has sent more than 330 million books. These things exist whatever people say. On the other hand, other people decide what to make of them: Warhol turned her into an icon, Miley sings her songs, and some people even invented a fake inheritance. That is why we must check our sources: a legacy should be remembered, not invented.

Homework (after the sequence)

  1. Self-correction of the summative assessment (grid above + improved version).
  2. Write three sentences: What can I say now that I could not say before this sequence?
  3. Optional, at home: watch the film 9 to 5 (1980) in English with English subtitles, and write five sentences comparing the film and the song.

Passing the torch. From the cabin in the Smoky Mountains to the Warhol portrait, from 9 to 5 to the Imagination Library, Dolly passed on songs, values and courage. Miley carries part of the torch; you can carry another part.
