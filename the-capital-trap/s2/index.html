Lesson 2. Discovering the map

Oceans, rivers and lakes first: read the map of the United States as a story of water.

Road map

Lesson of 55 minutes, timed for 52 minutes. 7 lessons, plus a written and an oral assessment.

  1. Warm-up: the political map, countries, oceans and lakes 8 min
  2. Photo or map: locate three photographs 10 min
  3. Expanding your vocabulary, and exercises 1 and 2 16 min
  4. Map search: oceans, neighbours, rivers, lakes, Alaska and Hawaii 10 min
  5. Written record 8 min
  • Written record. The USA is a country in North America. It has 50 states. It borders Canada and Mexico. Its capital is Washington, D.C. Alaska and Hawaii are not connected to the other states.
  • Participation. Everyone speaks at least once: each student locates one feature on the map, in a full sentence: “The Rio Grande is in the south, between Texas and Mexico.”
  • Homework for next lesson. Learn the locating verbs and the phrases in the north of / north of. On a map, find three states that border Canada.

Stopping point. After the map search, before a longer correction of exercise 2 if time is short.

Emergency exit · 8 min. If time runs short at this stopping point, condense the end of the lesson inside the 52 minutes (this is not 52 plus 8). Dictate a short written record (two sentences). A quick round so that everyone speaks at least once (one sentence each). Write the homework on the board.

Warm-up · 8 min

Project the National Atlas map. Ask: What can you see? Which countries, oceans and lakes? Where are Alaska and Hawaii?

Photo or map · 10 min

Show one photograph at a time. Students locate it on the map and say where it is, in a full sentence. Then reveal the caption.

Model sentence

It’s the Rio Grande. It’s in the south, between Texas and Mexico.

Expanding your vocabulary · 16 min

Exercise 1. Complete with the map

  1. The Mississippi ______ into the Gulf of Mexico.
  2. Texas ______ Mexico.
  3. Alaska is ______ from Russia by the Bering Strait.
  4. Niagara Falls are located ______ New York State and Canada.
  5. Hawaii is a group of ______ in the Pacific Ocean.
  6. Denver is ______, far from the coast.

Answer key

1. flows · 2. borders · 3. separated · 4. between · 5. islands · 6. inland

Exercise 2. In the north of or north of?

  1. Canada is ______ the USA.
  2. Chicago is ______ Illinois.
  3. Mexico is ______ Texas.
  4. Miami is ______ Florida.

Answer key

1. north of · 2. in the north of · 3. south of · 4. in the south of

Map search · 10 min

On the maps, students find:

  • two oceans and one gulf;
  • two neighbouring countries;
  • three rivers: the Mississippi, the Missouri, the Rio Grande;
  • the Great Lakes;
  • two states separated from the rest: Alaska and Hawaii.

Written record. The USA is a country in North America. It has 50 states. It borders Canada and Mexico. Its capital is Washington, D.C. Alaska and Hawaii are not connected to the other states.
