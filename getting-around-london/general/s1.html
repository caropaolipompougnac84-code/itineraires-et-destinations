Session 1: The ad that made us laugh

Part 1. Why is this ad about the Tube so funny?

Objectives

  • Describe a video in English
  • Spot British humour and hidden references

Warm-up

What do you know about London? Say three words. What makes British humour different?

Documents

Video. Watch the reel on Instagram. Watch it twice: once for fun, once with a pen.

Activities

  1. First viewing: say what happens in three sentences.
  2. Second viewing: list everything you can see in the background.
  3. Find references to British fantasy or famous British stories. Explain each one.
  4. Which moment is the funniest? Say why. Is it irony, understatement, absurd humour or self mockery?
  5. Look at the Baker Street photo. Explain why it is ironic.
  6. Write a paragraph of 120 words: how does the ad use British stereotypes to sell something?

Sentence starters

  • In the background, I can see...
  • It reminds me of...
  • This is funny because...
  • It is ironic because...

Role play

Student A has just watched the ad. Student B has not. A describes it and B asks questions.

Gap-fill worksheet

Word bank: Underground, background, ironic
  1. The ad is about the London ______.
  2. In the ______, I can see many details.
  3. Sherlock Holmes with a pipe next to a No smoking sign is ______.
Answer key
  1. Underground
  2. background
  3. ironic

Homework

Find one other British ad or sketch you like. Present it in five sentences.
