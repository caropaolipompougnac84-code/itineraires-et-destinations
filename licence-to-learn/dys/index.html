Licence to Learn · adapted version

Are real spies like James Bond? Six lessons you can do alone: SEGPA, CAP, CFA and Bac pro.

Road map

Six lessons of 55 minutes, each timed for 52 minutes. Every lesson has the same steps, a stopping point at minute 44, an 8-minute emergency exit, a written record, a moment when you speak, and homework.

  1. Lesson 1. Who will be the next 007? 52 min
    I can say that no official actor has been announced.
  2. Lesson 2. Bond, James Bond 52 min
    I can say that Bond is fictional and works for MI6.
  3. Lesson 3. MI5 or MI6? 52 min
    I can say which service stays at home and which works abroad.
  4. Lesson 4. Fiction vs reality 52 min
    I can tell a film title from a real fact.
  5. Lesson 5. The rules of a spy 52 min
    I can use must, mustn’t and don’t have to.
  6. Lesson 6. The job interview 52 min
    I can answer interview questions, even if I work alone.

Also: my flashcards and the teacher notes.

Start here

This version keeps the same themes as the standard sequence: the next Bond actor, MI5 and MI6, film and reality, obligation and prohibition, and a job interview.

You can do every lesson alone: without an AESH, without a partner, and even if you missed the class. Every exercise has a worked example before it and a self-check key after it. No exercise needs an internet search.

What you need

  • this page, on screen or printed;
  • paper or a notebook;
  • a pen, a keyboard or a speech-to-text tool;
  • a timer, if you have one.

You do not need

  • an AESH;
  • a printer;
  • an internet account;
  • another student;
  • a recording device;
  • a Bond film or a trailer (a short text is enough).

CAPRoute CA2A2 is the examination target where a language unit is included.
CFARoute C or Route P, according to your qualificationThe level of your qualificationA CFA is a training setting, not a CEFR level. Follow the CFA pathway in each lesson.
Bac proRoute PSupported B1, towards B1+ in LVAThe examination target is B1+ in LVA and B1 in LVB.

Vocational programme: langues vivantes in the vocational pathway, arrêté of 3 April 2019, Bulletin officiel spécial n° 5 of 11 April 2019. The routes are not fixed: if Route S is easy, move on; a Bac pro student may use a shorter step for a while without being kept at a lower target.

Dyslexia support does not mean a lower language level. Access support (layout, short instructions, read-aloud, less copying) is separate from the level of English. A student can need a lot of reading support and still work towards B1 or B1+.

How to do an exercise

  1. Read the short text.
  2. Read the worked example.
  3. Do the task.
  4. Read the self-check key.
  5. Correct one answer if necessary.
  6. Say your corrected answer aloud.

If you do not understand a word

  1. Look at the word box.
  2. Read the whole sentence again.
  3. Use the sentence starter.
  4. If you are still blocked, look at the key.
  5. Cover the key.
  6. Try again.

Using the key to restart is allowed. Copying it without trying again does not help you practise.

Reading and listening. The short texts can be read aloud by your device (button “Listen”). Listen once, then listen again while following the words, and pause after each sentence. Every task can also be done by reading only.

Timing for every lesson

MinutesWhat to do
0 to 4Say one sentence from the previous lesson (Lesson 1: what you already know about spies or Bond)
4 to 8Read the goal and the word box
8 to 16Read or listen to your route’s text
16 to 24Exercise 1, then check it
24 to 26Pause: look away from the text or move
26 to 34Exercise 2, then check it
34 to 40Exercise 3, then check it
40 to 44Say your final answer aloud with the speaking frame
44 to 48Written record
48 to 50Progress record
50 to 52Read and write down the homework
52 to 55Transition time. No new task.

Stopping point: stop the main exercises at minute 44. Keep the written record and the homework.

Emergency exit · 8 min, when only eight working minutes remain:
  1. Minutes 0 to 2: say the emergency sentence of the lesson aloud.
  2. Minutes 2 to 5: complete the short written record.
  3. Minutes 5 to 7: write down the homework.
  4. Minute 7 to 8: check one answer with the key. Do not rush through all the unfinished exercises.
