Learn how to learn · a method in every step

In this journey, you learn English, and you also learn how to learn. Scientists who study memory agree: these habits work better than reading your notes again and again.

Our four learning habits

Quick RecallEvery lesson starts the same way: 5 minutes, no notes. 1 minute to choose your method, 3 minutes to remember, 1 minute to check.
Say it, don’t re-read itExplain to a partner, defend a view, or write a 5-line summary, with sentence starters.
Mistakes are dataA mistake shows what to improve. After a game or a task: what went wrong, what I’ll try next. Nobody is blamed.
We help each otherGroups help each other progress. We criticise ideas, never people.

Each step also gives you one short method:

1 · What I know / what I don’t know yetStart by checking what you already know. 2 · Words + picturesDraw the numbers: your brain keeps them better. 3 · Test yourselfClose the text and remember. Background1921 · 1971

When the FA banned women’s football

What? On 5 December 1921, the Football Association (the FA) banned women’s teams from playing on the grounds of its member clubs.

Who? Women’s football was very popular during and after the First World War. On Boxing Day 1920, 53,000 fans watched Dick, Kerr Ladies play St Helen’s Ladies at Goodison Park, in Liverpool.

When? The ban lasted almost fifty years. The FA voted to end it in January 1970, and the rule changed in June 1971.

Why it matters For decades, women played in public parks, without big stadiums, money or television. This helps to explain why the women’s game is still catching up today, even after the Lionesses’ victories.

“…the game of football is quite unsuitable for females and ought not to be encouraged.”FA Council, 5 December 1921 (FA minute book, National Football Museum)

Sources: The FA: the story of women’s football in England · National Football Museum: FA minute book, 1921

Transcript

In 1968, the Ford car factory in Dagenham, east of London, was one of the biggest factories in Britain. Thousands of men built cars there.

In one workshop, a hundred and eighty-seven women worked as sewing machinists. They made the seat covers for the cars. It was difficult, skilled work.

Should footballers be paid the same, men and women?

Durée : 35 min · Documents : Lesson 1

Sans connexion, demander l’enregistrement à l’enseignante par l’ENT, ou faire lire le transcript deux fois par un proche. Noter qui sont Jordan et Amy, et les deux montants du gross pay (6 min).

PO Écrire puis s’enregistrer en trois étapes : un avis (In my opinion, it is fair / unfair because…), l’autre camp (On the other hand, the club says that…), une conclusion (So I think the club should…). Lesson summary

Copy it into your exercise book.

Jordan Reid and Amy Clarke both play for Riverside Rovers. They are both strikers, and they both scored three goals.

Jordan’s gross pay is £6,100. Amy’s gross pay is £2,150: almost three times as much. Gross pay is all the pay, before tax.

The club says the men’s matches sell more tickets and bring in more money. Amy says: we train just as hard, and we score just as many goals.

Check your answers

  1. Complete: Jordan earns almost three times ______ Amy.

    as much as

  2. What is gross pay?

    All the pay, before tax.

Lesson 3 · Dagenham, 1968

What is fact, and what is story, in the Dagenham strike and in the film?

Durée : 35 min · Documents : Lesson 3

  1. CO Écouter deux fois l’enregistrement Dagenham, 1968 sur la page de la lesson (ouvrir la lesson). Noter ce que les 187 femmes fabriquaient, la date de la grève, et les trois dates 1968, 1970 et 1984 (8 min).
  2. Spirale de Gorse sur la lesson : cinq étapes, l’idée à l’extérieur, la ligne de la fiche à l’intérieur (8 min).
  3. Étude de la langue Fermer le texte, faire le quiz de la page sans le regarder, puis se corriger d’une autre couleur. Recopier : In 1968, a hundred and eighty-seven women went on strike at Ford’s Dagenham factory. They made the seat covers for the cars. (6 min).
  4. CO Regarder deux fois la bande-annonce de Made in Dagenham (2010) (ouvrir la bande-annonce) et cocher les mots entendus : strike, equal pay, women, Ford, Parliament, fight (5 min).
  5. PE Pour chacune des quatre lignes Fact or story?, choisir true, simplified but not false ou not true, et expliquer pourquoi. Puis réécrire avec plus de précision : In 1968, women at Ford’s Dagenham factory went on strike for equal pay. (8 min).

Lesson summary

Copy it into your exercise book.

On 7 June 1968, 187 sewing machinists went on strike at Ford’s Dagenham factory. They made the seat covers. Their skilled work was put in a less skilled grade.

After about three weeks they went back with a pay rise, to 92 per cent of the men’s rate. It was not equality yet, but it was a start.

Parliament passed the Equal Pay Act in 1970. The sewing machinists were graded as skilled workers in 1984. Rita O’Grady, in the film, is an invented character.

Check your answers

  1. When did the Dagenham strike start, and what did the women make?

    On 7 June 1968. They made the seat covers.

  2. Is Rita O’Grady a real person?

    No. She is an invented character, inspired by the real women.

Version courte (15 min) : écouter l’enregistrement une fois, apprendre les trois dates 1968, 1970 et 1984, et répondre à la question : Rita est-elle réelle ?

À envoyer ou à rendre : la spirale, les choix Fact or story? et la phrase réécrite.

Lesson 4 · At Work Today

What does equal pay mean in a job advert, in the law, and in a future trade?

Durée : 35 min · Documents : Lesson 4

  1. CE Lire les quatre annonces inventées sur la page de la lesson (ouvrir la lesson). Souligner les mots qui excluent les hommes ou les femmes, expliquer le problème, puis réécrire chaque annonce pour tout le monde, avec les compétences qui comptent et We welcome all applicants. (10 min).
  2. CO Écouter deux fois l’enregistrement Equal pay at work: the rules today. Répondre au quiz de la page, puis recopier l’encadré Careful!: unequal pay n’est pas la même chose que the gender pay gap (8 min).
  3. Spirale de Gorse sur la lesson : cinq étapes, l’idée à l’extérieur, la ligne de la fiche à l’intérieur (8 min).
  4. PO Choisir un métier (le métier préparé, ou un métier de la liste). S’enregistrer une minute : In my future job, I will be a… In this job, men and women should… I want to be paid for my skills. Ne pas parler de sa propre paie, parler du métier. Une PFMP déjà faite peut servir d’exemple pour décrire le poste, pas le salaire (6 min).
  5. Étude de la langue Fabriquer huit flashcards (mot ou question au recto, réponse et exemple au verso), à partir des lessons déjà faites. Se tester une fois : pile I know et pile Not yet (3 min).

Lesson summary

Copy it into your exercise book.

A job advert cannot say “men only” or “women only”, except in very rare cases. A fair advert names the skills and says: we welcome all applicants.

In Great Britain today, the Equality Act 2010 protects workers. A man and a woman who do equal work for the same employer must get equal pay.

The gender pay gap compares all jobs together (6.9 per cent for full-time workers in April 2025). It is not the same thing as unequal pay, which is against the law.

Check your answers

  1. What is the difference between unequal pay and the gender pay gap?

    Unequal pay: less money for the same job, against the law. The gender pay gap: the difference in all jobs together.

  2. Which law protects workers in Great Britain today?

    The Equality Act 2010.

Version courte (15 min) : réécrire une annonce, recopier la distinction de l’encadré Careful!, et s’enregistrer trente secondes : In my future job…

À envoyer ou à rendre : la spirale, les annonces réécrites, l’enregistrement d’une minute et une photo des huit flashcards.

Lesson 5 · Create Your Own Game

How can a game, with clear rules in English, teach equal pay at work?

Durée : 35 min · Documents : Lesson 5

  1. CE Lire les cinq cas d’Equal Pay Quest sur la page de la lesson (ouvrir la lesson). Choisir une réponse par cas, former le code secret, puis se corriger (8 min).
  2. Étude de la langue Répéter à voix haute les amorces de règles : The aim of the game is to… Each player must… If you land on…, you… You win when… You miss a turn if… Before you start,… (4 min).
  3. PE Inventer un jeu sur equal at work, différent du modèle. Remplir la fiche Game Designer: nom, type, matériaux, et les règles en anglais avec les amorces (10 min).
  4. Spirale de Gorse sur la lesson : cinq étapes, l’idée à l’extérieur, la ligne de la fiche à l’intérieur (8 min).

Lesson summary

Copy it into your exercise book.

The secret code of Equal Pay Quest is EQUAL: Experience, Qualifications, Union, Ability, Law. At work, everybody should have the same chances and the same pay for the same job.

To write a rule: The aim of the game is to… If you land on…, you… You win when…

If a rule can be explained clearly to someone else, it is clear enough. We criticise ideas and rules, never people.

Check your answers

  1. What is the secret code of Equal Pay Quest, and what does each letter mean?

    EQUAL: Experience, Qualifications, Union, Ability, Law.

  2. Complete: If you land on a red square, you ______.

    answer a question (or: answer a question card).
