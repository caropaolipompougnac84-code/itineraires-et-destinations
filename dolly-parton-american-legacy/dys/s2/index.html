Lesson 2. Rags to rhinestones

What I need to say. I tell Dolly’s childhood and I describe her look.

Short text. Dolly was born in 1946 in Tennessee. Her family was poor. Her mother made her a coat with small pieces of fabric. At school, children laughed at her. In 1971, Dolly wrote a song about this coat.

Listen: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KFbYFHaigSk (from 0:00 to 3:04). While you listen, raise your hand or tick each time you hear the word coat.

Exact link, to keep unchanged: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KFbYFHaigSk

Rule.
- Past simple: make → made, wear → wore, write → wrote, grow up → grew up.
- Habit in the past: used to + verb → She used to sing with her mother.
- Order of adjectives: opinion → size → colour → noun: a beautiful long pink dress.

Word bank: cabin · coat · patchwork · rags · wig · curls · rhinestones · sequins · false lashes

Exercise 1. Past simple.
Example: sing → sang ✔
1. make 2. wear 3. write

Exercise 2. Put in order.
Example: hair / big / blonde → big blonde hair ✔
1. dress / pink / a / long 2. boots / white / high

Exercise 3. used to.
Example: She (live) in a cabin. → She used to live in a cabin. ✔
1. Her mother (sing) ballads.

Homework. Learn six words of the Dolly look (flashcards). Record one sentence about her look.

Answers. Ex 1: made, wore, wrote. Ex 2: a long pink dress; high white boots. Ex 3: Her mother used to sing ballads.
