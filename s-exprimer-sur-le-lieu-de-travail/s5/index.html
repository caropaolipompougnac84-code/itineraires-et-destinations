Lesson 5 · 55 minutes · CAP (A2) et bac pro (B1)

Lesson 5. Safety first

Pour l'enseignant. Comprendre et expliquer une règle de sécurité, employer les modaux.

Durée. 55 minutes.

Activités langagières. compréhension de l'écrit, expression orale en continu.

Objectifs. À la fin de la leçon, l'élève peut : comprendre un avis de sécurité écrit en anglais ; employer must, have to, should, can, can't dans un contexte professionnel ; présenter à l'oral, en continu, une règle de sécurité à un visiteur.

Langue de la leçon. You must wear safety glasses. You have to switch off the machine. You should ask your tutor. You can leave your bag in the locker. You can't go through this area. You mustn't run in the workshop. Nuance : must / have to (obligation), should (conseil), can / can't (permission ou capacité, interdiction pour can't et mustn't).

Phonologie. mustn't (/ˈmʌsnt/), sans faire sonner le premier t de manière lourde, mais en gardant le mot reconnaissable. have to (/hæftə/). Opposition can faible / can't plein.

Version adaptée dys de la Lesson 5.

Amorce (5 min)

Pour l'enseignant

L'œuvre au tableau. Question orale, sans encore enseigner les modaux de manière formelle : on recueille des propositions, on les reformule.

Pour les élèves

Look at the painting. This is a school workshop, with workbenches and cardboard boxes. A visitor is coming. What safety rules should these people follow? Say one rule with your partner, in English. You can start with You must... or Don't...

Activité 1. Read a safety notice (12 min). Compréhension de l'écrit.

Pour les élèves, texte A2

Workshop safety rules

Please read these rules carefully. They are for your safety and for the safety of other people.

  1. You must wear safety shoes in the workshop. You must also wear gloves when you handle materials.
  2. You have to switch off a machine before you clean it or check it.
  3. You should ask your tutor if you are not sure about a task.
  4. You can leave your bag and your phone in the locker. You can't leave them on the workbench.
  5. You mustn't run in the workshop. You mustn't block the area in front of the windows or the doors.
  6. If you see a problem, tell your tutor immediately.

Thank you for your attention. Have a safe day.

Pour les élèves, complément B1

Workshop safety rules

Please read these rules carefully. They are for your safety and for the safety of other people.

  1. You must wear safety shoes in the workshop. You must also wear gloves when you handle materials.
  2. You have to switch off a machine before you clean it or check it.
  3. You should ask your tutor if you are not sure about a task.
  4. You can leave your bag and your phone in the locker. You can't leave them on the workbench.
  5. You mustn't run in the workshop. You mustn't block the area in front of the windows or the doors.
  6. If you see a problem, tell your tutor immediately.

Thank you for your attention. Have a safe day.

These rules apply to everyone: young people, adults, visitors and PFMP students. If a visitor comes in, you should welcome them and explain the basic rules: safety shoes, no running, and no entry into a restricted area. You must not start an explanation near a machine that is on. Walk with the visitor, keep the workbenches clear, and never leave a cardboard box in a passageway.

Pour les élèves, questions

  1. What must you wear in the workshop?
  2. When do you have to switch off a machine?
  3. What should you do if you are not sure?
  4. Where can you leave your bag? Where can't you leave it?
  5. Find two rules with mustn't.
  6. True or false: If you see a problem, you can wait until the end of the day to talk about it.
  7. (B1) Who do these rules apply to?
  8. (B1) What should you do when a visitor comes in?

Corrigé

  1. Safety shoes, and gloves when you handle materials.
  2. Before you clean it or check it.
  3. You should ask your tutor.
  4. You can leave it in the locker. You can't leave it on the workbench.
  5. You mustn't run in the workshop. You mustn't block the area in front of the windows or the doors.
  6. False. You must tell your tutor immediately.
  7. To everyone: young people, adults, visitors and PFMP students.
  8. You should welcome them and explain the basic rules (safety shoes, no running, no entry into a restricted area). You must not start an explanation near a machine that is on.

Activité 2. Must, have to, should, can (12 min).

Pour l'enseignant

Tableau à quatre colonnes, rempli avec des exemples tirés du texte. Puis exercice d'application immédiat.

Pour les élèves

Expanding your vocabulary: safety

  • safety shoes, safety glasses, gloves, a helmet, a locker
  • a rule, a notice, a visitor, a restricted area, a passageway
  • to wear, to handle, to switch off, to block, to report a problem
  • immediately, carefully, basic rules, Have a safe day.

Grammar in context

  • must and have to: it is necessary. You must wear safety shoes. You have to switch off the machine.
  • should: it is a good idea. You should ask your tutor.
  • can: it is possible or permitted. You can leave your bag in the locker.
  • can't and mustn't: it is not permitted. You can't leave your bag on the workbench. You mustn't run.

Exercise. Complete with must, have to, should, can, can't or mustn't. More than one answer is sometimes possible. Be ready to explain your choice.

  1. You _______________ wear a helmet on the building site.
  2. You _______________ ask your tutor if you don't understand.
  3. Visitors _______________ enter this area. It is restricted.
  4. You _______________ leave your phone in the locker, if you wish.
  5. You _______________ switch off the machine before you check it. It is necessary.
  6. You _______________ run near the workbenches.

Corrigé

  1. must / have to
  2. should (aussi must ou have to si l'on insiste sur l'obligation ; should est le plus juste au regard du texte)
  3. can't / mustn't
  4. can
  5. must / have to
  6. mustn't / can't

Activité 3. Explain a safety rule to a visitor (12 min). Expression orale en continu.

Pour les élèves

A visitor is in the workshop (the workshop in the painting, or your own workplace). You speak for about one minute (A2) or about two minutes (B1). Use this plan:

  1. Greet the visitor and introduce yourself.
  2. Welcome them to the workshop (or site, shop, kitchen).
  3. Explain two safety rules, with must / have to and mustn't or can't.
  4. Give one piece of advice with should.
  5. Say where they can leave a bag or a coat.
  6. (B1) Explain why one of these rules is important.

Prepare notes (key words only). Practise with your partner. Then some students will present to the class. The class listens as visitors and asks one question.

Corrigé

Exemple A2 :

Good morning. I'm Samir, a PFMP student. Welcome to the workshop. You must wear safety shoes here. You mustn't run, because there are workbenches and cardboard boxes. You should ask the tutor if you are not sure. You can leave your bag in the locker, over there.

Exemple B1 :

Good morning. My name is Inès, I'm a PFMP student from the lycée. Welcome to our workshop. For your safety, you have to wear safety shoes, and you must wear gloves if you handle materials. You can't leave your bag on the workbench, and you mustn't block the area in front of the windows or the doors. You should stay with me or with the tutor when you walk around. These rules are important because people are working, and a box or a tool on the floor can be dangerous. You can leave your coat in the locker. Have a safe visit.

Trace écrite et travail à la maison (6 min).

Pour les élèves. Trace pour le cahier

Safety at work

  • You must wear safety shoes. / You have to switch off the machine.
  • You should ask your tutor if you are not sure.
  • You can leave your bag in the locker.
  • You can't leave it on the workbench.
  • You mustn't run in the workshop.
  • If you see a problem, tell your tutor immediately.

Pour les élèves

Homework, Lesson 5

  1. Rewrite each idea with must, have to, should, can, can't or mustn't. a. It is necessary to wear gloves. (must) b. It is a good idea to check the order twice. (should) c. It is not permitted to use a damaged box. (mustn't) d. It is permitted to ask a colleague for help. (can) e. It is necessary to tell the tutor about a problem immediately. (have to)
  2. Write a short safety notice (A2: 6 lines, B1: 10 lines) for your PFMP workplace. Use at least four different modals.
  3. Prepare your one-minute oral from Activity 3. You will reuse it in the final task.

Corrigé

  1. a. You must wear gloves. b. You should check the order twice. c. You mustn't use a damaged box. d. You can ask a colleague for help. e. You have to tell the tutor about a problem immediately.
  2. Exemple (atelier) : You must wear safety shoes and gloves. You have to switch off the machine before you check it. You should keep the workbench clear. You can leave your bag in the locker. You can't leave boxes in the passageway. You mustn't run. If you see a problem, tell your tutor immediately.
