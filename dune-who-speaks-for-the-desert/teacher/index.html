Teacher's guide

Fiche professeur, one to two pages. Print this opening, then keep the listening texts, the quotation checks and the grids that follow (and the folds on each lesson page).

Before the first lesson

  • One copy of Dune (1965, first novel only) for the teacher. Students do not need the whole book. Give a two-line summary when a chapter is set and a student has no copy (the summary is in a teacher fold on Lesson 2).
  • Check every short quotation (Q1 to Q9) in your edition. Wording that may differ is flagged in the teacher folds, not on the student pages. Points to check: Q4 (exact Missionaria Protectiva wording), Q8 (two attested formulations), Q9 (a shorter form exists), the spelling of Lisan al-Gaib, and the continuation of the litany (Q3).
  • Print the blank spiral sheet (in the downloads) , one per student, for the whole sequence.
  • The official axis titles used on the presentation page are those of the programme for Première, BO n° 22 du 29 mai 2025. The full descriptive paragraphs of the annexe are not quoted here; they should be read in the published text if you wish to cite them.

ProblématiqueWhen a desert, a resource and a prophecy make a hero, is the hero the voice of a people or the instrument of those who wrote his legend, and who pays the price?
LevelPremière technologique, lycée général et technologique, LVA and LVB, B1-B2

MethodLa spirale de Gorse (the name honours the grandmother of Madame Carole Paoli Pompougnac's husband), in five steps: Je remarque, Je repère, Je comprends, J'anticipe, Je trouve le sens.
LengthEight lessons of 55 minutes, timed on 52, each with a stopping point and an 8-minute emergency exit.
Final taskThe Council of the Deep Desert: a briefing (3 to 4 minutes) and a written statement (180 to 250 words; B1, 150 to 180).
Fiche de lectureMadame Carole Paoli Pompougnac's book-study worksheet will be associated with this sequence, in its latest version, in pdf, docx and odt, once the model has been received.

Each lesson, in practice

  • At least two illustrations, placed where they serve the work of the day. Captions and licences are on the page and on the sources page.
  • One teacher-read listening text (natural speed, twice; three times in assessments). The text sits in a teacher fold, so that students do not read it during the listening.
  • One short quotation, or a scene summary when the exact wording must come from your edition (Q4).
  • One spiral turn. Display the navy diagram. Students keep one sheet for the whole sequence.
  • A written trace, a speaking turn for every student, and homework.
  • B1 and B2 work on the same supports. The dys version can be used alone, and after an absence.

Films, and a line that is not in the novel

  • Denis Villeneuve's Dune (2021) and Dune: Part Two (2024) may be suggested as optional homework only, for context. No clip is used in class, and no still or poster is shown. After a viewing, a possible question: what does the film keep, and what does it lose, of the epigraphs and of Kynes's warning?
  • "The spice must flow" is a line from the 1984 film, not a quotation from the novel. Do not attribute it to the book, and do not write it on the board as Herbert's wording.

Biography, and what not to assert

The Florence dunes project and the 1965 publication are widely documented and may be told to the class (Lesson 1 listening). Do not tell the class that the novel appeared "six years later" or after "several refusals from publishers" until those details have been checked in Brian Herbert, Dreamer of Dune (2003), listed in the bibliography for the teacher only.

The mid-sequence listening paraphrases a widely reported idea (Herbert wanted to show the danger of charismatic leaders). It does not quote an interview. If you wish to quote him, check a source first.

Orwell, and Fictions et réalités

Three optional bridges, one short stage each, are offered in Lessons 4, 7 and 8: two ways of controlling language; the leader as a manufactured figure; the resource and the story about it. They remain secondary. This is a study of Dune.

The English sequence Big Brother Is Watching · When Fiction Warns Reality is designed for Terminale, under the Terminale axis Fictions et réalités, which remains in the programme. This Première sequence prepares students for that later work; it does not recall a course they have already taken. Do not file this sequence as if Fictions et réalités had disappeared.

Barjavel, for a later French sequence

Three bridges (technical dependence; the guide's cost; the land that remakes its inhabitants) are listed on Lesson 8. They will be taken up in Lettres, on René Barjavel's Ravage (1943). They are not developed here.

Assessments

  • Diagnostic, Lesson 1, not graded: a positioning at A2, B1 or B2 for each skill, with a self-correction ("What I understood / What I missed / My improved answer").
  • Mid-sequence, Lesson 5, out of 20 (CO 5, CE 5, EE 5, EO 5). Oral talks continue in Lessons 6 and 7. Grids and a key are on the Lesson 5 page.
  • Summative, Lesson 8, out of 20 (EOC 6, EOI 4, EE 6, language 4). Grids, models and common errors are on the Lesson 8 page.

Filled spiral, on the death of Liet-Kynes (Book II)

A model for the teacher, and for a class correction after Lesson 6 or 7. Outer side: the idea. Inner side: the reference. The question at the tip is not given in advance. Expected kind of tip: "A people can be saved by the story that enslaves it."

TurnOuter side: the ideaInner side: the reference
Je remarqueA man is dying of thirst; the scene feels like a lesson, not an action scene.Book II, the chapter in which the Harkonnens leave Kynes in the desert without a stillsuit.
Je repèreTwo voices: the dying son and the remembered father. The father's sentence is in direct speech."No more terrible disaster could befall your people than for them to fall into the hands of a Hero."
Je comprendsThe scientist understands too late that a saviour can destroy the project he served.The same chapter. Compare Book I, the Missionaria Protectiva.
J'anticipeKynes asked the Fremen to protect Paul. The warning predicts the danger of Paul's success.Book III, final chapter: "The power to destroy a thing is the absolute control over it." (Check the wording in your edition.)
Je trouve le sensThe hero is the disaster. Freedom given by a saviour is a new dependence.Appendix I, The Ecology of Dune: "the highest function of ecology is the understanding of consequences."

Time-savers

Every lesson has a stopping point and an 8-minute emergency exit, printed at the head of the lesson page. Keys are in "Key" folds on the site, and on the correction sheet in the downloads (pdf, docx and odt).

Copyright

The novel remains protected (Frank Herbert died in 1986). Only short quotations are used, with precise book and chapter references. No chapter is reproduced. No publisher cover, and no film poster or still, is hosted.
