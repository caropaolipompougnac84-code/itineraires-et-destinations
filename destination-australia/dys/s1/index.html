Lesson 1. Your project and Coming South

How can a young French citizen prepare for life and work in Australia?

Road map

Lesson of 55 minutes, timed for 52 minutes. 8 lessons.

  1. Welcome and the key question 5 min
  2. Describe Coming South and compare then and now 15 min
  3. The project, the final task and the five-cell grid 12 min
  4. Speaking: hopes and worries for a journey today 12 min
  5. Written record, exit ticket and homework 8 min
  • Written record. The key question, a short then-and-now comparison, and the final task in one sentence.
  • Participation. Everyone speaks at least once: one hope or one worry about travelling to Australia today, or one difference with the 1886 voyage.
  • Homework for next lesson. Write 8 to 10 lines: what you already know about Australia, and three questions you want this sequence to answer.

Stopping point. End of this lesson.

Emergency exit · 8 min. If time runs short at this stopping point, condense the end of the lesson: dictate a short written record (two sentences); a quick round so that everyone speaks at least once (one sentence each); write the homework on the board.

Start · marked /3
  1. Where is Australia, in your own words?
  2. Name one reason why a young person might work there for a year.
  3. What document do you need before you get on a plane?

Answer key

  1. Accept a clear location (an island continent in the Southern Hemisphere; cities such as Sydney or Melbourne).
  2. Accept: work, travel, English, a working holiday, meeting people.
  3. A valid passport (and, for this project, a visa grant).
  • Describe the passengers, their belongings and the ship.
  • What hopes or worries might they have?
  • Compare their journey with a young traveller’s journey today (time, cost, documents, reasons for leaving).

Answer key · model points

The passengers travel by sea, with luggage, for weeks or months. They may hope for work or a new start, and worry about illness, money or not knowing the country. Today a flight takes about a day, but a young traveller still needs a passport, a visa, money and a plan. The painting is not a picture of present-day visa procedures.

Your project. You are a young French citizen planning a working holiday in Australia. You will prepare a realistic travel plan, organise your first week, apply for a job and discuss everyday life with new people.

Final task. A job interview for a Melbourne café: experience, availability, visa status, hourly pay, weekend rates and shifts.

Understand Practical information and a job advert.
Listen Key information at Sydney Airport.
Speak Plans and precise questions.
Write A resume and a short application.
Act Responsible choices about work and travel.
Exit ticket
  1. Write the key question of the sequence.
  2. Give one difference between the 1886 voyage and a journey today.

Homework

Write 8 to 10 lines: what you already know about Australia, and three questions you want this sequence to answer. Start a pack of bristol flashcards with these words: passport, visa, working holiday.
