Lesson 2. Skyfall: listening to a hero under pressure

Students can identify a trailer’s situation and main conflict; retrieve selected spoken information; distinguish hearing, seeing and inferring; and give an organised oral account.

Road map

Lesson of 55 minutes, timed for 52 minutes. Six lessons of 55 minutes, each timed for 52 minutes. The remaining three minutes allow for arrival, equipment and departure. Lesson 6 is the final-task interview.

  1. Retrieve Lesson 1 vocabulary 0 to 5
  2. Pre-listening: predict and learn essential words 5 to 12
  3. First listening: overall situation 12 to 16
  4. Second listening: complete the listening grid 16 to 23
  5. Third listening in short chunks; pair checking 23 to 29
  6. Grammar practice and correction 29 to 36
  7. Prepare and deliver oral restitution 36 to 44
  8. Each student gives one final evidence-based sentence 44 to 47
  9. Homework 47 to 48
  10. Written record 48 to 52
  • Written record. Complete the four-sentence written record on crisis, relationship, tension and inference.
  • Participation. Everyone speaks at least once: every student gives a 40-second account to a partner, then answers a follow-up.
  • Homework for next lesson. three cards (threat, return, although) and a six-sentence account of the extract.

Stopping point. Stopping point: minute 44.

Emergency exit · 8 min. If time runs short at this stopping point, this eight-minute ending replaces unfinished extension work inside the 52 minutes; it is not added to them.
  • 44 to 47: each student says the situation and one heard detail;
  • 47 to 48: homework;
  • 48 to 52: written record.

Listening document

SKYFALL, Official Trailer, Sony Pictures Entertainment, uploaded 31 July 2012, approximately 2 minutes 33 seconds. Use the version identified by YouTube video ID 6kw1UVovByw: official trailer on YouTube.

Teacher preparation. Preview the exact upload. Use the opening 75 to 90 seconds as the core extract. Stop after the exchange in which Bond explains his return, before the later extended action montage. Adjust the endpoint by the dialogue cue rather than assuming that all uploads have identical logos or timings. Check suitability for the class, including weapons and threat.

The official upload and film description can be verified, but this page does not supply a full transcript. The dialogue-answer points below should therefore be checked against the playable upload before teaching. No full transcript is reproduced, and no film still is hosted here.

The official film synopsis confirms that Bond is presumed dead, MI6 is attacked and Bond returns. These plot facts support the contextual correction, not a claim that every detail is audible in the selected extract.

While-listening worksheet

First listening: choose the best summary

  1. Bond is enjoying a routine day at work.
  2. Bond and MI6 face a serious crisis.
  3. Bond is attending an ordinary job interview.

Answer key

B.

Second listening: retrieve meaning

QuestionNotes, not full sentences
What does Bond say he has been doing?
How does M react to his unexpected presence?
What kind of emotional relationship do we notice?
What brings Bond back?
Which information is still unclear?

Teacher correction guide. Check these against the selected upload:

  • Bond refers to enjoying death.
  • M responds with dry humour rather than a warm greeting.
  • Their relationship combines familiarity, tension and professional authority.
  • Bond’s return is connected with the service being under attack.
  • The extract does not explain the entire plot or fully identify the threat.

For the emotional relationship and missing information, accept other well-supported answers. Do not require students to reproduce the film’s exact wording.

Third listening: confidence check

Mark each answer:

  • H: I heard it.
  • V: I saw it.
  • I: I inferred it.

This separates listening comprehension from visual interpretation.

Building your vocabulary: grammar practice

Exercise 1. Present continuous

Use the verbs in brackets.

  1. Bond to M. (speak)
  2. The characters a crisis. (discuss)
  3. The trailer tension. (create)

Answer key

  1. is speaking
  2. are discussing
  3. is creating

Exercise 2. From scene description to plot account

Rewrite in the past simple.

  1. Bond returns.
  2. M reacts.
  3. The service faces a threat.

Answer key

  1. Bond returned.
  2. M reacted.
  3. The service faced a threat.

Present forms describe the film as a work or an image on screen. Past forms can retell events. Choose one viewpoint and remain consistent.

Exercise 3. Link the ideas

  1. Bond returns the service is in danger.
  2. M is under pressure, she uses humour.
  3. The trailer gives clues. , it does not explain everything.

Answer key

  1. because
  2. Although
  3. However

Oral restitution

Every student gives a 40-second account to a partner.

Required structure:

  1. identify the document;
  2. explain the situation;
  3. report two details;
  4. interpret one effect;
  5. state one uncertainty.

This is an extract from the official Skyfall trailer. It presents Bond’s return during a crisis. His conversation with M suggests that they know each other well, but there is tension between them. The trailer makes us curious because it gives clues without explaining the whole threat.

Partner follow-up: which part did you hear, and which part did you infer?

  • □ I gave a complete spoken answer.
  • □ I listened to my partner.
  • □ I asked or answered a question.

Homework

  1. Make three cards: threat, return, although.
  2. Write a six-sentence account of the extract.
  3. Underline one present continuous form and circle one linking word.

No home video access is required. Students can use their corrected listening grid.
