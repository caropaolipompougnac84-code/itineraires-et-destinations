Lesson 3. If you own the wall, do you own the art?

The question today. Can someone own the wall without owning every right in the painting?

Three questions, not one

  1. The wall. Who owns the physical wall, or the piece of wall that was cut out?
  2. The image. Who has rights in the artistic image (copyright)?
  3. Public access. Who can still see the work?

Worked example. A café owner cuts a mural out of a wall and sells it. The owner may then hold the piece of wall. That does not automatically settle copyright. Passers-by may also lose public access.

A court case, in short

In Folkestone, in 2014, a mural called Art Buff appeared on a building. The tenant had a section of wall cut out, without the landlord's permission. In 2015, the High Court said the section of wall should be returned. The case was about the physical object. It did not say that the public owns every Banksy.

Exercise 1. True or false?

  1. The tenant was the freehold owner of the building.
  2. The court case was about a physical section of wall.
  3. The court decided that the public owns every Banksy mural.

Corrigé

1 False. 2 True. 3 False.

Two photographs

No Ball Games, in Tottenham, was later removed and sold. The Irpin gymnast was cut out of a building in 2023, in order to save it, and later moved.

Grammar: the passive

We use was / were + past participle when the object is the focus: The mural was painted in 2009. It was removed later. It was sold in sections.

Worked example. Active: Someone removed the mural. Passive: The mural was removed.

Exercise 2. Rewrite in the passive. 1. Someone painted the mural on a wall. 2. Someone cut the gymnast out of the building.

Corrigé

1 The mural was painted on a wall. 2 The gymnast was cut out of the building.

Speak (mid-point practice)

A café owner wants to cut out a mural and sell it. Residents want it to stay. Use this frame, for 40 seconds:

The owner wants... The residents want... The wall is not the same as the image. I think it should / should not... because...

Homework

Make two flashcards: was removed and copyright. Learn them.
