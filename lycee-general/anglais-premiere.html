Première

English in Première, general and technological pathway

The sequences for the year, with the programme axis when it is given. Reference: ministerial order of 5 May 2025 (official bulletin no. 22, 29 May 2025).

Six axes. In the general pathway, five of the six axes are studied in the year, and axis 6 ‘The American English-speaking areas’ is required. In the technological pathway, at least three axes. LVA B1+, LVB B1.
