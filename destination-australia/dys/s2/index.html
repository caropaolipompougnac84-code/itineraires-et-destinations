Lesson 2. Before departure

What must be ready before you book, and what is only a classroom estimate?

Road map

Lesson of 55 minutes, timed for 52 minutes. 8 lessons.

  1. Recap (marked /3) 5 min
  2. Passport and the Working Holiday visa, subclass 417 14 min
  3. Funds, flight and private insurance 15 min
  4. Budget table and the planning task 10 min
  5. Written record, exit ticket and homework 8 min
  • Written record. A departure checklist (passport, visa, funds, flight, insurance) and the difference between available savings and a visa fee.
  • Participation. Everyone speaks at least once: one item from the checklist, or one sentence explaining why the AUD 5,000 is not a visa charge.
  • Homework for next lesson. Estimate your own visa and flight subtotal with a source for the flight price, and list three costs still missing. Check the live visa fee on the official Home Affairs page (give the link, do not invent a price).

Stopping point. End of this lesson.

Emergency exit · 8 min. If time runs short at this stopping point, condense the end of the lesson: dictate a short written record (two sentences); a quick round so that everyone speaks at least once (one sentence each); write the homework on the board.

Recap · marked /3
  1. What is the final task of this sequence?
  2. Give one difference between the 1886 voyage and a journey today.
  3. Name one document you need before you fly.

Answer key

  1. A job interview for a Melbourne café (experience, visa, pay and shifts).
  2. Accept: a flight instead of a long sea voyage; official visas; faster travel; still the need for money and a plan.
  3. A valid passport (and a visa grant for this project).

A valid passport

Check your passport details and expiry date. Use the same name on your visa application and your flight booking. Keep secure copies of essential documents.

Apply for the Working Holiday visa, subclass 417

French passport holders can generally apply from age 18 to 35 inclusive. Apply for a first visa from outside Australia and wait for the grant before making commitments you cannot change. A first visa normally allows a stay of up to twelve months. Read the individual conditions on your grant notice.

Authentic document, not reproduced. Australian Government, Department of Home Affairs, Working Holiday Maker program and first Working Holiday visa (subclass 417). No imitation visa or application form is used here.

Plan your funds

Home Affairs normally asks for enough money to support your initial stay, usually about AUD 5,000, plus a ticket out of Australia or enough money to buy one. This is available savings, not a payment to the government. Keep evidence available if requested. Recheck the official page before you apply.

Choose your flight

Compare the total cost, baggage allowance and change conditions. A one-way ticket does not remove the need for funds to leave Australia.

Arrange private health and travel insurance

For this project, suitable private health and travel insurance is a required item in your departure checklist. Compare cover from a travel insurer or an appropriate overseas visitor policy, for example from Medibank or Bupa. Check exclusions, regional work, hospital treatment, repatriation and the whole length of your stay.

French visitors do not qualify for Medicare simply because they hold a WHV. France has no reciprocal health care agreement with Australia. Check your individual eligibility. Insurance is legally required if condition 8501 appears on your visa; it is not automatically a universal condition of every subclass 417 grant.

Your task. Estimate a visa and flight subtotal using the budget range and a flight costing AUD 1,200. Explain why savings are not a visa charge. List three costs still missing from your plan.

Answer key

Planning subtotal: about AUD 1,850 to 2,050. Confirm the actual visa fee before using your budget. The AUD 5,000 is available money for living costs, not a payment to Home Affairs. Possible extra costs: insurance, hostel accommodation, food, onward travel, transport and document costs.

Exit ticket
  1. Name the five items of the departure checklist.
  2. Why is the AUD 5,000 not a visa fee?

Answer key

  1. Passport, visa, available funds, flight, private insurance.
  2. It is money you must have available for living costs (and a way to leave), not a payment to the government.

Homework

Estimate your own visa and flight subtotal. Check the live visa fee on the official Home Affairs pricing page and give the link. List three costs still missing. Add flashcards: subclass 417, available savings, condition 8501.
