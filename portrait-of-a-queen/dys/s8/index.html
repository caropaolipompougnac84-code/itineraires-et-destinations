Lesson 8. “Sleep, Dearie Sleep”: three actresses, one life

The Crown, Season 6, Episode 10: the last scene, and the end of a reign.

Key question: How do we tell the end of a life that was entirely public, and how do we separate fiction from history?

1The teacher’s briefing8 min

For the teacher

Taking stock of a life and looking ahead requires every future form, including those seen from the past: will, be going to, the present continuous with future meaning, be about to, be to, the future continuous, the future perfect. Start from the question the series gives the Queen: Will I hand over to Charles? Then stand in 1953: By 2022 she will have reigned for seventy years.

Starting point

In 1953 nobody could say: "By 2022 she will have reigned for seventy years."

Future forms

  • will: prediction, decision now: The crown will pass to Charles.
  • be going to: intention, evidence: She is going to carry on.
  • Present continuous: arrangement: The family is meeting at Balmoral next week.
  • be about to: very near future: The reign is about to end.
  • be to: official plan, destiny: The funeral is to take place at Westminster Abbey.

Future continuous and future perfect

  • Future continuous: in progress at a future time: This time next year, historians will be writing about her reign.
  • Future perfect simple: completed before a future time: By September 2022 she will have outlived her husband.
  • Future perfect continuous: duration up to a future time: By then she will have been reigning for 70 years.

Second conditional and wish + past

If she abdicated, Charles would become King. I wish I had more time.

Help: future perfect = will have + past participle: by then it will have happened.

A six-minute official look back at the series across eight years.

The last scene. In the final episode, written by Peter Morgan and directed by Stephen Daldry, the Queen (Imelda Staunton) imagines her own funeral in St George's Chapel and is joined by her younger selves, Claire Foy and Olivia Colman, who argue for and against handing over to Charles. According to Variety, the chapel scenes were filmed in York Minster, and all three actresses were physically present. She finally walks alone towards the light.

The real end. Elizabeth II died at Balmoral on 8 September 2022, aged 96, after 70 years and 214 days on the throne, the longest reign in British history. Two days earlier she had appointed her 15th prime minister. In April 2020, during the first lockdown, she had told the nation: "We will meet again."

Fact-check each statement. Say verified or false, and give the source.

Understanding the document

  1. What dilemma does the final episode dramatise?
  2. Why are three actresses in the same scene?
  3. What does "the woman I put aside" mean?
  4. Where was the scene filmed?
  5. When and where did the real Queen die?
Answers
  1. Whether the Queen should abdicate and hand over to Charles or carry on to the end.
  2. They embody three ages of the same woman, her inner debate, and the history of the series itself.
  3. Her private self, sacrificed to her public role.
  4. In York Minster, standing in for St George's Chapel, Windsor.
  5. At Balmoral, on 8 September 2022.

Exercise 2. Rumour or verified fact?

Fact-check. Give your verdict and the source.

  1. Netflix describes The Crown as a "fictional dramatisation".
  2. In 1982 the intruder Michael Fagan and the Queen had a long conversation on her bed, as in Season 4, Episode 5.
  3. Churchill's 80th-birthday portrait by Sutherland was really destroyed.
  4. At his investiture, Charles secretly gave a defiant speech in Welsh written against the government's wishes.
  5. In 1947 the future Queen needed clothing ration coupons for her wedding dress.
  6. Philip refused to kneel to the Queen at the coronation.
Answers
  1. Verified. Since October 2022 Netflix's description reads: "Inspired by real events, this fictional dramatisation tells the story of Queen Elizabeth II...", added after criticism from Judi Dench and John Major (BBC News).
  2. False, according to Fagan himself: "She went past me and ran out of the room" (2012 interview; see The Telegraph, 2020). The long conversation is fiction.
  3. Verified. Lady Churchill had it destroyed; it became known after her death in 1977.
  4. False. He studied Welsh at Aberystwyth and spoke in Welsh, but the secret defiant speech is invented by the series (see WalesOnline).
  5. Verified. On 22 October 1947 the House of Commons was told that one hundred coupons had been issued to her for the wedding (Hansard).
  6. False as history. The series shows him resisting, but on 2 June 1953 he paid homage to her, kneeling, as the first layman to do so.

4Grammar in practice

From recognition to open production.

RecognitionExercise 3. Which future?

Name the form and its meaning.

  1. The funeral is to take place on Monday.
  2. Look at those clouds: it's going to rain.
  3. By then she will have reigned for 70 years.
  4. This time tomorrow we will be watching the procession.
  5. I'm meeting the curator at ten.
Answers
  1. be to: official plan
  2. be going to: evidence
  3. future perfect: completed before a future time
  4. future continuous: in progress
  5. present continuous: arrangement

Controlled practiceExercise 4. Future perfect or future continuous?

Put the verb in the correct form.

  1. By 2015 she (overtake) ______ Queen Victoria's record.
  2. In 2052 people (still / talk) ______ about her reign.
  3. By the end of the series, three actresses (play) ______ her.
  4. By 2022 she (reign) ______ for 70 years.
Answers
  1. will have overtaken
  2. will still be talking
  3. will have played
  4. will have been reigning / will have reigned

TransformationExercise 5. From 1953 into the future

Rewrite as a prediction made in 1953.

  1. She reigned until 2022.
  2. She saw 15 prime ministers.
  3. She became the longest-reigning British monarch.
  4. If she abdicated, Charles would become King. (second conditional, opinion)
Answers
  1. In 1953 nobody knew she would reign until 2022.
  2. By 2022 she will have seen 15 prime ministers.
  3. She was to become the longest-reigning British monarch.
  4. If she abdicated, Charles would become King.

6Idioms

to pass the torch
to hand over a role to the next generation.
From the torch relays of ancient Greek races.
In 2022 the torch was passed to Charles.
the end of an era
the end of a long, important period.
Common in journalism.
Her death marked the end of an era.
heavy is the head that wears the crown
great responsibility brings great worry.
Adapted from Shakespeare, Henry IV, Part 2: "Uneasy lies the head that wears a crown."
Heavy is the head that wears the crown: she never stopped working.
duty calls
an obligation must be fulfilled.
Used humorously when leaving to work.
"Duty calls," she said, and went back to her red boxes.
