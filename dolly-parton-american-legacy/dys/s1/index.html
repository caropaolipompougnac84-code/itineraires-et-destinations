Lesson 1. An icon in colour

What I need to say. I describe the Warhol portrait of Dolly.

Rule.
- Always true → present simple: She has green eyes.
- Now, in the picture → present continuous: She is smiling.
- Impression → She looks like a film star.

Word bank: portrait · platinum blonde (very light blonde) · lilac (light purple) · scarlet (bright red) · turquoise (blue-green) · background (behind) · icon (a very famous person)

Exercise 1. Match the detail and the colour.
Example: eyes → green ✔
1. hair 2. lips 3. earrings → scarlet · turquoise · platinum blonde

Exercise 2. Complete with the present continuous.
Example: She (look) at us. → She is looking at us. ✔
1. She (smile). 2. She (wear) earrings.

Exercise 3. Complete with looks or looks like.
Example: She ... happy. → She looks happy. ✔
1. She ... a doll. 2. She ... glamorous.

Homework. Record yourself: three sentences about a famous person.

Answers. Ex 1: hair → platinum blonde; lips → scarlet; earrings → turquoise. Ex 2: She is smiling. She is wearing earrings. Ex 3: looks like; looks.
