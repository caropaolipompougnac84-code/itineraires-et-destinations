Read and look · step one

Read, one sentence per line

  1. Election Day is the first Tuesday after the first Monday in November.
  2. On that day, citizens vote for a president in their state.
  3. But the president is not elected directly: the votes are counted state by state, and each state has a number of electors.
  4. A state has as many electors as members of Congress: its representatives plus its two senators.
  5. Washington, D.C. has three.
  6. In total there are 538 electors.

Source: USAGov, Electoral College

Grammar · step one

Grammar on the board

To explain who chooses the president, we need the passive with by: The president is elected by the Electoral College. The votes are counted by Congress.

Listen to your teacher, then copy the board.

On the board

The passive voice (4): by + agent; prepositions by and with

Rule
by = who does the action.
The president is elected by the Electoral College.
Form
The president is elected by the Electoral College. The votes are counted by Congress. The bill was signed by the president. No agent: Senators are elected for six years.
Examples
  • The president is elected by the Electoral College.
  • The electoral votes are counted by Congress in early January.
Watch out
  • The president is elected from the electors. → by the electors.
  • The ballots are counted by someone. → The ballots are counted (no useful agent).
French and English
par → by (agent); avec → with (tool). Le président est élu par les grands électeurs = The president is elected by the electors.

Quick check

  1. The president is elected ______ the Electoral College.
  2. The ballot is marked ______ a pen.
  3. Keep by? Senators are elected for six years by people.
Answer key
  1. by
  2. with
  3. No: Senators are elected for six years.

Grammar · step three

Figure of speech

Figures of speech: metonymy

Learn

Metonymy uses a place or an object to speak about the people linked to it. The White House announced means the president and his team. Capitol Hill means Congress. Washington often means the federal government.

  1. The White House said no. Who said no?
  2. Capitol Hill passed the law. Who passed it?
  3. Washington and Paris disagree. Who disagrees?
Answer key
  1. The president and his team.
  2. Congress.
  3. The American and the French governments.
