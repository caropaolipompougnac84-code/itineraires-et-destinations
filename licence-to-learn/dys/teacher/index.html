Teacher notes

This adapted pathway follows the same six lessons as the standard sequence. It includes its own short texts and corrected exercises. It is usable alone without an AESH.

Route letters on this official site are S (SEGPA), C (CAP) and P (Bac pro). In the source brief, bac pro was labelled Route B; the content is the same. CFA is a training setting, not a CEFR level.

Show the full lesson on the page. Do not hide answer keys behind tabs or accordions. The layout choices in the brief (generous spacing, left alignment, a reading guide) remain useful in class. They are not a promise that one layout will suit every student.

Lesson 2 uses the same official Sony Pictures Skyfall trailer as the standard lesson (YouTube video ID 6kw1UVovByw). A catch-up text is provided if the video is unavailable. Work done from that text should be labelled a reading-based oral account, not a completed listening assessment. No film still and no full transcript are reproduced.

Teacher update rule for lesson 1: date the news box on the day it is used. If an official actor announcement appears, replace the uncertainty paragraph. Do not use betting odds as evidence. Facts on this site were checked on 11 November 2026: at that date, no new Bond actor had been officially announced.

Standard lesson teacher notes (trailer extract, correction guide, speaking guarantee) remain on the standard pages, which stay visible to teachers.

Official programmes are summarised on the standard hub: 2025 English programme (BO n° 22 of 29 May 2025) for lycée général; 2019 programme (BO spécial n° 5 of 11 April 2019) for bac pro and CAP.
