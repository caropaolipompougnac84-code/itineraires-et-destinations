Lesson 6. Aunt Dolly: passing the torch to Miley

Debatable question: What can you really pass on to someone who is not your child?
By the end of this lesson, I can: tell the story of a bond between two people, report what they said, compare two voices.

For the teacher: timing (52 minutes)
Step Time Activity
1 0-4 Recap and flashcard duel; correction of the mid-point assessment (self-correction to finish at home)
2 4-6 The question of the day
3 6-19 Document 1 (reading) + two clips: Jolene (1:32) and Rainbowland (2:00)
4 19-25 Expanding your vocabulary: heritage
5 25-31 The teacher’s point: reported speech
6 31-39 Exercises
7 39-47 Speaking task: “The best advice I’ve received”
8 47-50 Written record
9 50-52 Homework

Stop point (minute 47). Every student has reported one piece of advice.

Emergency exit (8 minutes). Play only Jolene (0:00 to 1:32) with the gap task; then each student reports Dolly’s 2009 sentence in reported speech.

Step 1. Recap

  1. When did Dollywood open? 2. Complete: The park has existed ... 1986. 3. Two jobs at Dollywood.

    Answer key

    Answer key: 1 On 3 May 1986. · 2 since · 3 lifeguard, ride operator, chef.

Step 2. The question of the day

Ask: Who is your godmother, godfather, tutor or mentor? What did this person give you that money cannot buy?

Transition. Dolly had no children, but she had a famous goddaughter. What did she pass on?

Step 3. Documents

Document 1. A godmother (reading)

Miley Cyrus was born in November 1992. She is the daughter of the country singer Billy Ray Cyrus, who worked with Dolly. In a 2009 interview with Robin Roberts on ABC’s Good Morning America, Dolly explained:

“I’m her honorary godmother. I’ve known her since she was a baby. Her father’s a friend of mine, and when she was born, he said, ‘You just have to be her godmother,’ and I said, ‘I accept.’”
(Dolly Parton, Good Morning America, ABC, 2009)

In the Disney series Hannah Montana, Dolly played “Aunt Dolly”, Hannah’s godmother, in several episodes between 2006 and 2010 (dollyparton.com). In 2024, the genealogy company Ancestry found that they were also distant relatives: seventh cousins once removed (CNN, People).

Shared songs (timeline)
- 2010: Jolene together for the 25th anniversary of Dollywood.
- 2012: Miley sings Jolene in her Backyard Sessions.
- 2016: Jolene on the TV show The Voice.
- 2017: Rainbowland, written and sung together, on Miley’s album Younger Now.
- 10 February 2019: Jolene together at the 61st Grammy Awards, during a tribute to Dolly.
- 2023: Wrecking Ball, Miley’s hit, sung together on Dolly’s album Rockstar.

Reading task. Who asked Dolly to be the godmother? What does “honorary” mean here? Which song comes back most often in the timeline?

Answer key

Answer key: Billy Ray Cyrus · it is not an official religious role, it is a title of honour and affection · Jolene.

Document 2. Advice and tribute

“You do you, I’ll do me, and together we’ll be us.”
(Dolly’s advice, as reported by Miley Cyrus, Billboard, 2025)

After Dolly’s death, Miley wrote on X (26 August 2026):

“The feeling of losing my Aunt Dolly is beyond what feels right to share. Our most sacred moments were private [...] What I KNOW is Dolly would want me to feel it, write a song about it, and then continue on and be strong.”
(quoted by Lead Stories, 9 September 2026)

Reading task. What does “You do you” mean?

Answer key

Answer key: be yourself, follow your own way, do what is right for you.

Step 4. Expanding your vocabulary: heritage

Word Meaning Example
a godmother / a goddaughter a woman chosen to support a child / the child Dolly was Miley’s godmother.
honorary given as an honour, not official She was her honorary godmother.
a role model a person you admire and want to copy Dolly was Miley’s role model.
a bond a strong link between people They had a real bond.
a mentor an experienced person who guides you A tutor can be a mentor.
an heir a person who receives property when someone dies Who is Dolly’s heir?
a legacy what a person leaves after their life Her songs are her legacy.
to look up to to admire Miley looked up to Dolly.
to take someone under your wing to protect and guide someone Dolly took Miley under her wing.
to pass on / to hand down to give to the next generation She passed on her songs.
a cover (version) a song sung by another artist Miley’s Jolene is a cover.

Words to report and comment: according to... · she claimed that... · she admitted that... · she explained that... · in other words,...

Exercises on vocabulary

Exercise F1. Match. 1 role model · 2 heir · 3 bond · 4 cover / a strong link · b a person you admire · c a song sung by another artist · d a person who inherits

Answer key

Answer key: 1b, 2d, 3a, 4c.

Exercise F2. Fill in the gaps: looked up to · passed on · under her wing · legacy
Miley (1) __ Dolly. Dolly took her (2) _. She (3) songs and advice. Today, this is part of her (4) ___.

Answer key

Answer key: 1 looked up to · 2 under her wing · 3 passed on · 4 legacy.

Flashcards

Front Back
to look up to someone (blue) admirer · Miley looked up to Dolly.
She said (that) + past (red) “I accept.” → She said that she accepted.
in other words (green) autrement dit · You do you: in other words, be yourself.

Step 5. The teacher’s point

Teacher script. Read Dolly’s 2009 quotation aloud. Ask: What did Billy Ray say? What did Dolly answer? Write both sentences in reported speech; underline the tense change.

Point Form Example
Tense change present simple → past simple; present continuous → past continuous; will → would “I am proud.” → She said (that) she was proud.
Pronouns and time words my → her; now → then; today → that day “my goddaughter” → her goddaughter
say / tell say something; tell somebody something She said that... / She told her that...
Orders and advice tell / advise somebody to + verb She told her to be herself.
Questions ask + if / wh-word + normal word order He asked if she would be her godmother.

Quick check. Report: “I am so proud of her.” → She said (that) she was so proud of her.

Step 6. Exercises

Exercise 1. Who said it? Dolly, Miley or Billy Ray?
1. “You just have to be her godmother.” 2. “I accept.” 3. “You do you, I’ll do me, and together we’ll be us.” (who reported it to the press?)

Answer key

Answer key: 1 Billy Ray Cyrus · 2 Dolly · 3 the advice came from Dolly; Miley reported it.

Exercise 2. Timeline. 2006 · 2010 · 2017 · 2023 / a Rainbowland · b Aunt Dolly in Hannah Montana · c Wrecking Ball on Rockstar · d Jolene at Dollywood

Answer key

Answer key: 2006 b · 2010 d · 2017 a · 2023 c.

Exercise 3. Report the words.
1. Dolly: “I am her honorary godmother.” → Dolly said that... 2. Dolly to Miley: “Be yourself.” → Dolly told Miley... 3. Billy Ray: “Will you be her godmother?” → Billy Ray asked Dolly if... 4. Miley: “I will always love her.” → Miley said that...

Answer key

Answer key: 1 she was her honorary godmother · 2 to be herself · 3 she would be her godmother · 4 she would always love her.

Exercise 4. say or tell? 1 Dolly ... Miley to be herself. 2 Miley ... that she was heartbroken. 3 Dolly ... the journalist that she had known Miley since she was a baby.

Answer key

Answer key: 1 told · 2 said · 3 told.

Exercise 5. Report the best advice you have received from an adult, a tutor or a boss (five sentences).
Model answer: My tutor told me to arrive ten minutes early. She said that customers always remembered a smile. She advised me to ask questions. She asked me if I liked the job. She told me not to give up.

Help box. Frames: He/She told me to... · He/She said that... · He/She asked me if...
Going further. Use reporting verbs: advise, encourage, warn, admit, insist. Dolly encouraged Miley to follow her own path. Write an 80-word article: “Dolly and Miley, a family bond”.

Step 7. Speaking task: “The best advice I’ve received”

  1. Pairs (4 min). Student A tells a piece of advice in direct speech; student B reports it to a third student in reported speech. Swap.
  2. Round table (4 min). Each student answers the question of the day in one sentence: What can you pass on to someone who is not your child? Start with I think you can pass on... and give one example from Dolly and Miley.

Possible answer: I think you can pass on songs, values and confidence, but not your talent. Dolly told Miley to be herself, and Miley sang Dolly’s songs in her own way.

Step 8. Written record

Lesson 6. Aunt Dolly
Dolly was Miley Cyrus’s honorary godmother. They sang Jolene together many times and wrote Rainbowland (2017). Dolly passed on songs and advice: “You do you, I’ll do me, and together we’ll be us.”
Grammar. Reported speech: the tense goes back (am → was, will → would). say something / tell somebody. tell somebody to + verb. ask if...
My answer: You can pass on..., but not...

Step 9. Homework

  1. Finish the self-correction of the mid-point assessment (improved version of part C).
  2. Make five flashcards from this lesson.
  3. Report in five sentences a piece of advice given by an adult or a tutor.
  4. Help box: record your five sentences.
