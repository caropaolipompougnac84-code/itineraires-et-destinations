Assessment

Classroom assessments aligned with the sequence objectives. These are not official examination papers.

Road map

Lesson of 55 minutes, timed for 52 minutes. 8 lessons, plus this assessment page.

  1. Instructions and a one-sentence recap of the Big Four (everyone speaks once) 5 min
  2. Evaluation 1 · reading a job advert 24 min
  3. Evaluation 2 · listening and vocabulary, or briefing for the oral 23 min
  • Written record. The completed scripts (or the oral notes if the class is doing the interview).
  • Participation. Everyone speaks at least once: one item of the Big Four, named in the opening recap, so that everyone has spoken at least once before writing.
  • Homework for next lesson. None, unless the teacher asks you to finish at home. Rehearse the interview if it takes place in a later lesson.

Stopping point. End of this assessment lesson. The oral interview may take place in a later lesson.

Emergency exit · 8 min. If time runs short, finish Evaluation 1 and the first five gaps of Evaluation 2, or the interview notes if the class is preparing the oral. Hand in what you have. Everyone has already spoken once in the opening recap.

Evaluation 1 · Reading a job advert · 10 points

Classroom job advert · fictional employer and conditions for the exercise. This is not a real vacancy or a universal wage schedule.

The Daily Grind Café · Casual café all-rounder / barista

Location: Flinders Lane, Melbourne CBD.

Pay for this calculation exercise: AUD 36 per hour on a weekday and AUD 48 per hour on Sunday, before tax. Actual lawful rates must be checked against the applicable award and classification.

The role: Join our team for busy brekkie shifts. Take orders, make flat whites and manage the till. We also need help this arvo.

Requirements: Valid work rights, suitable availability and a current Victorian RSA for shifts involving alcohol service. Early shifts start at 6 am. Bring your resume, not private tax or passport details.

Apply: Ask for the manager, Jake, in person between 2 pm and 4 pm.

  1. Give the street or area and city. (1 point)
  2. State the weekday and Sunday hourly earnings in this advert. (2 points)
  3. Identify two required administrative conditions or certifications. (2 points)
  4. Find the words for breakfast, this afternoon and cash register. (3 points)
  5. Explain how to apply and whom to ask for. (2 points)

Answer key

  1. Flinders Lane, Melbourne CBD. (1)
  2. AUD 36 on a weekday; AUD 48 on Sunday. (1 each)
  3. Valid work rights and a relevant Victorian RSA. (1 each)
  4. Brekkie, arvo, till. (1 each)
  5. Apply in person between 2 pm and 4 pm; ask for Jake. (1 for the method and time, 1 for the manager)

Evaluation 2 · Listening and vocabulary · 10 points

Listen to your teacher or use the audio. Complete one word in each gap. One point per correct word.

_____ (1) everyone! Welcome to Cairns. First, put on your _____ (2) and some sunscreen. For a familiar quick meal, some people go to _____ (3). You can also organise a _____ (4) with your _____ (5) in a park. Before travelling through the _____ (6), check your route and supplies. For a second working holiday _____ (7), check the specified-work rules: three months, normally at least 88 calendar days of eligible full-time-equivalent work during your first _____ (8). _____ (9) _____ (10): ask for help when you need it!

Teacher script and answers

G’day everyone! Welcome to Cairns. First, put on your sunnies and some sunscreen. For a familiar quick meal, some people go to Macca’s. You can also organise a barbie with your mates in a park. Before travelling through the Outback, check your route and supplies. For a second working holiday visa, check the specified-work rules: three months, normally at least 88 calendar days of eligible full-time-equivalent work during your first year. No worries: ask for help when you need it!

Answers: 1 G’day; 2 sunnies; 3 Macca’s; 4 barbie; 5 mates; 6 Outback; 7 visa; 8 year; 9 No; 10 worries.

Oral assessment grid · 20 points

Final task: a job interview for a Melbourne café. Award up to 5 points per criterion. Use intermediate scores where appropriate; zero is possible if there is no assessable evidence. Clarity and interaction matter more than copying an accent.

Criterion5 points3 points1 point
Task completionClear and realistic discussion of experience, work rights, availability, pay and budget.Main information included, with some missing details.Very limited information or task not followed.
Workplace vocabularyAccurate professional vocabulary and suitable everyday expressions.Understandable vocabulary, with some repetition or errors.Very limited vocabulary makes the message difficult to follow.
IntelligibilityClear speech, effective stress and understandable pronunciation.Mostly understandable, with occasional repetition needed.Frequent difficulty understanding the message.
InteractionResponds naturally, asks questions and clarifies meaning.Maintains the exchange with some pauses or help.Reads a script or rarely responds to the partner.

Self-check

  • I can explain my travel plan.
  • I can identify the Big Four.
  • I can ask about hourly pay.
  • I can distinguish specified work from any farm job.
  • I can understand a short workplace exchange.
