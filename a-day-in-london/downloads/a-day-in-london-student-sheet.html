A Day in London

Student sheet · A1 towards B1 · 9 lessons of 55 minutes, timed for 52 minutes · Leo and Amy in London.

Use this sheet in class or at home. Play the missions in the game, then write here. Make your own bristol flashcards: the sequence page lists the 127 words and gives a blank template. This sheet is not a ready-made deck.
