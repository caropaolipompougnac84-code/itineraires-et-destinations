Grammar: emphatic inversion

The rule

Start with Never, Rarely or Not only.

Then: auxiliary + subject + verb.

Like a question.

NormalWith inversion
I have never seen such a crowd.Never have I seen such a crowd.
New York rarely sees such unity.Rarely does New York see such unity.

My bristol flashcards · how to use them

Principle: each student makes their own cards by hand, on bristol cards. Writing the word is already a way of learning it. The pack stays in their bag: they can revise anywhere, with no screen.

Materials

A7 bristol cards (74 × 105 mm), or A6 bristols cut in half. Coloured felt-tip pens. An elastic band or an envelope for the pack. A small cardboard box with three compartments for practice (a shoe box with two dividers is enough). You can also print the blank template and cut it out.

Part 1: reading (10 points)

A text written for this class. It is not a real essay.

  • For 364 days a year, New York is a city of business and speed.
  • But on Marathon Sunday, the streets change.
  • More than a million people cheer the runners.
  • Rich and poor stand side by side.
  • Never before have they been so close.
  • But the next day, everything is normal again.
  1. What is New York like on a normal day? (2 pts)
  2. What changes on Marathon Sunday? (3 pts)
  3. Does the unity last? Justify. (2 pts)
  4. Copy the sentence with an inversion. (3 pts)

Expected answers

  1. A city of business and speed.
  2. The streets change; more than a million people cheer; rich and poor stand side by side.
  3. No: the next day, everything is normal again.
  4. Never before have they been so close.

Facts and figures

Every figure comes from official or recognised sources, checked on 4 November 2026 (Sources section). The 2026 figures (61,529 starters, 61,146 finishers) are those of the NYRR press release of 2 November 2026, « as of 1 November, subject to final verification ». The spectator figure kept is that of the World Marathon Majors: more than a million.

The graph in your model (a drop in pace on the Queensboro Bridge) is not based on any verifiable public data: it is replaced by a real graph of finishers (1970, 2017, 2024, 2025, 2026). The real fact that is kept: spectators are not allowed on the bridges of the course.

The assessment text is presented to students as a text written for the class, not as a genuine essay; the expression « collective emotional refueling » is given as an image, not as a term from psychology. « Millions of spectators » has become « More than a million spectators », a checked figure.
