Name, describe, share

Speak for one minute on a trade and share the words of your trades.

Short quizzes to learn the everyday English words of a trade: the watering can, the wheelbarrow, the whisk, the adjustable spanner. One page for each course, each with its own trade. Open to all colleagues.

In each quiz: from French to English, listen and choose (British English), match, and complete a sentence at work. A score at the end, a button to start again, a dys version, a word list and a sheet to print in PDF, docx and odt.
