Teacher notes

Access, timing, sources and care, so that the sequence can be taught as it stands on this official site.

Level and programme

Première générale, LVA, towards B1+. The BO also sets B1 in LVB at the end of Première. Axes 6 and 2 are associated, as the official wording on the sequence homepage quotes in French. Grammar points follow the BO (modals and space in lesson 1; reported speech and was going to in lesson 2; past habits and the past perfect in lesson 3; causatives and complex comparison in lesson 4; obligation, permission and concession in lesson 5; wish, regret and the passive in lesson 6; the gerund as subject and phrasal verbs in lesson 7).

What was recut

The source dossier ran lesson 1 well over 52 minutes (two paintings, a long interpretation, a speaking turn and a 12-minute paragraph). The official lesson is recut: the paragraph is finished for homework. The two paintings are now on the lesson page, reproduced with permission.

Lesson 8 in the source gave 30 minutes to the hearing and 20 minutes to the open letter. In a large class, a three-minute presentation per group plus questions may need to be organised separately. The official road map keeps a shorter hearing inside 52 minutes and finishes the letter for homework. The assessment grids still cover both tasks.

Paintings in lesson 1

Norval Morrisseau, Loon and Fish, © Norval Morrisseau Estate, and Jim Logan, No One to Play with but his Dog, © Jim Logan, are reproduced on the lesson 1 page with the permission of the estate and of the artist. A short quotation by Jim Logan, published by the Canada Council Art Bank in July 2024, is kept with its source. Links: officialmorrisseau.com and jim-logan.net.

Videos

Eleven YouTube links, verified in the teaching dossier on 4 November 2026, are offered as optional documents. Core questions are answerable from the text. In lesson 5, only the extract from 0:00 to 3:10 of the Power & Politics report is proposed. In lesson 7, the Telefilm interview raises difficult topics (deaths in the community) between 0:35 and 1:28 and between 3:50 and 4:46: preview it, and use the advised extracts (0:15 to 0:35, 1:28 to 2:15, 4:47 to 5:12) in class. The film study of The Grizzlies is not yet on this official site; lesson 7 is written to stand alone.

Figures and a licence note

Drinking-water advisories: Indigenous Services Canada, as of 21 October 2026 (158 lifted since November 2015, 39 still in effect, 37 communities). Trans Mountain: the September 2026 offer of a 15 per cent stake was in negotiation at the date of the dossier. Recheck both before class.
