Lesson 3. Your first week in Sydney

What do you do in the first days so that you can live and work legally?

Road map

Lesson of 55 minutes, timed for 52 minutes. 8 lessons.

  1. Recap (marked /3) 5 min
  2. Arrival and biosecurity, then a first hostel 12 min
  3. The Big Four: SIM, bank account, TFN, insurance 18 min
  4. Your checklist and two questions 10 min
  5. Written record, exit ticket and homework 7 min
  • Written record. The Big Four (SIM or eSIM, bank account, TFN, private insurance) and one arrival rule: if you are unsure, ask an officer.
  • Participation. Everyone speaks at least once: one item of the Big Four and why it matters, or one question to ask a bank or a mobile provider.
  • Homework for next lesson. Complete your Big Four checklist with one action for each item. Start the official TFN page (give the link); do not pay an intermediary.

Stopping point. End of this lesson.

Emergency exit · 8 min. If time runs short at this stopping point, condense the end of the lesson: dictate a short written record (two sentences); a quick round so that everyone speaks at least once (one sentence each); write the homework on the board.

Recap · marked /3
  1. Name the five departure items from lesson 2.
  2. Why is the AUD 5,000 not a visa fee?
  3. At what ages can a French passport holder generally apply for a first subclass 417 visa?

Answer key

  1. Passport, visa, available funds, flight, private insurance.
  2. It is available money for living costs (and a way to leave), not a payment to Home Affairs.
  3. From 18 to 35 inclusive, on the official rules consulted for this sequence; always recheck the official page.

Arrival

Follow airport signs. Keep your passport and visa grant information available. Read the arrival and biosecurity instructions carefully. Declare items when required; if unsure, ask an officer.

A backpacker hostel

Book a short initial stay, for example one or two weeks. Check reception hours, storage, transport links and the total price. A hostel can give you time to look for longer-term accommodation.

Your Big Four checklist

  1. A local SIM or eSIM. Compare coverage for your actual destinations, data, identification requirements and price.
  2. An everyday bank account. Ask about identity checks, account fees and access to your money.
  3. A Tax File Number. Apply through the ATO once you are in Australia and have a visa with work rights.
  4. Private insurance. Check that your cover has started and save your policy and emergency contact details.

Get connected

Open a bank account

Apply for your TFN

The ATO application is available at no cost. Use the official page rather than a paid intermediary. You must already be in Australia for the online temporary-visitor application. Keep the application receipt securely.

Authentic document. Australian Taxation Office: TFN application for permanent migrants and temporary visitors.

If you have applied, tell your employer: the ATO normally allows 28 days to provide your TFN. A 45% withholding rate can apply if you do not provide it and no relevant exception applies. Do not put a TFN on a public resume or send it to an unverified recruiter.

Your task. Put the Big Four in your own checklist. Write one question to ask a bank and one question to ask a mobile provider.

Answer key

SIM or eSIM, bank account, TFN and private insurance. Examples: “What account fees will I pay?” and “Does this plan cover the places where I will work?”

Exit ticket
  1. List the Big Four.
  2. What should you do if you are unsure about an item in your luggage?

Answer key

  1. SIM or eSIM, bank account, TFN, private insurance.
  2. Ask an officer before leaving the controlled area; declare items when required.

Homework

Complete your Big Four checklist with one action for each item. Open the official TFN page and write the link in your notebook. Do not pay an intermediary. Add flashcards: TFN, eSIM, account fees.
