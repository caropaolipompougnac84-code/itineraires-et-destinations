Grammar · step one

Grammar on the board

To explain how the system works, we need the passive; to give possible results, we need the conditional: The president is elected by 538 electors. If nobody gets 270 votes, the House will choose the president.

Listen to your teacher, then copy the board.

On the board

Review: passive and conditionals; question word order; linking words for a presentation

Rule
Passive: is elected, was signed. Real: If..., will... Imaginary: If... (past), would...
Form
The president is elected by 538 electors. Lincoln was chosen in 1860. If nobody gets 270, the House will choose. If I were American, I would vote.
Examples
  • How is the president elected? He or she is elected by the Electoral College.
  • Who chooses the president? The electors do.
Watch out
  • How the president is elected? → How is the president elected?
  • Who does choose the president? → Who chooses the president? (subject question)
French and English
French can keep statement order in questions: Le président est élu comment ? English never does: How is the president elected?

Quick check

  1. Question: the president / elect / how?
  2. Subject question: Who ______ (choose) the president?
  3. Passive past: The bill ______ (sign) last week.
Answer key
  1. How is the president elected?
  2. chooses
  3. was signed

Stage 2. Transform

Rewrite as asked.

  1. Congress makes the laws. (passive)
  2. The electors chose the president in December. (passive)
  3. She wins Georgia. She gets sixteen votes. (if, real)
  4. I am not American. I can't vote. (if, imaginary)
  5. The House impeaches; the Senate holds the trial. (passive)
Answer key
  1. The laws are made by Congress.
  2. The president was chosen by the electors in December.
  3. If she wins Georgia, she will get sixteen votes.
  4. If I were American, I could vote.
  5. A president is impeached by the House and tried by the Senate.

Model answer

Hello, today I am going to explain American elections. First of all, the president is elected by 538 electors. Then, the laws are made by Congress. If you want to understand the midterms, look at the seat bars: if a party has 218 seats, it controls the House. That's a good question: the results are counted state by state. To sum up, it is a complex but logical system.
