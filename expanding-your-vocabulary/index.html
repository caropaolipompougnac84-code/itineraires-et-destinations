Expanding your vocabulary

Level
Première and Terminale · general pathway: tronc commun B1+ towards B2 in Première, B2 in Terminale, spécialité LLCER towards C1 by the end of Terminale · technological pathway: B1 towards B2
Aims
Build a word bank for each of the eight axes of the programme, then use five methods well: collocations, register, word formation, linking words and metaphorical language, with ways to remember words for good.
Length
Two lessons of 55 minutes, timed for 52 minutes, in each track. A marked recap, exercises with answer keys, a game, a short marked assessment, homework, and self-made bristol flashcards.

Each track opens with an introduction card and a marked recap, offers progressive exercises with answer keys, a game, a paragraph and a one-minute oral to reuse the words, a short marked assessment, and homework at the end of every lesson. Answers stay hidden until you open them.

Help in class

  • Can you repeat, please?
  • Can you speak more slowly?
  • What’s … in French?
  • How do you say … in English?
  • I need help.

Road map

Lesson of 55 minutes, timed for 52 minutes. 2 lessons in each track.

  1. General track · B2 towards C1 2 lessons

    Six words or phrases per axis, collocations, formal register and the passive, prefixes and suffixes, linking words for the essay, metaphors.

  2. Technological track · B1 towards B2 2 lessons

    Four words per axis, common collocations, polite English, word building, simple linking words.

The timed steps, the stopping point, the emergency exit and the three end outcomes (written record, participation, homework) are on each track page.
