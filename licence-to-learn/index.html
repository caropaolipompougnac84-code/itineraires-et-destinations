Licence to Learn

MI5, MI6 and the world of James Bond. Big question: are real spies like James Bond?

Road map

Six lessons of 55 minutes, each timed for 52 minutes. Lesson 6 is the final-task interview.

  1. Lesson 1. Who will be the next 007? 52 min
  2. Lesson 2. Bond, James Bond 52 min
  3. Lesson 3. MI5 or MI6? 52 min
  4. Lesson 4. Fiction vs reality 52 min
  5. Lesson 5. The rules of a spy 52 min
  6. Lesson 6. The job interview 52 min
  • Written record. Each lesson ends with a short written record, copied in the notebook.
  • Participation. Everyone speaks at least once in every lesson.
  • Homework for next lesson. Each lesson page states the homework; lesson 5 prepares the interview, and lesson 6 is the final task.

Stopping point. Each lesson page marks a stopping point, so that the end of the lesson can be condensed if need be.

Emergency exit · 8 min. If time runs short, a condensed eight-minute ending (inside the 52 minutes, not 52 plus 8) still leaves every student with the written record, one spoken sentence, and the homework.

The assessment page has the oral grid for the interview and the written marking grid. Lesson 6 is timed for 52 minutes; there is no extra written test to invent on top of the Spy Code and the interview.

Official programme, lycée général. Langues vivantes: arrêté of 5 May 2025, BO n° 22 of 29 May 2025 (NOR MENE2504621A), annexe 4. In force for Seconde from the 2025 to 2026 school year, and for Première and Terminale from the 2026 to 2027 school year. The closest cultural frames in Seconde are axe 5, « Créer et recréer » (the Bond films, the trailer, the debate about the next actor), and axe 1, « Représentation de soi et rapport à autrui » (the job interview and the public image of a spy). This is not Seconde axe 6, which is the Commonwealth. The closest frame in Première is axe 3, « Art et pouvoir ». This is not Première axe 6, « Les aires anglophones américaines ».

Official programme, lycée professionnel. The vocational English programme is still BO spécial n° 5 of 11 April 2019. The final-task interview is written so that it can also serve a professional oral.

Official wording from the Bulletin officiel stays in French, clearly labelled, as above. The language work stays at B1.

For the teacher. The earlier online edition was labelled “seconde ou première bac pro, B1”, and it used the 2019 cycle-terminal axes « espace privé et espace public » and « pouvoir et contre-pouvoirs ». In the catalogue of this site the sequence is listed under lycée général / lycée professionnel. This official edition follows the catalogue: Seconde or Première, B1, général and professionnel, and it updates the lycée général framing to the 2025 programmes. If the stream should be narrower than that, that is a catalogue decision.

The earlier edition counted five sessions, but the tab “Who will be the next 007?” is already a full lesson (news, biographies, the simple past, an argued vote). The official road map therefore has six timed lessons. Facts about the next Bond actor were checked on 11 November 2026 against the BBC (9 November 2026): no official announcement had been made at that date. The three names most often cited as frontrunners remain Jack Lowden, Callum Turner and Josh O’Connor; other names also circulate in the press. The classroom vote can stay; the news box should be updated if an actor is officially named.

A listening in Racing to the Skies, lesson 8 mentions MI6 in the Mitrokhin Archive. The documents and the task are not the same, and that page is left unchanged. Josh O’Connor’s biography names his role in The Crown; no still from the series is reproduced here.

Your mission

Big question: Are real spies like James Bond?

Final task: you will play a job interview to join MI5 or MI6.

Grammar: the simple past, then must, have to, mustn’t and don’t have to.
