Appendix B. Couture pathway: the dresser's card

For: students of the CAP Métiers de la mode, vêtement flou, the bac pro Métiers de la couture et de la confection, and any bac pro class working on the world of shows.
How to use it: after Lessons 2 (the Dolly look, Coat of Many Colors) and 3 (backstage). One lesson of 55 minutes (planned over 52).
Debatable question: Is a stage costume a piece of art or a technical tool?

For the teacher: timing (52 minutes)
Step Time Activity
1 0-4 Recap
2 4-11 Expanding your vocabulary
3 11-17 The teacher’s point: present passive for a technical description
4 17-25 Exercises
5 25-40 The dresser scene (every student plays both roles)
6 40-50 Technical sheet + patchwork presentation
7 50-52 Homework

Stop point (minute 40). Every student has played the dresser.
Emergency exit (8 minutes). Each student describes one costume in three passive sentences, aloud.

Step 1. Recap

  1. What did Dolly’s mother use to make the coat? 2. Three words for bling. 3. Make it polite: Make it shorter.

    Answer key

    Answer key: 1 scraps of fabric (patchwork) · 2 rhinestones, sequins, beads · 3 Could you make it a bit shorter, please?

Step 2. Expanding your vocabulary

Word Meaning Example
a fitting trying on a garment to adjust it The fitting is at six.
a hem / a seam the folded bottom edge / the line where two pieces are sewn Let’s shorten the hem.
to take in / to let out to make tighter / to make wider Can you take it in at the waist?
a zip / Velcro / snaps fastenings A zip is faster for a quick change.
rhinestones / sequins / beads shiny decorations sewn or glued on The suit is covered in rhinestones.
fringe hanging threads or strips as decoration a white fringe jacket
patchwork fabric made of small pieces sewn together The coat of many colours was patchwork.
a quick change a very fast change of costume during a show She has a quick change after song three.
a rack / a steamer a rail for clothes / a machine that removes creases Put the costumes on the rack.
lining fabric inside a garment The lining is in satin.

Exercise 1. Match. 1 hem · 2 lining · 3 to take in · 4 steamer / a fabric inside · b remove creases · c bottom edge · d make tighter
Answer key: 1c, 2a, 3d, 4b.

Exercise 2. Fill in the gaps: zip · fitting · rack · rhinestones
At the (1) __, Dolly tries the pink suit. It is covered in (2) _. For the quick change, we use a (3) . After the show, the costumes go back on the (4) ___.

Answer key

Answer key: 1 fitting · 2 rhinestones · 3 zip · 4 rack.

Flashcards (model). to take in · reprendre (resserrer) · Can you take it in at the waist? / is made of + material (red) · The jacket is made of satin.

Step 3. The teacher’s point: the present passive

Point Example
be + past participle The jacket is made of satin. The rhinestones are sewn by hand. The dress is lined with silk.
by + agent (if useful) The costume was designed by a stage designer.
Adjective order a long sparkly pink satin gown

Quick check. Make it passive: We sew the sequins by hand. → The sequins are sewn by hand.

Step 4. Exercises

Exercise 3. Transform into the passive. 1 We close the jacket with a hidden zip. 2 We decorate the sleeves with fringe. 3 They line the trousers with silk.

Answer key

Answer key: 1 The jacket is closed with a hidden zip. 2 The sleeves are decorated with fringe. 3 The trousers are lined with silk.

Exercise 4. Adjective order. 1 a (satin / pink / beautiful) jacket 2 (long / white / silk) fringe

Answer key

Answer key: 1 a beautiful pink satin jacket · 2 long white silk fringe.

Step 5. The dresser scene

Use Scene 1 of Lesson 3 (role cards, starters, professional words and model dialogue). Add one constraint: the dresser must explain one alteration in the passive (The hem will be shortened by two centimetres.).

Step 6. Productions

Technical sheet (60 words). Garment, fabric, colour, decoration, fastening for the quick change. Three passive forms.
Model answer: Stage suit, pink. The jacket is made of satin and it is decorated with rhinestones on the shoulders. The trousers are lined. The jacket is closed with a hidden zip, so it can be removed in less than a minute during the quick change. White fringe is sewn on the sleeves.

Patchwork presentation (oral, 1 minute). Inspired by the coat of many colours, design a patchwork piece. Present it: colours, fabrics, meaning, and what you want to pass on.

Assessment grid (couture)

Criterion Not yet Nearly Reached Beyond
Interaction (dresser scene) I read my card short answers I answer and ask I suggest and negotiate
Technical description no structure list of words garment, fabric, decoration, fastening precise and justified
Professional vocabulary fewer than 3 3 to 5 6 to 9 10 or more
Language: vocabulary reused and syntax (passive, adjective order) many errors some errors mostly correct varied and correct

Self-correction grid (couture). Same lines; columns: What I did / What I missed / My improved version. Rewrite your technical sheet following the grid.

Homework

  1. Learn the vocabulary (flashcards).
  2. Describe in 60 words your favourite stage costume (any artist), with a link to an official source.
  3. Record your patchwork presentation.
