Your group reads one text only. Then you become the expert of your period.

Text 1 · The Musician and Model (1986 to 2004)

Robert Pattinson was born on 13 May 1986 in Barnes, a quiet part of London. He has two older sisters. When he was four, he started to play the piano and the guitar. At school, he was not a perfect student: he often forgot his homework, and a teacher advised him not to join the drama club.

At twelve, he started his first job: he worked as a model for British fashion brands and magazines, for about four years. As a teenager, he loved music. He sang his own songs at open mic nights in London pubs, sometimes under a stage name, Bobby Dupea.

He was shy, so his father encouraged him to join a local amateur theatre group, the Barnes Theatre Company, at thirteen. First, he worked backstage. Then he auditioned and got a small role with no lines. In the next play, he had the main role, and a talent agent in the audience noticed him.

Texte : Carole Paoli Pompougnac

Text 2 · The Teen Idol (2005 to 2012)

In 2005, Robert played Cedric Diggory in Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire. It was the most successful film of the year, and the newspaper The Times named him “British Star of Tomorrow”.

Three years later came his big breakthrough. About three thousand young men applied for the role of Edward Cullen, the vampire in Twilight, and the director chose Robert. For the role, he learned an American accent and wore contact lenses. He also sang two songs on the film's soundtrack.

Between 2008 and 2012, the five Twilight films made over 3.3 billion dollars around the world. Robert became a teen idol: thousands of fans screamed his name, and photographers waited outside his house. But some journalists said that he was typecast. For many people, he was only “the vampire”.

Texte : Carole Paoli Pompougnac

Text 3 · The Indie Risk-Taker (2012 to 2019)

After Twilight, Robert could choose easy, commercial films. Instead, he chose small independent films with demanding directors. In 2012, he starred in Cosmopolis, by the Canadian director David Cronenberg. The film competed at the Cannes Film Festival, and critics praised his performance.

Then he played very different characters: a naive young American in the Australian desert in The Rover (2014), and a British explorer in the Amazon rainforest in The Lost City of Z (2016). For that role, he grew a thick beard and lost about 16 kilos.

In Good Time (2017), he played a bank robber in New York, and a critic from the magazine Variety called it a “career peak”. In 2019, he played a lighthouse keeper in The Lighthouse. These films did not make billions, but they showed that he was bold and versatile.

Texte : Carole Paoli Pompougnac

Text 4 · The Dark Knight (2020 to today)

In 2020, Robert went back to big films: he played a spy in Tenet, by Christopher Nolan. Then came his biggest challenge: Batman. The director Matt Reeves wrote the character with Robert in mind, because he loved his performance in Good Time.

At first, some Batman fans did not like the choice: for them, the Twilight vampire could not become the Dark Knight. Robert did not give up. The Batman came out in 2022, and it was a critical and commercial success. Critics praised his performance.

Since then, he has starred in Mickey 17 (2025), by Bong Joon-ho, and in The Odyssey (2026), by Christopher Nolan. In November 2026, Amazon MGM Studios announced that he will be the next James Bond. His story shows that a label is not a life sentence.

Texte : Carole Paoli Pompougnac

Grammaire, pas à pas

Étape 1 · Les verbes irréguliers

Ils ont une forme propre au prétérit : become, became ; begin, began ; choose, chose ; sing, sang ; make, made ; grow, grew ; lose, lost.

Étape 2 · Raconter dans l'ordre

Les mots de liaison organisent la chronologie : first, then, after that, three years later, in 2012, finally.

Étape 3 · Comparer deux périodes

While / whereas opposent deux moments : In his first life he was shy, whereas in his third life he was bold.

Phonologie · Les trois sons de -ed

/ɪd/ après les sons t et d : started, wanted, decided. /t/ après les sons sourds p, k, f, s, sh, ch : worked, noticed, asked. /d/ dans tous les autres cas : played, joined, loved.

Figures de style

La métaphore filée
Tout le texte file l'image de plusieurs vies : four lives, a new chapter, a label is not a life sentence.
L'antithèse
Deux idées opposées côte à côte : From Vampire to Superhero, small films, big risks.

Useful expressions

  • Expliquer sa période: My period is… / In this part of his life, he… / The main challenge was…
  • Vérifier qu'on est compris: Is that clear? / Do you want me to repeat?
  • Demander: Can you say that again? / What does … mean?
