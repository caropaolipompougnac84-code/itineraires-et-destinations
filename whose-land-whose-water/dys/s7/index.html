Lesson 7. The Grizzlies

A film, a team, and hope in a small Arctic community.

Stopping point. Minute 44, after the speaking frame.

Emergency exit · 8 min. See the box at the end of the lesson.

Recap · 0 to 4 min

Give one figure about drinking water, with its date and source.

Self-check

For example: 158 long-term advisories have been lifted since November 2015 (Indigenous Services Canada, 21 October 2026).

4 to 8 min

Today’s goal

I can say what the film is about and why a team can become a family.

a communitythe people who live in one place

resiliencethe ability to recover after hard times

Route S · SEGPA

I can: say where the film is set, and what the team becomes.

The Grizzlies is a Canadian film. It is set in Kugluktuk, a small Inuit community in Nunavut, in the Arctic. A teacher from the south starts a lacrosse team with young people there. They say they made their own family. They do not give up. You do not need to watch the film: this short text is enough.

Route C · CAP

I can: compare the film with the lonely boy in one sentence.

The Grizzlies, directed by Miranda de Pencier, tells a true story from 1998. Life is hard for many teenagers in Kugluktuk. Playing lacrosse gives them a reason to stay in school. One character says: ‘Broken promises are dangerous here.’ Unlike the lonely boy in Jim Logan’s painting (lesson 1, not shown on this site), these young people stand up for each other. You do not need the film or an interview: the text is enough.

Route P · Bac pro

I can: explain why broken promises can be dangerous, in your own words.

The film is about hope and self-determination in a community that has lived with the legacy of colonialism. The teacher later said that the students changed him more than he changed them. Playing lacrosse, and not giving up, are at the centre of the story. A separate film study is not yet on this official site; this lesson stands alone. Difficult topics from a long interview are not used here.

Exercise 1

Where is the film set?

Worked example (already corrected). Worked answer: In Kugluktuk, Nunavut, in the Arctic.

Your turn. Who starts the lacrosse team?

Self-check

A teacher from the south (Russ Sheppard, in the true story).

Exercise 2

Complete: They made their own ________.

Worked example (already corrected). Worked answer: They made their own family.

Your turn. They do not ________. They ________ each other.

Self-check

They do not give up. They stand up for each other.

Exercise 3

Write one sentence with a gerund as the subject, or with a phrasal verb.

Worked example (already corrected). Worked answer: Playing lacrosse gave them a reason to stay in school.

Your turn. Write your sentence about the film.

Self-check

Possible sentence: They stood up for each other and did not give up.

CFA task · instead of Exercise 3

If you are in a training centre, do this task instead of Exercise 3. You may follow route C or route P for the reading.

Worked example (already corrected). A trainee writes a two-line review: ‘The Grizzlies is set in Kugluktuk. Playing as a team helped the young people not to give up.’

Your turn. Write a short review of four sentences: the place, the story, one strong idea, and your opinion. You do not need to have seen the film.

Self-check

Possible answer: The Grizzlies is set in Kugluktuk, Nunavut. A teacher starts a lacrosse team with Inuit teenagers. Playing together, they become a family and do not give up. I would watch it because it talks about hope and broken promises.

Speaking frame · 40 to 44 min

Say this aloud, and change the words in brackets if you need to:

The Grizzlies is set in [Kugluktuk], Nunavut. A lacrosse team becomes a [family]. They do not [give up].

Written record · 44 to 48 min

Route S. The film is set in Kugluktuk. A team becomes a family.

Route C. The Grizzlies, set in Kugluktuk, Nunavut, shows young people who stand up for each other. They do not give up.

Route P. Playing lacrosse gives hope in a hard place. Unlike the lonely boy in Jim Logan’s painting, the team becomes a family.

Progress record · 48 to 50 min

  • I read the short text for my route.
  • I did two exercises and checked the self-check key.
  • I said one sentence aloud.
  • I copied the written record.

Homework. Write six to eight sentences about the film, from this page only. Use one phrasal verb.

Emergency exit · 8 min

  • 0 to 2. Say this sentence: “In The Grizzlies, a team becomes a family.”
  • 2 to 5. Copy the written record for your route.
  • 5 to 7. Write down the homework.
  • 7 to 8. Check one self-check answer.
