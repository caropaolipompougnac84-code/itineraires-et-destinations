Grammar lesson: concession and polite disagreement

To take part in a hearing: give an opinion, react to others and disagree politely, we need concession and polite disagreement
For the teacher: how to run it (8 to 10 minutes)
  1. Ask: in a hearing, how can you disagree without being rude?
  2. Board: Admittedly, ..., but ...; Although + clause; That's a fair point; however, ...; With all due respect, I disagree. Students copy.
  3. Show that a concession first accepts part of the other view, then gives your point.
  4. Quick check, then exercises.
On the board, to copy

Rule

A concession admits a point before answering it: Admittedly, + fact, but + your point; Although + clause, main clause. To disagree politely: That's a fair point; however, ...; With all due respect, I disagree. Reuse the tools of the whole learning journey: comparatives, the passive, should have, third conditional.

Form

  • Admittedly, ..., but ...: Admittedly, Concorde was a technical triumph, but it never made money.
  • Although + clause: Although it was fast, it burned a lot of fuel.
  • That's a fair point; however, ...
  • With all due respect, I disagree.

Use

  • To take part in the Supersonic Hearing.
  • To answer another witness.
  • To write a balanced argument.

Examples from the lesson

  • “Admittedly, Concorde was a technical triumph, but it never made money.”
  • “That's a fair point; however, the climate matters too.”
  • “With all due respect, I disagree.”

Common mistakes

  • Not Although Concorde was fast, but it was expensive., but Although Concorde was fast, it was expensive.
  • Not With all respect, you are wrong., but With all due respect, I disagree.
  • Not However the climate matters., but However, the climate matters.

Figures of speech in the documents

Metaphor and rhetorical question

« Concorde's heir »; the hearing question: should public money go to the past or to the future?

Concorde's heir is a metaphor that treats a plane as a person with a family. In a hearing, a rhetorical question (Do we really want another race for prestige?) makes the committee think without waiting for an answer.

EntryWho or what could be Concorde's heir?

A future supersonic airliner, or a research plane such as NASA's X-59.

Show a model answer

Admittedly, Concorde proved that supersonic flight was possible: Mach 2, the Atlantic in about three and a half hours. That's a fair point about the noise; however, research aircraft such as the X-59 are designed to replace the boom with a quiet thump. With all due respect, the question is not whether we can fly faster, but whether we should. Had the governments of the 1960s asked this question, would the costs have tripled?

The Supersonic Hearing

Situation. 2026. A company asks for public money to build a new supersonic airliner, « Concorde's heir ». A committee of the British Parliament holds a public hearing before voting. In groups of four to six, you play the hearing.

Format. 7 to 8 minutes per group. 1. The chair opens the hearing. 2. Each witness gives a one-minute statement. 3. The chair and the witnesses ask questions, react and argue. 4. The chair sums up. The class votes.

Rules. Notes with key words only. Use at least two facts from the documentary and one from the reading text of session 6. Re-use comparatives, past modals and the third conditional.

The chair of the committee

You are neutral. You open the hearing, give the floor, ask follow-up questions (Could you be more specific? What do you mean by...?), make sure everyone speaks, and sum up.

The company's chief executive

You want the money. Concorde proved it was possible: delta wing, Mach 2, the Atlantic in about three and a half hours. New technology can make supersonic flight quieter. Jobs, prestige, competition.

An aviation historian

You remind the committee of the past: the costs more than tripled by 1967, national pride drove the race (Kennedy, Nixon, the Tu-144), and the dream « fell apart enormously quickly ». Lessons must be learnt.

An environmental scientist

Supersonic planes burn much more fuel per passenger; sonic booms; the climate emergency. You may mention NASA's quiet supersonic research aircraft, the X-59, but you question whether it should be a priority.

An air safety expert

You speak about safety and responsibility, with respect for the victims of the 2000 accident: the BEA investigation, the modifications made before 2001, the responsibility of engineers, airlines and governments.

A heritage curator from Scotland or Bristol

Concorde is a shared heritage: G-BOAA at the National Museum of Flight, East Fortune, in Scotland; G-BOAF at Aerospace Bristol; Delta Golf at Brooklands. Should public money go to the past (museums) or to the future?

Bac pro role: head of an aircraft maintenance company

You know what it costs to keep an aircraft flying: inspections, spare parts, documentation, trained technicians. British Airways retired Concorde partly because of rising maintenance costs. What would a new airliner need?

Useful language. I'd like to point out that... / Let me remind you that... / Admittedly..., but... / Whereas Concorde..., today... / That's a fair point; however... / With all due respect, I disagree... / Had we learnt from Concorde, we would not... / We should have...

Listening sheet for the audience

While a group plays, note: two arguments for, two arguments against, the best question, one sentence with a past modal you heard.

Possible answers

For: technological lead, jobs, the Concorde experience, quieter design. Against: cost to taxpayers, fuel and climate, noise, a service for a wealthy few, the lessons of the 1970s and of 2003.

C1: the student presents a clear, well-structured position, uses precise facts from at least two sessions, reacts to the others without notes, concedes and refutes, and uses a varied range (inversion, past modals, nuance).

Notes pour l'enseignante

Déroulé (55 min). 10 min préparation en groupes ; 35 min passage des groupes (7 à 8 min chacun) ; 10 min vote et bilan. Si la classe est nombreuse, faire passer une partie des groupes au début de la séance 8 ou enregistrer.

Rôles. Le rôle « résident de Gonesse » de la version initiale est remplacé par un expert en sécurité aérienne, pour traiter l'accident avec sobriété et respect. Le rôle de conservateur ou conservatrice de musée relie la tâche aux axes 2 et 6. Le rôle bac pro est facultatif pour les autres classes.
