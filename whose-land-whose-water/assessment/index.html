Assessment

Lesson 8, the final task: a citizens’ hearing in groups of four, then an open letter to a Member of Parliament.

Hearing procedure

Each group of four follows this order:

  1. Two minutes: silent preparation.
  2. Six minutes: four statements of up to 90 seconds.
  3. Four minutes: every student asks and answers one short question.
  4. Two minutes: agree on a recommendation and identify any remaining disagreement.

No student has to impersonate an Indigenous person or disclose a personal political position.

Dossier choices

A. The White Paper and Red Paper: a proposed policy and its rejection.

B. James Bay or one later dam: negotiated development, benefits and losses.

C. One pipeline: representation, consultation and disagreement.

D. Drinking water: a dated pledge, progress and an unmet deadline.

Do not force every dossier into an identical “promise broken” story. Where the evidence concerns a proposal or disputed commitment, say so.

Assessment grids

Oral, out of 20

CriterionPointsSuccessful performance
Evidence and attribution6Identifies the case, gives accurate facts and names sources.
Communication6Gives an understandable, organised contribution using practised language.
Interaction4Asks a relevant question and answers or requests clarification.
Qualification4Identifies a different viewpoint or limitation without distortion.

Written, out of 20

CriterionPointsSuccessful performance
Task and evidence6Explains the issue, includes sourced facts and makes a relevant request.
Language6Uses sufficiently clear sentences, appropriate vocabulary and practised structures.
Organisation and tone4Uses paragraphs and a respectful letter format.
Accuracy and qualification4Dates figures, avoids invented evidence and states a limitation.

For B1, accept connected simple sentences and brief reasons. For B1+, expect a more developed explanation and clearer qualification. Do not turn the rubric into a checklist requiring every advanced grammar structure.

Student self-check before handing in

  • □ I named the subject.
  • □ I used information from the lessons.
  • □ I named the source.
  • □ I dated any changing figure.
  • □ I made a clear request.
  • □ I did not invent evidence.
  • □ I said the message aloud.
