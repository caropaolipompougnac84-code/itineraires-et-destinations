Final Task and Assessment

Final task: your application file

You want to leave for Australia, to work or to study. You prepare a complete file, as if you were sending it to an Australian employer or school. Everything goes into one envelope (or one folder), in this order:

  1. A cover letter: who you are, what you want to do, and above all why Australia.
  2. Your resume (Australian style: no photo, two referees).
  3. All the forms filled in: Incoming Passenger Card, TFN declaration, new employee details or student information, rental application (Real-life Forms).
  4. Your visa checklist: which visa, the documents, the cost.
  5. Your budget, printed from the calculator.
  6. Your contact list: consulate, first hostel, community group, Fair Work.

Then you present your project in a phone interview with an Australian employer or school adviser.

Why Australia? The key paragraph

Australians are very proud of their country, its nature, its sports and its way of life. An employer wants to know that you chose Australia for real reasons, not only for the beaches. Show that you know the country and respect it.

Write your "Why Australia?" paragraph (★ 60 words, ★★ 120 words). Use at least one fact about history, one about society, and one personal reason.

Avoid: "Because it's always sunny." Prefer: "I want to work in a country that values hard work and outdoor life, and to learn from the oldest living culture in the world."

Assessment grid: the application file /20

CriterionPoints
The file is complete and in order (letter, resume, forms, checklist, budget, contacts)/4
Forms filled in correctly and consistently (same name, dates, visa)/4
Cover letter: format, clear purpose, motivation/4
"Why Australia?": precise knowledge of the country, respectful and personal/4
Language: grammar, vocabulary of work, money and travel/4

On peut faire réellement constituer l'enveloppe : documents imprimés, signés, classés. Le contrôle de cohérence (même nom, mêmes dates, même visa partout) est très formateur : c'est exactement ce que vérifient les services d'immigration et les employeurs.
