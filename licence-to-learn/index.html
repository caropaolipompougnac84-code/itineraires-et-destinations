Road map

Six lessons of 55 minutes, each timed for 52 minutes. The remaining three minutes allow for arrival, equipment and departure. The stopping point in every lesson is minute 44. If time runs short, an eight-minute emergency exit replaces unfinished extension work inside the 52 minutes; it is not added to them.

  1. Lesson 1. The new James Bond: a convincing choice?
  2. Lesson 2. Skyfall: listening to a hero under pressure
  3. Lesson 3. MI5 or MI6: understand before you compare
  4. Lesson 4. A good Bond, a good employee?
  5. Lesson 5. The responsible officer’s code
  6. Lesson 6. The interview: evidence, not just adjectives
  • Written record. Every lesson ends with a short written record that students keep.
  • Participation. Everyone speaks at least once, through protected paired turns, not only volunteers.
  • Homework for next lesson. Student-made flashcards and a short written task, set at the end of each lesson.

Stopping point. Minute 44 in every lesson.

Emergency exit · 8 min. Each lesson page gives a replacement ending that still leaves students with the written record, at least one spoken turn, and the homework.

Your mission

Central question: are real intelligence officers like James Bond?

Cultural question: how does a screen hero shape the image of British intelligence?

Final task: take part in a fictional recruitment interview for MI5 or MI6. Explain your choice, describe your skills, support one quality with an example, explain security rules and respond to an unexpected question.

This is a classroom simulation, not a real recruitment exercise. Students use invented identities and experiences if they prefer. They must not disclose private information.

Written production across the unit: a five-rule Spy Code with explanations, completed in lesson 5.

Level and official programmes

The official end-of-year targets in the 2025 English programme (arrêté of 5 May 2025, BO n° 22 of 29 May 2025, NOR MENE2504621A) are:

ClassLVALVB
SecondeB1+A2+
PremièreB1+B1
TerminaleB2B1

These are annual targets, not a requirement that every activity in every unit must already operate at the target level. A B1 consolidation sequence is legitimate, but “B1, Seconde or Première LVA” should not be presented as the complete official annual expectation. The plus sign indicates that the next level is reached in at least one language activity. It should not be treated as a simple synonym for “more difficult vocabulary”.

The programme applies to Seconde from 2025-2026 and to Première and Terminale from 2026-2027. It therefore applies to both classes at the date of this page.

Recommended label: Seconde or Première, LVA: B1 consolidation towards B1+. The teaching level of this unit is a B1 core, with B1+ development in reception, interaction and mediation.

For bac pro and CAP, the 2019 programme remains the reference (BO spécial n° 5 of 11 April 2019, arrêté of 3 April 2019). The final published programme states:

QualificationThreshold stated in the 2019 programmeWider objective distinguished by that programme
CAPA2 for the relevant language unitProgress beyond A2 where appropriate
Bac pro LVAB1+ at the end of the course and examination framework describedB2 as the wider end-of-secondary objective
Bac pro LVBA2+B1 as the wider objective

Consequently, B1 can be a suitable working level in Seconde or Première bac pro. The programme does not assign a universal B1 end-of-year requirement to each of those two classes. B1 should not be labelled the final bac pro LVA target. CAP requires a distinct A2 pathway, not simply the same B1 tasks with fewer questions. CFA identifies a training setting, not one language level. The qualification being prepared determines the relevant pathway. The 2019 programme explicitly includes apprentices among its learners.

Cultural axes

This sequence is presented here as a Première sequence. Official axis titles are those of the programme for the lycée général et technologique, BO n° 22 du 29 mai 2025, NOR MENE2504621A.

Première, lycée général et technologique. The sequence cites two axes only:

  • Axe 3 « Art et pouvoir », objet d'étude « Mise en scène et représentation du pouvoir politique dans le cinéma et les séries de fiction » : how cinema represents an institution, authority and national protection.
  • Axe 4 « Innovations scientifiques et responsabilité » : the intelligence services and the responsibilities attached to information and technology.

Suggested framing: What does James Bond tell us about power, institutions and responsibility in the United Kingdom?

The sequence can combine these two coherent axes. It should not claim to cover every axis touched by one isolated detail. In the general and technological pathway, the annual programme requires five axes, including Axis 6; this sequence does not replace that annual requirement. Axes of Seconde are not cited here.

Bac pro. Do not transfer the general and technological lycée axis system onto bac pro. The 2019 programme organises communication around everyday, personal, social and civic situations, and professional situations. This unit connects the two through British institutions and cultural representation; media literacy and stereotypes; teamwork and confidentiality; understanding a vacancy; presenting skills and experience; and participating in an interview. The spy setting remains a cultural simulation. For vocational transfer, students should also connect its language to their actual sector, for example: a receptionist must protect customer information; a mechanic has to check the instructions; a catering employee mustn’t ignore a safety rule. These are classroom transfer examples, not comprehensive sector-specific regulations. The word PFMP, when it appears, belongs only to the bac pro pathway, not to a CAP version.

Learning outcomes

By the end of the unit, students should be able to:

  • distinguish fiction, verified information, opinion and speculation;
  • explain the main difference between MI5 and MI6 without presenting it as an absolute geographical division;
  • identify how films construct an exciting image of intelligence work;
  • describe a past experience using the past simple;
  • compare fictional and institutional representations;
  • express obligation, prohibition and absence of obligation;
  • participate in an interview and ask for clarification;
  • revise independently using student-made flashcards.

Not every grammar point is a new lesson objective. Some are retrieval or extension. The compulsory core tasks fit the timings; optional challenges replace an easier task or become homework.

In real intelligence terminology, an officer works for a service, while an agent can be a person recruited to provide information. The everyday word “spy” is less precise. This distinction is introduced in lesson 3.

A speaking guarantee that works

Do not rely on volunteers or a whole-class round. In every lesson:

  1. Students prepare briefly.
  2. Partner A speaks.
  3. Partner B speaks.
  4. Each asks or answers one question.
  5. Both tick the participation box.

For a group of three, give each student a separate timed turn. A whole-class discussion is additional. It never replaces the protected paired turns. Reading a prepared sentence is an acceptable first contribution. It is not, by itself, evidence of B1 interaction.

Student participation box:

  • □ I gave a complete spoken answer.
  • □ I listened to my partner.
  • □ I asked or answered a question.

Comment travailler

  • Faire les 6 lessons dans l’ordre, une lesson par moment de travail de 30 à 40 minutes. Les jours de fatigue, faire seulement la version courte (15 minutes) : rien n’est perdu, la suite se rattrape au retour.
  • Ouvrir la page de la lesson (lien « Documents ») : textes, images, vidéos et enregistrements y sont en libre accès. Sans connexion, travailler avec la fiche imprimable (PDF, Word ou LibreOffice) proposée en téléchargement.
  • Chercher d’abord, seul et sans traducteur, puis cliquer sur « Afficher la réponse » pour se corriger, avec un stylo d’une autre couleur.
  • Production orale et interaction en travail seul : enregistrer un message vocal (téléphone ou ENT), ou écrire le texte qui serait dit, y compris les répliques des deux rôles.
  • Recopier la trace écrite de chaque lesson dans le cahier : elle sert pour les évaluations.
  • Les évaluations sont accessibles dans cette version : les faire au moment indiqué, sans aide, sans document sauf mention contraire, puis les envoyer.
  • Envoyer le travail par la messagerie de l’ENT (photo nette ou fichier) ou le rendre au retour. L’enseignante corrige et renvoie un commentaire.

Lesson 1 · The new James Bond: a convincing choice?

Is this choice convincing, and what kind of Bond could he create?

Durée : 35 min · Documents : Lesson 1

  1. CE Lire le Student document A. The official announcement et remplir la Worksheet 1A. News verification grid : confirmed, reported, opinion ou not established (10 min).
  2. CE Lire les trois profils du Student document B (Jack Lowden, Callum Turner, Josh O’Connor) et remplir la Worksheet 1B. Candidate grid (8 min).
  3. Étude de la langue Faire les exercices 1 à 4 : prétérit, prétérit ou present perfect, might / could, comparer avec prudence (8 min).
  4. PO Enregistrer l’intervention de 30 à 40 secondes : I find this choice… because… He could create a Bond who… (4 min).
  5. PE Devoir : 70 à 90 mots : ce choix est-il convaincant, et quel James Bond pourrait-il créer ? (une phrase au prétérit, une comparaison, une phrase avec might ou could), et trois flashcards : successor, announcement, convincing (5 min de plan).

Trace écrite à recopier dans le cahier

On 16 November 2026, Amazon MGM Studios announced that Robert Pattinson will be the new James Bond. Before this announcement, Lowden, Turner and O'Connor had been linked to the role in media reporting.

I find this choice … because … This actor might … His biography is studied in A Life in Roles.

Je vérifie

  1. Rewrite with might: Perhaps Pattinson will create a thoughtful Bond.

    Pattinson might create a thoughtful Bond.

  2. Choose: Turner played / has played a rower in The Boys in the Boat in 2023.

    played (finished past, with a date)

Version courte (15 min) : faire la Worksheet 1A et recopier la trace écrite.

À envoyer ou à rendre : les deux grilles, les exercices, l’enregistrement et le texte de 70 à 90 mots.

Lesson 2 · Skyfall: listening to a hero under pressure

What does the trailer tell us, and what does it hide?

Durée : 35 min · Documents : Lesson 2

  1. PE Remplir la colonne My prediction de la Prediction grid avant l’écoute (3 min).
  2. CO Regarder la bande-annonce officielle de Skyfall (Sony Pictures, 2 min 33, lien de la séance). Première écoute : choisir le meilleur résumé. Deuxième écoute : la grille retrieve meaning, en notes. Troisième écoute : marquer chaque réponse H (entendu), V (vu) ou I (déduit). Sans vidéo, travailler avec la grille corrigée (12 min).
  3. PE Compléter la Post-listening worksheet (5 min).
  4. Étude de la langue Faire les exercices 1 à 3 : présent continu, du présent au prétérit, because, although, however (8 min).
  5. PO Enregistrer la restitution de 40 secondes : identifier le document, expliquer la situation, dire ce qui reste incertain (4 min).

Trace écrite à recopier dans le cahier

The official trailer presents Bond's return during a crisis. M and Bond have a relationship marked by tension and trust.

The trailer creates tension through dialogue, images and music. We must distinguish what we hear from what we see or infer.

Je vérifie

  1. Choose the best summary: routine day, serious crisis, ordinary job interview?

    Bond and MI6 face a serious crisis.

  2. Rewrite in the past simple: Bond returns. M reacts.

    Bond returned. M reacted.

Version courte (15 min) : faire la première et la deuxième écoute, puis recopier la trace écrite.

À envoyer ou à rendre : les grilles d’écoute, la fiche d’après-écoute, l’enregistrement et le compte rendu de six phrases (devoir).

Lesson 3 · MI5 or MI6: understand before you compare

What is the difference between the two services?

Durée : 35 min · Documents : Lesson 3

  1. CE Lire les Student documents A (Two connected services) et B (Officer or agent?), puis remplir la Worksheet 3A. Institutional grid (10 min).
  2. CE Corriger les affirmations de la Worksheet 3B (5 min).
  3. Étude de la langue Faire les exercices 1 à 4 : présent simple, questions, whereas, définition avec who (10 min).
  4. PO PI En version seul : enregistrer l’explication de MI5 en trois phrases (rôle A), puis écrire la question et la correction du rôle B et les enregistrer aussi (5 min).
  5. PE Devoir : quatre questions et réponses pour un quiz, dont une qui commence par What does…? (5 min).

Trace écrite à recopier dans le cahier

MI5 protects UK national security. MI6 collects intelligence overseas. Their missions differ, but their work can cross borders.

An MI5 officer is an employee. An agent provides information but is not an MI5 employee.

Je vérifie

  1. Correct: “An MI5 agent is an employee.”

    An agent provides information but is not an MI5 employee; officers are employees.

  2. Join with whereas: MI5 focuses on UK national security. MI6 focuses on foreign intelligence.

    MI5 focuses on UK national security, whereas MI6 focuses on foreign intelligence.

Version courte (15 min) : faire la Worksheet 3A et recopier la trace écrite.

À envoyer ou à rendre : les deux fiches, les exercices, l’enregistrement et le quiz.

Lesson 4 · A good Bond, a good employee?

Are screen appeal and professional suitability the same thing?

Durée : 35 min · Documents : Lesson 4

  1. CE Lire les Student documents A et B, puis faire la Worksheet 4A. Which panel? (casting, professional recruitment ou both) (8 min).
  2. CE Remplir la Worksheet 4B. Claim and evidence (6 min).
  3. Étude de la langue Faire les exercices 1 à 4 : comparatifs, too / enough, nuancer avec may, some, not necessarily, because / although (10 min).
  4. PO Enregistrer une réponse de 40 secondes : Should the qualities used to choose the next Bond also be used to recruit an intelligence officer? (4 min).
  5. PE Devoir : 80 à 100 mots, What can Bond show us, and what might Bond hide? (une comparaison, une affirmation nuancée, une référence à un document) (7 min de plan).

Trace écrite à recopier dans le cahier

Casting and recruitment have different aims. A convincing performance does not necessarily prove professional suitability.

Official careers documents provide information, but they also aim to attract applicants. A careful argument uses evidence and avoids absolute claims.

Je vérifie

  1. Qualify: Screen heroes are good employees.

    Screen heroes are not necessarily good employees.

  2. Complete: This statement is ___ general to be reliable.

    too

Version courte (15 min) : faire la Worksheet 4B et recopier la trace écrite.

Lesson 5 · The responsible officer’s code

What must, mustn’t and don’t have to mean at work?

Durée : 35 min · Documents : Lesson 5

  1. CE Lire le Student document A (règles fictives) et la Grammar reference, puis faire la Worksheet 5A. Meaning first (8 min).
  2. Étude de la langue Faire la Worksheet 5B : exercices 1 à 3 (8 min).
  3. CE PE Lire le Student document B. The unsupported report et remplir la Worksheet 5C. Resolve the scenario (6 min).
  4. PE Worksheet 5D : écrire cinq règles expliquées (deux obligations, deux interdictions, une absence d’obligation) (8 min).
  5. PO Enregistrer une règle, son sens et sa raison, puis transférer la structure à un lieu de travail connu (4 min).

Trace écrite à recopier dans le cahier

Must / have to: it is necessary (You must check sources). Mustn't: it is forbidden (You mustn't invent evidence). Don't have to: it is not necessary (You don't have to wear a suit).

An officer must check sources because decisions depend on reliable information.

Je vérifie

  1. Correct: “He doesn’t has to travel.”

    He doesn’t have to travel.

  2. Keep the meaning: It is forbidden to invent evidence. You…

    You mustn’t invent evidence.

Version courte (15 min) : faire la Worksheet 5A et recopier la trace écrite.

À envoyer ou à rendre : les fiches 5A à 5D, l’enregistrement et la carte d’entretien préparée pour la lesson 6 (devoir).

Lesson 6 · The interview: evidence, not just adjectives

How can I support a quality with an example?

Durée : 40 min · Documents : Lesson 6 et la page Assessment

  1. CE Lire l’offre fictive (Student document A) et la meilleure réponse (Student document B) (6 min).
  2. Étude de la langue Faire les exercices 1 à 3 : construire des questions, corriger une réponse, demander une clarification (8 min).
  3. PE Remplir la Candidate worksheet (8 min).
  4. PI Évaluation sommative, oral : entretien d’embauche à distance. L’enseignante (ou un adulte qui lit les questions de la Recruiter worksheet) pose au moins cinq questions par appel ou par messages vocaux ; enregistrer les réponses. Sans interlocuteur : enregistrer les réponses aux cinq questions, une par une, puis une demande de clarification (10 min).
  5. PE Remplir la Peer-feedback grid pour soi-même, puis le devoir : 80 à 100 mots, How did my interview improve? (8 min).

Trace écrite à recopier dans le cahier

I chose … because … I supported the quality … with the example … I used … correctly. My next target is …

Je vérifie

  1. Correct: “I would like join MI6.”

    I would like to join MI6.

  2. Build the question: your / are / what / qualities?

    What are your qualities?

Version courte (15 min) : remplir la Candidate worksheet ; l’entretien se fait un autre jour.

À envoyer ou à rendre : les exercices, la Candidate worksheet, l’enregistrement de l’entretien, la grille et le texte de 80 à 100 mots.

Compréhension de l'écrit (/10)

Document : un article de presse britannique authentique (2 300 à 4 000 signes) sur le recrutement du MI6 ou sur le choix d'un nouvel acteur pour James Bond, ou un extrait du site officiel du Secret Intelligence Service (page « Our history »).

Questions de guidage (en français) :

  • Présentez le document.
  • Quelle image du renseignement britannique le document donne-t-il ?
  • Relevez deux faits et une opinion.
  • Quel lien le document établit-il entre la fiction et l'institution réelle ?
