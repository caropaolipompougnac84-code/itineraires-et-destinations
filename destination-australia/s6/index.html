The Working Holiday visa (subclass 417)

  • Who? French citizens aged 18 to 35 when they apply.
  • How long? 12 months. You can get a 2nd and a 3rd year if you do "specified work" in regional Australia (3 months for the 2nd visa, 6 months for the 3rd).
  • Work? Yes, but maximum 6 months with the same employer (check current exemptions, including some work in remote resources, on the official page).
  • Study? Up to 4 months.
  • Cost? AUD 840 for a first visa (since 1 July 2026).
  • Money? About AUD 5,000 in savings, plus a return ticket or the money to buy one.
  • Where? Online, on the Department of Home Affairs website, with an ImmiAccount. No English test is required.

Your checklist before you leave

DocumentWhy?
PassportValid for your whole stay. In France it costs about €86 and can take weeks: apply early.
Visa grant letterYour visa is electronic, linked to your passport. Keep a copy.
Health insuranceFrench social security does not cover you in Australia, and medical care is very expensive. Buy a "working holiday" insurance before you leave (about €430 to €450 a year).
Vaccination recordTake your carnet de santé or international vaccination certificate, and your prescriptions in English.

It is not a holiday: it is an expatriation

You leave France for at least one year, maybe more. In French administration, you become a "Français établi hors de France".

In France, before you leaveWhy?
Register on the registre des Français établis hors de France (online, without a fee)Recommended for stays over 6 months: the consulate can help you, and you can vote from Australia.
Ask the CFE (Caisse des Français de l'Étranger) or a private insurerTo keep health cover; French social security stops when you leave.
Tell the tax office, your bank, France Travail and your phone companyChange of address, end of contracts, taxes for the year you leave.
Scan all your documents (passport, diplomas, licence, vaccinations)Keep them online, in case you lose the originals.
Think about the future: staying longer?A second and third Working Holiday visa, a Student visa, or an employer-sponsored visa (Skills in Demand, subclass 482).

When you arrive

  1. Get an Australian SIM card.
  2. Open a bank account.
  3. Apply for a Tax File Number (TFN) with the Australian Taxation Office: without it, you pay more tax.
  4. Register with the French consulate (recommended, and it does not require a fee).
