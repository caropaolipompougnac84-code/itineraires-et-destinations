Séance 7 · CE, médiation

Lea's bag at customs

WhatRule (check ABF)
AlcoholDuty-free allowance: 2.25 litres.
Cigarettes25 cigarettes.
Other goodsAUD 900.
CashDeclare AUD 10,000 or more.
Food, plants, animal productsDeclare them. When in doubt, declare.

For the student

  1. Léa packed: a ham sandwich, two litres of wine, a wooden spoon from home, and AUD 11,000 in cash.
  2. What must she declare? What can be a problem?
  3. Complete this sentence: When in doubt, ...
  4. Does declaring cost money? Answer in one sentence.
  5. ★★ Write 5 lines to Léa, in English, before she boards the plane.

Pour le professeur

20 minutes. Activité d'abord, barème ensuite.

  1. Insistez : déclarer ne coûte rien. Cacher un produit alimentaire peut coûter cher.
  2. Sources : Australian Border Force. Faites revérifier les seuils.
Corrigé

Declare: the sandwich (food), the wooden spoon (wood), the cash (over 10,000). The wine is under 2.25 L if it is her only alcohol, but she should still be ready to show it. When in doubt, declare. Declaring does not cost you anything.

Trace écrite

At the border, declare food, plants, animal products, wood, and cash of AUD 10,000 or more. When in doubt, declare. Declaring does not cost you anything.

Travail à la maison

Make a checklist of 8 items for your own bag. Tick what you must declare.

Court, à rendre par écrit. Correction au début de la séance 8.
