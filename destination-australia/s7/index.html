Arrival: Customs and Biosecurity

Australia protects its farms and its unique wildlife. What seems ordinary in a suitcase can be a serious problem at the border.

  • Food, plants, seeds, wood, animal products: you must declare them, even a sandwich from the plane or a jar of homemade jam.
  • Muddy shoes, hiking or camping equipment: declare them.
  • Medicines: declare them, with your prescription in English.
  • Duty-free allowance (adults): 2.25 litres of alcohol, 25 cigarettes, and AUD 900 of goods bought abroad.
  • Cash: AUD 10,000 or more must be declared.
  • If you do not declare and the officers find it: an on-the-spot fine of thousands of dollars, and in serious cases your visa can be cancelled.

The golden rule: Unsure? Declare it, or bin it. Declaring does not cost you anything. Bins are waiting for you before the biosecurity area.

Sources : Australian Border Force, duty free. Cash of AUD 10,000 or more must be declared (Incoming Passenger Card).
