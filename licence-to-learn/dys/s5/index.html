Lesson 5. The rules of a spy

Must, mustn’t and don’t have to are not the same. Students write a Spy Code, then a public-domain poem that M reads in Skyfall.

Road map

Lesson of 55 minutes, timed for 52 minutes. Six lessons of 55 minutes, each timed for 52 minutes. Lesson 6 is the final-task interview.

  1. Recap and the grammar table 8 min
  2. Practice: must, mustn’t or don’t have to? 12 min
  3. Write a Spy Code of five rules 14 min
  4. Going further: M reads Tennyson 12 min
  5. Share two rules orally, written record 6 min
  • Written record. Copy the three forms (must / have to, mustn’t, don’t have to) with one example each, and two of your Spy Code rules.
  • Participation. Everyone speaks at least once: each student reads one completed gap, or later shares one Spy Code rule.
  • Homework for next lesson. Learn your five rules. Tomorrow you will be the candidate or the recruiter: prepare three qualities and one sentence with must and one with mustn’t.

Stopping point. After the Spy Code has been written. The Tennyson poem is for students who are ready to go further; if time is short, leave it as optional homework.

Emergency exit · 8 min. Check three gaps orally. Each student says one rule with must or mustn’t. Write the homework (prepare the interview) on the board.

Recap. Who is C today? Who is the Director General of MI5? Why are most agents’ names secret?

Spy code

  • A spy must keep secrets.
  • A spy mustn’t tell his family about his job.
  • An MI6 officer has to travel a lot.
  • An MI5 officer doesn’t have to live abroad.
The ruleFormMeaningIn French

don’t have tono obligationit is not necessaryne pas être obligé de

Careful! mustn’t is not the same as don’t have to. You mustn’t lie = it is forbidden. You don’t have to wear a suit = it is not obligatory.

Practice: must, mustn’t or don’t have to?

  1. You ______ be British to work for MI6.
  2. You ______ post photos of your job on Instagram.
  3. You ______ drive an Aston Martin. That’s only in the films!
  4. You ______ be discreet.
  5. You ______ tell anyone your password.

Answer key

  1. must (or have to)
  2. mustn’t
  3. don’t have to
  4. must (or have to)
  5. mustn’t

Write your Spy Code

Write five rules for a new agent. Use must, mustn’t, has to and doesn’t have to.

Going further: M reads a poem

In Skyfall, M is in trouble. In front of the ministers, she reads the end of “Ulysses”, a poem by Alfred Tennyson (1842). The poem is in the public domain.

Tho’ much is taken, much abides; and tho’ We are not now that strength which in old days Moved earth and heaven, that which we are, we are; One equal temper of heroic hearts, Made weak by time and fate, but strong in will To strive, to seek, to find, and not to yield.

Why does M read this poem? What is she saying about MI6 and about herself?

Help: to yield = céder · to strive = lutter · strong in will = déterminé.

For the teacher. This poem is for the students who are most at ease. Possible paths: growing older without giving up; remaining true to oneself; an old institution that is still useful.

Answer key (possible readings)

M is defending an old service that some people think is weak or out of date. The poem says that much has been lost, but that the will remains: “to strive, to seek, to find, and not to yield.” She is speaking about MI6 and about herself.

Written record and homework. Copy the three forms with one example each, and two of your rules. Homework: learn your five rules and prepare three qualities, one must and one mustn’t for the interview.
