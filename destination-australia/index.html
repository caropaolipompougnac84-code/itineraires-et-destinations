Destination Australia

A guide for young French citizens planning to move to Australia.

Level
Baccalauréat professionnel and CFA, CEFR B1. Official programme: langues vivantes in the vocational pathway, arrêté of 3 April 2019, Bulletin officiel spécial n° 5 of 11 April 2019. At the baccalauréat professionnel, the levels expected at the examination are B1+ for langue vivante A and B1 for langue vivante B. This sequence is written for B1.
Enquiry
How can a young French citizen prepare for life and work in Australia?
Theme
The Working Holiday visa in Australia and job hunting abroad. Students read practical information, compare costs, write an Australian resume and take part in a café job interview.
Lessons
Eight lessons of 55 minutes, timed for 52 minutes, plus an assessment page. Each lesson has a stopping point, an 8-minute emergency exit, and three visible outcomes: a written record, participation (everyone speaks at least once), and homework for the next lesson.
Assessment
A reading test on a classroom job advert (/10), a listening and vocabulary test (/10), and an oral interview grid (/20). These are classroom assessments, not official examination papers.

Overlaps with other sequences. This sequence is the only official resource on this site that studies the Australian Working Holiday visa (subclass 417), the Tax File Number, specified work and lawful pay in Australia. Getting Around London, India mentions Australia only as an optional country in a homework bank on railways; it does not teach visas, work or everyday life there. Service dialogues in other vocational sequences, when you study them, do not replace the visa, pay and safety work done here.

  5. Lesson 5. Regional work and the White Card 52 min

The assessment page has the reading test, the listening test and the oral grid, with the answer keys visible.

Your project

You are a young French citizen planning a working holiday in Australia. You will prepare a realistic travel plan, organise your first week, apply for a job and discuss everyday life with new people.

Final task. Take part in a job interview for a Melbourne café. Explain your experience, availability and visa status. Ask about hourly pay, weekend rates and shifts.

Understand Read practical information and a job advert.
Listen Identify key information at Sydney Airport.
Speak Explain your plans and ask precise questions.
Write Prepare a resume and a short application.
Act Make responsible choices about work and travel.

My bristol flashcards · how to use them

There is no ready-made deck. Make your own bristol cards, in English. On the front, write the word or short phrase. On the back, write a short definition or an example sentence from this sequence. Test yourself, then test a partner. Keep a pack for visa and money words, a pack for work and pay, and a pack for everyday Australian expressions.

Useful items to start with: Working Holiday visa, subclass 417, available savings, grant notice, condition 8501, TFN, casual loading, award, specified work, 88 calendar days, White Card, RSA, bond, red and yellow flags, arvo, brekkie, barbie, mates, no worries, flat white, CBD.

Official information and checks

Research date: 6 November 2026, Europe/Paris. Figures can change. This sequence is a language-learning resource, not individual immigration, tax or insurance advice.

  • Fair Work Ombudsman, National Minimum Wage from 1 July 2026: AUD 26.44 an hour, or AUD 33.05 for a casual employee including the 25% casual loading, where the adult National Minimum Wage applies. Check the applicable award. Pay guides.
  • Australian Taxation Office, Working Holiday Maker withholding: a registered employer generally withholds 15% on the first AUD 45,000. TFN application for temporary visitors.
  • Home Affairs, first Working Holiday visa (subclass 417) and current visa pricing. For classroom budgeting, allow about AUD 650 to 850 and confirm the live fee before applying. This range is not an official quotation. Usual initial funds: about AUD 5,000 plus a ticket out, or enough money to buy one. Recheck before you apply.
  • Home Affairs, specified-work conditions and the Working Holiday Maker program.
  • Services Australia, reciprocal health care agreements: France is not on the list. A French WHV alone does not grant Medicare eligibility. Insurance condition 8501: check the conditions of your own visa.
