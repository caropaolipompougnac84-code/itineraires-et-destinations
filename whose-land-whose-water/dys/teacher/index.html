Teacher notes

This adapted route follows the same eight lessons and the same cultural enquiry as the standard sequence. It has its own short texts, a worked example before each exercise and self-check answers. It is usable alone without an AESH, including by an absent student.

Routes on this site: S (SEGPA), C (CAP), P (bac pro), G (lycée général, LVA or LVB). CFA is a training setting, not a CEFR level: CFA apprentices follow Route C or Route P according to the qualification prepared.

Every lesson keeps the same structure: road map, short text with device read-aloud, Building your vocabulary, numbered exercises, speaking by route, written record, homework, and an eight-minute exit from minute 44, inside the 52 minutes.

The whole film The Grizzlies is compulsory home viewing before lesson 7, set as homework in lesson 6, in the adapted version too. The student may watch it in two parts, with English subtitles. Lesson 7 has a short step-by-step guide, a care note and the film viewing worksheet, with worked examples and visible self-check answers. The adapted worksheet is on the adapted lesson 7 and in the downloads.

The method The Sentence I Cannot Finish Alone opens the study of the film. The student document includes an adapted dyslexia route. The teacher sheet and the correction sheet are on that page and in the downloads.

Listening: the YouTube documents of the standard sequence are kept, and lesson 7 adds the official trailer of The Grizzlies. When a video cannot be played, the student uses the support account and labels the work “Reading-support route”, not unaided listening.

The two paintings are reproduced with permission and described in words in lesson 1, so that the activity remains possible if the image cannot be seen.

Show the whole lesson on the page. Answer keys stay visible: no tabs, no accordions.

Programmes: 2025 English programme (BO n° 22 of 29 May 2025) for lycée général; 2019 programme (BO spécial n° 5 of 11 April 2019) for bac pro and CAP. Full notes are on the standard teacher page.
