Lesson 8. An Australian resume

How do you present your experience honestly, without private tax or passport details?

Road map

Lesson of 55 minutes, timed for 52 minutes. 8 lessons.

  1. Recap (marked /3) 5 min
  2. Read the model resume and what not to include 12 min
  3. Write your one-page resume and a short application message 18 min
  4. Prepare the assessments and the final interview 10 min
  5. Written record, exit ticket and homework 7 min
  • Written record. A one-page resume with visa status and availability, no TFN, no passport number, no date of birth and no photograph; plus a short application message.
  • Participation. Everyone speaks at least once: one skill or one experience from your resume, said aloud to a partner.
  • Homework for next lesson. Finish the one-page resume (use fictional contact details for the classroom version). Prepare a budget with sources and an emergency reserve. Find one real job advert, record its source and date; do not apply as part of this exercise. Rehearse the interview.

Stopping point. End of this lesson.

Emergency exit · 8 min. If time runs short at this stopping point, condense the end of the lesson: dictate a short written record (two sentences); a quick round so that everyone speaks at least once (one sentence each); write the homework on the board.

Recap · marked /3
  1. Which documents does the airport officer ask for?
  2. What does CBD mean, and what does “arvo” mean?
  3. Give one useful sentence for a job interview.

Answer key

  1. Passport and visa grant information.
  2. Central business district; afternoon.
  3. Accept any from the useful language (experience, availability, weekend rate, award, “Could you repeat that, please?”).

This model is entirely fictional. Replace the experience and skills with your own accurate information. Do not include a passport number, TFN, date of birth or photograph.

Alex Example

Melbourne, VIC · Fictional phone: 0000 000 000 · alex@example.invalid

Visa status: Working Holiday visa, subclass 417. Availability: early mornings and weekend shifts, subject to visa conditions.

Professional summary

Reliable French applicant with customer-service and café experience. Comfortable working in a team and learning new routines. Looking for a casual café role.

Relevant certification

Victorian RSA: include only if actually completed and valid for the role. This fictional example does not claim a real certificate.

Work experience

Café assistant · Example Café, France · 2024 to 2026

  • Prepared coffee drinks and served customers.
  • Used the till and checked orders.
  • Kept the service area clean during busy shifts.

Skills and languages

Teamwork, customer service, time management. French: fluent. English: B1, able to handle familiar workplace situations and ask for clarification.

References

Available on request, with the referees’ permission.

Your task. Write your own one-page resume. Add one example showing teamwork. Write a short application message with a greeting, the role you want, relevant experience and availability.

The assessment page has the reading test, the listening test and the oral grid. The oral interview uses the same situation as the final task.

Exit ticket
  1. Name two things you must not put on this classroom resume.
  2. What four elements belong in the short application message?

Answer key

  1. Any two of: passport number, TFN, date of birth, photograph, real private contact details in a public file.
  2. A greeting, the role you want, relevant experience, availability.

Homework

  1. Prepare a budget with your sources and an emergency reserve.
  2. Finish a one-page resume using fictional contact details for the classroom version.
  3. Write six interview questions and rehearse a short introduction.
  4. Find one real job advert. Record its source and date. Do not share private information or apply as part of this exercise.
