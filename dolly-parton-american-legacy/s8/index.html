Lesson 8. Dolly's legacy

Debatable question: Is generosity a private choice or a public duty for the very rich?
By the end of this lesson, I can: pay tribute to someone, explain the goal of an action, describe how a programme works, say what will remain.

Teacher’s note. This is a lesson of tribute. We speak about Dolly with respect.

For the teacher: timing (52 minutes)
Step Time Activity

2 4-7 The question of the day
3 7-18 Documents 1 to 3 + video (3:06)

Stop point (minute 47). Every student has said one sentence of tribute.

Emergency exit (8 minutes). Play the video (0:52 to 3:58) with the passive task, then each student says one purpose sentence (to / so that).

Step 1. Recap

  1. Two signs of an AI-generated video. 2. Complete: There is no ... that Miley inherited the fortune. 3. Fact, claim or fake: Dolly left Jolene to Miley.

    Answer key

    Answer key: 1 monotone voice, lips that don’t match the words · 2 evidence · 3 unverified claim.

Step 2. The question of the day

Ask: If you were very rich, would you have to give money away? Or is it your private choice?

Transition. Dolly gave a lot. Let’s see what, how, and why.

Step 3. Documents

Document 1. The announcement

Dolly Rebecca Parton died in Nashville, Tennessee, on 25 August 2026, aged 80 (official announcement on dollyparton.com). According to AP, she died after a brief battle with cancer. Her publicist announced the news in a statement, and her official website published a video message from her nephew Bryan Seaver.

Document 2. The Buddy Program and the Imagination Library

In 1988, Dolly created the Dollywood Foundation in Sevier County, where many students dropped out of high school. Through the Buddy Program, every seventh- and eighth-grade student paired up with a “buddy”, and each student who graduated from high school received $500 from Dolly. According to the Dollywood Foundation, the dropout rate for these classes fell from 35% to 6%.

In 1995, Dolly created the Imagination Library as a tribute to her father, who could not read. Every month, registered children receive a book by post, from birth to the age of five, at no cost to families. The programme now works in five countries: the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Ireland and Australia. In Europe, it started in Rotherham, England, in 2007 (BBC). More than 330 million books have been sent (CBS 8, 2026), and the programme continues after her death.

Video. The journey of an Imagination Library book

Watch or listen: The Journey of an Imagination Library Book, Dolly Parton’s Imagination Library channel: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IjxcK1ZZw3c
Play from 0:52 to 3:58 (3 minutes 6 seconds).

Exact link, to keep unchanged: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IjxcK1ZZw3c

Task (while watching). Put the steps in order and note the verb used.
a. The books are mailed by the postal service. b. Titles are selected by a committee. c. Books are printed. d. Each book is labelled and shrink-wrapped. e. Parents register their children.

Answer key

Answer key: b (1:00-1:14), c (1:37-2:09), e (2:17-2:34), d (3:29), a (3:44-3:52).

Document 3. A donation for research

In April 2020, at the start of the COVID-19 pandemic, Dolly gave one million dollars to Vanderbilt University Medical Center in Nashville, in honour of her friend Dr Naji Abumrad. According to Vanderbilt, the Dolly Parton COVID-19 Research Fund contributed to early research linked to the Moderna vaccine (VUMC).

Reading task. For each action, find the year and the goal.

Answer key

Answer key: 1988 Dollywood Foundation and Buddy Program: fight dropout · 1995 Imagination Library: help young children love reading, in honour of her father · 2020 donation to Vanderbilt: support COVID-19 research.

Step 4. Expanding your vocabulary: generosity and memory

Word Meaning Example
to pass away to die (polite) Dolly passed away in 2026.
a tribute words or actions to honour someone Miley wrote a tribute.
a charity an organisation that helps people The Dollywood Foundation is a charity.
a donation / to donate money given to help / to give She donated a million dollars.
a philanthropist a rich person who gives to good causes Dolly was a philanthropist.
literacy the ability to read and write The library helps literacy.
to drop out (of school) to leave school without a diploma Many students used to drop out.
research scientific work Her money helped vaccine research.
to give back to help the community that helped you Dolly wanted to give back.
to live on to continue to exist Her music will live on.
at no cost to without paying anything Books are sent at no cost to families.

Words to argue about duty and choice: It is up to each person to... · Society expects... · It is a moral duty to... · Nobody can force someone to... · It depends on...

Exercises on vocabulary

Exercise H1. Match. 1 literacy · 2 to drop out · 3 a donation · 4 to live on / a money given · b to continue to exist · c reading and writing · d to leave school

Answer key

Answer key: 1c, 2d, 3a, 4b.

Exercise H2. Fill in the gaps: philanthropist · give back · at no cost · tribute
Dolly was a great (1) __. She wanted to (2) _ to her region. Children receive books (3) to families. This lesson is a (4) ___ to her.

Answer key

Answer key: 1 philanthropist · 2 give back · 3 at no cost · 4 tribute.

Step 5. The teacher’s point

Teacher script. Start from the video: ask what happens to the book (selected, printed, labelled, mailed). Write the passive forms. Then ask why Dolly did it, and collect purpose sentences.

Point Form Example
Purpose to + verb; in order to + verb (formal); so that + subject + can / could She gave money to help research. She created the library so that children could love books.
Present passive be + past participle (+ by + agent) A book is sent every month. The titles are chosen by a committee.
will prediction about the future Her songs will live on. Children will still receive books.

Quick check. Make it passive: The library sends a book every month. → A book is sent every month (by the library).

Step 6. Exercises

Exercise 1. Match the action and the goal.
1. She created the Imagination Library · 2. She gave a million dollars to Vanderbilt · 3. She built Dollywood near her home · 4. She paid $500 to graduates
a. to support COVID-19 research · b. so that children could love reading · c. to create jobs in her region · d. so that students would finish school

Answer key

Answer key: 1b, 2a, 3c, 4d.

Exercise 2. The journey of a book (present passive). Each book (choose) ... by a committee. Then it (print) ... and (label) ... . Finally it (mail) ... to the child, at no cost to the family.

Answer key

Answer key: is chosen · is printed · is labelled · is mailed.

Exercise 3. Transform into the passive.
1. Volunteers register the children. 2. The postal service delivers the books. 3. Local partners fund the programme.

Answer key

Answer key: 1 The children are registered by volunteers. 2 The books are delivered by the postal service. 3 The programme is funded by local partners.

Exercise 4. Purpose: reformulate with so that.
1. She opened the park to create jobs. (so that people / can) 2. She sent books to help children read. (so that children / could)

Answer key

Answer key: 1 She opened the park so that people could find jobs. 2 She sent books so that children could read.

Exercise 5. A tribute (80 to 100 words): who she was, two things she did (past simple), why (to / so that), what will remain (will). Respectful tone.
Model answer: Dolly Parton was a singer, a songwriter and a generous woman. She grew up poor in Tennessee. She created the Imagination Library so that children could have books at home, and she gave money to help research during the COVID-19 pandemic. Her songs, like Jolene and 9 to 5, will live on, and millions of children will remember the books with her name. Her life shows that you can come from nothing and give a lot.

Help box. Frame: Dolly Parton was... She created... so that... She gave... to... Her songs will...
Going further. Present perfect for a review: She has inspired millions of people. The library has sent more than 330 million books.

Step 7. Speaking task: “The radio tribute”

  1. Individual preparation (3 min). Prepare a 45-second radio tribute: one fact, one goal, one thing that will remain.
  2. Groups of four (4 min). Each student performs; the others note one purpose sentence they hear.
  3. Class debate (2 min). Is generosity a private choice or a public duty for the very rich? Three volunteers answer with one argument each.

Possible answer: It is up to each person to decide, but I think the very rich have a moral duty to give back. Dolly did not have to create the library, yet she did it so that children could read. That is why her name will live on.

Step 8. Written record

Lesson 8. Dolly’s legacy
Dolly Parton died on 25 August 2026, aged 80. She created the Dollywood Foundation (1988) to fight school dropout and the Imagination Library (1995) so that young children could receive a book every month. More than 330 million books have been sent. In 2020, she gave a million dollars for COVID-19 research.
Grammar. Purpose: to / in order to / so that + could. Present passive: is sent, are chosen. will: her music will live on.
My answer: I think generosity is... because...

Step 9. Homework

  1. Prepare the final task: choose your group and your role (see Lesson 9).
  2. Revise all flashcards from Lessons 1 to 8 (3-box method).
  3. Help box: record your 45-second tribute and listen to it once.

Passing the torch. “I will always love her and want to make her proud.” (Miley Cyrus, 26 August 2026). How can we carry on someone’s legacy?
