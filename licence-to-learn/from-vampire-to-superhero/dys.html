The 4 lives of Robert Pattinson

I · The Musician and Model

1986 to 2004

Robert was born in London in 1986. He was shy. At 12, he worked as a model. He played the guitar and sang in pubs. He joined a theatre group at 13.

II · The Teen Idol

2005 to 2012

In 2005, he played in Harry Potter. In 2008, he became Edward Cullen, the vampire of Twilight. He was very famous. But people saw him only as the vampire.

III · The Indie Risk-Taker

2012 to 2019

After Twilight, he chose small films. He took risks. He played an explorer and a bank robber. Critics said he was very good.

IV · The Dark Knight

2020 to today

In 2022, he became Batman. Some fans did not like it at first. The film was a big success. He did not give up.
