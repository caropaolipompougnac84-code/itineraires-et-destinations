Séance 1 · EOC

Look at the paintings

For the student

  1. Choose one painting.
  2. Say what you see: in the foreground..., in the background...
  3. Which painting shows hard work? Which one shows a lonely man? Which one shows an arrival?
  4. ★★ In the Cook painting, Aboriginal people are far away, on the side. What does this tell us about the painter's point of view?
  5. ★★ Which painting would you choose for a poster called "Work in Australia"? Give one reason.

Pour le professeur

15 à 20 minutes. EOC, puis mise en commun.

  1. Projetez les quatre tableaux. Laissez d'abord regarder en silence (une minute).
  2. ★ : description guidée (foreground / background).
  3. ★★ : point de vue colonial dans le tableau de Fox, puis choix argumenté d'une affiche.
  4. Huit minutes de devoir : six à huit lignes sur un tableau, à ramener séance 2.
Corrigé

Work: Shearing the Rams. Lonely man: Down on His Luck. Arrival: Fox (Cook). Fox places Aboriginal people on the margin: a colonial point of view, painted for the anniversary of Federation.

Trace écrite

In the foreground I can see... In the background... This painting shows...

Travail à la maison

Choose one painting. Write 6 to 8 lines: what you see, and what it tells us about Australia.

Court, à rendre par écrit. Correction au début de la séance 2.

Pour en savoir plus · Repères pour la classe
