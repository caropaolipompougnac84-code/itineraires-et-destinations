Assessment

The final task is the interview in lesson 6. Use the speaking routes on that page. If you work alone, read a recruiter question, pause, answer aloud, and tick each completed question.

End-of-unit student check:

  • □ I can distinguish a casting rumour from an announcement.
  • □ I can describe one actor’s past experience.
  • □ I have completed the trailer task or its clearly labelled catch-up alternative.
  • □ I can explain one difference between MI5 and MI6.
  • □ I can question one stereotype.
  • □ I know the difference between mustn’t and don’t have to.
  • □ I have spoken in every lesson.
  • □ I have six written records.
  • □ I have completed an interview.
