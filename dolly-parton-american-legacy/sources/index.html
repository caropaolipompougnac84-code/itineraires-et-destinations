Sources and fact-check notes for the teacher

Facts rechecked on 13 November 2026. Earlier checks on the original site were dated 2 November 2026.

15.1 Corrections made to the previous version

Previous text Status What the new version says
Former word for each 55-minute class (S1 to S9) changed “Lesson” everywhere
Footer CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 changed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, as requested
Entry / Core / Stretch tiers changed one progression for Première bac pro, with Help boxes and Going further boxes inside each lesson (instruction “Niveau imposé”)
“died peacefully [...] after a brief illness” made more precise died in Nashville on 25 August 2026, aged 80, after a brief battle with cancer (AP, People)
“Her nephew announced the news in a video, as she had asked him years before” partly unverified her publicist announced it (AP); the official site published a video message from her nephew Bryan Seaver. “As she had asked him years before” has not been confirmed: removed.
“Her accountants were not sure [Dollywood] was a good idea” unverified removed
“Dolly told Miley that Hannah Montana had introduced her to a whole new set of young fans” no source found removed
Quotation “She’s just trying to find her own place and wings and learn to fly” (London Evening Standard) not rechecked removed from student documents
Quotation “Nobody gives Miley advice...” (Us Weekly, 2022) not rechecked removed from student documents
Stella Parton: “exploiting the loss and tragedies of others” not found in the sources consulted replaced by her quotation reported by NBC News: “There is a tremendous amount of AI and endless garbage being posted everyday”
fox43: the rumour “doesn’t appear to have any backing” not rechecked removed from student documents
Vanderbilt researcher’s quotation about the Moderna test wording not rechecked replaced by a sentence based on the VUMC page
Coat of Many Colors, “top five country hit” confirmed number 4 on the Hot Country Songs chart (American Songwriter)
Rainbowland: “Miley Cyrus, Topic” to be checked the video page shows the channel name “Miley Cyrus”; same URL kept
9 to 5 video: “DollyPartonVEVO channel” to be checked the video page shows the channel name “Dolly Parton”; same URL kept
“Instant Graduation” (mentioned in the brief) not found in any source no programme with this name could be confirmed. The verified programme is the Buddy Program of the Dollywood Foundation ($500 for each student who graduated). Its start date varies by source: 1988 for the Foundation (Knox News, TSPR), “early 1990s” for the Buddy Program (Dollywood Foundation website), 1990 on the Foundation’s history page. The sequence says “In 1988, Dolly created the Dollywood Foundation” and does not give a year for the Buddy Program.
Inheritance rumour unverified claim kept as a rumour only. No will has been made public; the D.P. Dean Trust (1997) and She’s Alive LLC are described in court documents reported by the Tennessean.

15.2 Points still uncertain (to be checked before publication)

  • Number of Dollywood employees (4,000 to 4,500) and visitors (about 3 million): figures from WATE, Knox News and CNN Business listed in the previous version, not rechecked on 13 November 2026.
  • Quotations of Lesson 2 on her look (BBC, The Guardian, CBS News 2003, People): listed in the previous version, not rechecked word for word on 13 November 2026. “It costs a lot of money to look this cheap” is confirmed by USA Today (21 February 2024) according to the previous sources page.
  • Dates of the Warhol retrospective at the Museum Ludwig: the poster gives 21.11.1989 to 11.2.1990; the LMU library catalogue gives an opening on 30 November 1989.
  • 9to5 organisation founded in Boston in 1973: widely documented, not rechecked against a primary source.
  • Legal dispute of September 2026 (She’s Alive LLC against Bryan Seaver): allegations not ruled on by a court; not used with students.
