Lesson 2. Bond, James Bond

I can say that Bond is fictional and works for MI6.

Road map

Lesson of 55 minutes, timed for 52 minutes. Lesson 2 of 6. Minutes 52 to 55: no new task. Working at home and need longer? Split the lesson into two blocks: this is a classroom structure, not a speed test.

  1. Say one sentence from the previous lesson (Lesson 1: no official actor has been announced) 4 min (minutes 0 to 4)
  2. Read the goal and the word box 4 min (minutes 4 to 8)
  3. Read or listen to your route’s text 8 min (minutes 8 to 16)
  4. Exercise 1, then check it 8 min (minutes 16 to 24)
  5. Pause: look away from the text or move 2 min (minutes 24 to 26)
  6. Exercise 2, then check it 8 min (minutes 26 to 34)
  7. Exercise 3, then check it 6 min (minutes 34 to 40)
  8. Say your final answer aloud with the speaking frame 4 min (minutes 40 to 44)
  9. Written record 4 min (minutes 44 to 48)
  10. Progress record 2 min (minutes 48 to 50)
  11. Read and write down the homework 2 min (minutes 50 to 52)
  • Written record. One sentence for your route (see below), copied or typed.
  • Participation. Say at least one answer aloud. In class, use a quiet voice; at home, say it to yourself. You do not need to record yourself.
  • Homework for next lesson. Make two flashcards: fictional and agent. Say your written record twice.

Stopping point. Minute 44: stop the main exercises. Keep the written record and the homework, even if an exercise is unfinished.

Emergency exit · 8 min. If only eight working minutes remain, this replaces the unfinished plan; it is not added to it.
  1. Minutes 0 to 2: say the emergency sentence of the lesson aloud.
  2. Minutes 2 to 5: complete the short written record.
  3. Minutes 5 to 7: write down the homework.
  4. Minute 7 to 8: check one answer with the key. Do not rush through all the unfinished exercises.

You do not need a film or a trailer to complete this lesson. The short text gives the key facts. If you can watch an official trailer later, that is optional.

Word box

WordMeaning
fictionalnot real; invented for a story
agenta person who works for an intelligence service
trailera short film used to present a film
attacka violent action against a place or a person

Choose your route: Route S · SEGPA · Route C · CAP · Route P · Bac pro · CFA. Do only your route.

Route S · SEGPA

Short text

James Bond is a fictional secret agent.

He works for MI6.

MI6 is a British service.

In the films, his boss is called M.

In the film Skyfall, MI6 is attacked in London.

Exercise S1

Find information

Worked example. Question: Is Bond real? Answer: No. He is fictional.

Now you.
1. Who does Bond work for?
2. Where is MI6 attacked in Skyfall?

Self-check

1. MI6.
2. In London.

Exercise S2

Complete

Worked example. “James Bond is a fictional secret agent.”

Now you.
Use: M, MI6.
1. He works for .
2. His boss is called .

Self-check

1. MI6.
2. M.

Exercise S3

Write and speak

Worked example. “Bond is fictional. He works for MI6.”

Now you.
Complete: “James Bond is . He works for .” Say it aloud.

Self-check

“James Bond is fictional. He works for MI6.”

Route C · CAP

Short text

James Bond is a fictional secret agent. He is not a real person.

He works for MI6, the British Secret Intelligence Service.

In the films, his boss is called M.

In Skyfall (2012), MI6 is attacked in London.

A trailer can help you guess the story, but the key facts are already in this text.

Exercise C1

True or false

Worked example. “Bond works for a French service.” False. He works for MI6, a British service.

Now you.
1. Bond is a real agent.
2. M is the name of his boss in the films.
3. In Skyfall, MI6 is attacked in London.

Self-check

1. False. He is fictional.
2. True.
3. True.

Exercise C2

Complete

Worked example. “Bond works for MI6.”

Now you.
1. MI6 is the British Secret Service.
2. A trailer is optional: the key facts are in the .

Self-check

1. Intelligence.
2. text.

Exercise C3

Write and speak

Worked example. “Bond is fictional, but the service in the story has a real name: MI6.”

Now you.
Write three short sentences with: Bond is… / He works for… / In Skyfall…

Self-check

Possible answer: “Bond is fictional. He works for MI6. In Skyfall, MI6 is attacked in London.”

Route P · Bac pro

Short text

James Bond is a fictional character created for books and films.

In the stories, he works for MI6, which is a real British service: the Secret Intelligence Service.

His boss, in the films, is called M.

In Skyfall (2012), the story shows an attack on MI6 in London.

A film can use a real place and a real service name, however the plot remains fiction.

You do not need to watch the trailer to state these facts.

Exercise P1

Identify the main point

Worked example. “The film is useful only because it is famous.” This is not the main point.

Now you.
Choose the best answer.
A. Everything in Skyfall is a real event.
B. The films use some real names, but Bond himself is fictional.
C. You cannot understand the lesson without the trailer.

Self-check

B.

Exercise P2

Qualify

Worked example. “MI6 is only a film idea.” Improved: “MI6 is a real service, but Bond is fictional.”

Now you.
Improve:
1. “Skyfall is a documentary about MI6.”
2. “M is the real title of the MI6 chief.”

Self-check

Possible answers:
1. “Skyfall is a fiction film that uses the name of a real service.”
2. “M is the name used in the films; the real title is not M.”

Exercise P3

Explain

Worked example. “A film can use a real building and still tell a fictional story.”

Now you.
Write 50 to 70 words. Use however. Mention Bond, MI6 and Skyfall.

Self-check

Your answer should separate fiction from real names and use however.

CFA pathway

CAP apprentice: use Route C. Bac pro apprentice: use Route P. Do Exercises 1 and 2 of your route, then do this task instead of Exercise 3. Keep the same written record and homework.

CFA task

Separate the story from the workplace

Worked example. “A training video may show a real factory. The story in the video can still be invented.”

Now you.
Write one sentence about what is real (the service, the city) and one sentence about what is fiction (Bond, the plot). Starter: “I can see that is real. However, is fiction.”

Self-check

The real element must be MI6 or London. The fiction element must be Bond or the plot of the film.

Written record

S: “James Bond is fictional. He works for MI6.”

C: “Bond is a fictional agent, but he works for a service with a real name: MI6.”

P: “The films use real names such as MI6, however James Bond remains a fictional character.”

Speaking frame. “James Bond is . He works for .”

Emergency sentence. “James Bond is fictional. He works for MI6.”

Homework

Make two flashcards: fictional and agent. Say your written record twice.

My progress record

  • I read or listened to the text.
  • I checked my answers.
  • I said at least one answer aloud.
  • I kept my written record.
  • I know the homework.
