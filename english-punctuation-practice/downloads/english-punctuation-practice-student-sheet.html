English Punctuation: Practice

Student sheet · A2 to B1 · 1 lesson of 55 minutes, timed for 52 minutes.

Use this sheet in class or at home. The video, the six sets and the answer keys are on the sequence page. Make your own bristol flashcards: the sequence page lists items to put on the cards and gives a blank template. This sheet is not a ready-made deck.

I can…

☐ I can choose a full stop, a question mark or an exclamation mark.

☐ I can use it’s / its, and the apostrophe of possession.

☐ I can use capital letters for days, months, languages, countries, cities and names.

☐ I can explain how a comma changes the meaning, and punctuate a short dialogue or a short text.

Set 1 · End marks

Where do you live   ·   I work in a garage   ·   Watch out   ·   What time is it   ·   My sister is a nurse   ·   Well done

Set 2 · The apostrophe

Its / It’s raining again.   The cat is eating its / it’s food.   brother’s / brothers’ car (one brother).   student’s / students’ bags (many students).   I dont / don’t like coffee.   Were / We’re late.

Set 3 · Capital letters

on monday i have english at nine. · my birthday is in july. · sara is spanish and lives in madrid. · we visited london in december.
