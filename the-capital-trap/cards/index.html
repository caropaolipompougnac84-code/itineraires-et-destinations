Cards B. Surprise cards

Surprise 1Juneau, the capital of Alaska, cannot be reached by road. You go there by plane or by boat.
Surprise 2Frankfort is the capital of Kentucky. It’s not Frankfurt in Germany!
Surprise 3Washington, D.C. is not in Washington State. They are nearly 4,000 km apart.
Surprise 4Montpelier, Vermont, is the smallest state capital in the USA.
Surprise 5Denver is called the “Mile High City” because it is about one mile above sea level.
Surprise 6Puerto Rico is a territory of the United States. It is not a state.
Surprise 7Tallahassee was chosen as the capital of Florida because it was halfway between two old cities.
Surprise 8Austin calls itself the “Live Music Capital of the World”.
Surprise 9There are two Springfields on Route 66. Only one is a capital.

Cards C. City ID cards

Four clues, from the most difficult to the easiest. A photograph of the city, when there is one in the sequence, can go on the back.

City ID · Chicago
  1. It’s in the Midwest.
  2. It’s next to a big lake.
  3. It’s the biggest city in Illinois, but not the capital.
  4. Its name starts with “Ch”.
City ID · San Francisco
  1. It’s on the West Coast.
  2. It’s famous for a red bridge.
  3. It’s in California.
  4. The bridge is the Golden Gate.
City ID · New Orleans
  1. It’s in the South, near the Gulf of Mexico.
  2. The Mississippi flows through it.
  3. It’s famous for jazz.
  4. It’s in Louisiana.
City ID · Las Vegas
  1. It’s in the desert.
  2. It’s famous for casinos and shows.
  3. It’s in Nevada, but it isn’t the capital.
  4. Its name means “the meadows” in Spanish.
City ID · Boston
  1. It’s on the East Coast.
  2. It’s a very old city with famous universities.
  3. It’s the capital of Massachusetts.
  4. A famous “Tea Party” took place there in 1773.
City ID · Honolulu
  1. It’s on an island in the Pacific Ocean.
  2. It’s very far from the other big cities.
  3. It’s the capital of Hawaii.
  4. Pearl Harbor is very close.
City ID · Miami
  1. It’s in the south-east.
  2. It’s famous for beaches and sunshine.
  3. It’s in Florida, but it isn’t the capital.
  4. Many people speak Spanish there.
City ID · Seattle
  1. It’s in the north-west.
  2. It rains a lot there.
  3. It’s in Washington State, but it isn’t the capital.
  4. It’s famous for the Space Needle.
