Issue de secours

  • La classe s'agite. On arrête deux minutes, on revient à une tâche courte et orale, chacun dit une phrase, puis on reprend le minutage.
  • Vous avez fini trop tôt. On allonge l'oral : un second exemple, une phrase de plus, ou une carte bristol.
  • Le matériel tombe en panne. On passe à la fiche papier, au tableau, ou à une dictée de la trace. La séance continue sans écran.
  • Tempête émotionnelle. Si un élève met vraiment le bazar, on l'écarte un moment selon le règlement de l'établissement (vie scolaire, autre salle, lieu surveillé), on lui confie une tâche courte liée à la séance (écris ce que signifie FIFO et le temps de vol depuis la France), puis on le réintègre calmement. Le cours minuté continue pour les autres.
★ · Read this text

There is no regular direct flight from Paris to Sydney. The trip is about 17,000 kilometres. With one stop, it usually takes 22 to 24 hours. Book your ticket only after your visa is granted. Home Affairs often needs about three months to process a Working Holiday visa.

Australia is a huge country. People use domestic flights, long-distance coaches, cars, and two famous trains: The Ghan (Adelaide to Darwin, 2,979 km, about 48 hours) and the Indian Pacific (Sydney to Perth, 4,352 km, about 68 hours).

In the mining regions, especially in the Pilbara in Western Australia, many workers are FIFO: fly-in fly-out. Small planes, often charter flights from Perth, take them to the mine for a roster such as 8 days on and 6 days off, or 2 weeks on and 1 week off. Shifts can last 12 to 12.5 hours.

★★ B2 · FIFO and the Working Holiday visa

FIFO can pay well, and you go home between rosters. It can also be hard: long shifts, isolation, heat, and time away from friends. A first Working Holiday visa (subclass 417) does not automatically open a FIFO job. Mines often want experience and safety tickets. Condition 8547 usually limits work with the same employer to six months, unless an exemption applies. Check the official page before you apply.

For the student

  1. How long is the flight from France, and why is there usually a stop?
  2. Name two ways to travel across Australia, and give one distance or time.
  3. What does FIFO mean? Where is it common?
  4. ★★ Would you accept a FIFO job? Give one advantage and one drawback.
  5. Plan a simple route: city of arrival, one trip inland or to another city, one way to come back.

Pour le professeur

20 minutes de CE, 15 minutes de parcours en binômes.

  1. Projetez l'image, puis un plan de l'Australie (sans le recopier : un fond de carte libre ou le tableau).
  2. Terme exact : fly-in fly-out, souvent abrégé FIFO. Pas « Fly and Fly Out » dans les documents élèves.
  3. Le visa 417 n'est pas un ticket automatique pour les mines. Condition 8547 : en général 6 mois chez le même employeur.
Corrigé

About 17,000 km, usually 22 to 24 hours, one stop; no regular direct Paris-Sydney flight. Ghan: 2,979 km, about 48 h. Indian Pacific: 4,352 km, about 68 h. FIFO: workers fly to the mine and home again, often from Perth to the Pilbara. Advantage: pay, time off. Drawback: isolation, long shifts. Last task is personal.

Trace écrite

The flight is long. Australia is huge, so people fly, drive or take trains such as The Ghan. FIFO means fly-in fly-out: workers fly to the mine, then home again.

Travail à la maison

Write 8 to 10 lines: your route from France to one Australian city, then one trip inside the country. Add one sentence about FIFO.

Court, à rendre par écrit. Correction au début de la séance 6.
