Sources

Only short educational paraphrases and teacher-written texts are used as classroom reading. Protected texts are not copied in full. Links below are the references.

Programme

  • Programme de langues vivantes des classes préparant au CAP et au baccalauréat professionnel, arrêté du 3 avril 2019, BO spécial n° 5 du 11 avril 2019. education.gouv.fr.

Identity, reporting and fact-checking

  • Simon Gardner, James Pearson and Blake Morrison, "In search of Banksy", Reuters, 13 March 2026, 10 a.m. GMT. reuters.com/investigates/special-report/global-art-banksy/. This is the source of the attributed identity claim used in Lesson 2. It is not treated as a confirmation by the artist.
  • NPR interview with Simon Gardner, 22 March 2026. NPR stated that it had not independently confirmed Reuters' findings. npr.org.
  • Reuters Fact Check, "London man misidentified as street artist Banksy", 19 March 2026, about photographs of George Georgiou. The person's photograph is not used on this site. reuters.com/fact-check.
  • Mail on Sunday, July 2008: an earlier press claim putting forward the name Robin Gunningham, already hedged at the time. Consulted through later reporting, including the Reuters investigation itself.

Ownership, authentication and related law

  • The Creative Foundation v Dreamland Leisure Ltd, High Court, 11 September 2015, about a section of wall bearing Art Buff.
  • Pest Control official site and FAQ, on certificates of authenticity and on works it does not certify. pestcontroloffice.com.
  • EUIPO Fifth Board of Appeal, 25 October 2022, case R 1246/2021-5, on a trademark concerning a monkey image. Trademark law only; not a general rule for this sequence.

Audiovisual

  • banksyfilm, Dismaland trailer, 25 August 2015, extract 00:00 to 02:07. youtube.com/watch?v=V2NG-MgHqEk.
  • banksyfilm, Shredding the Girl and Balloon: The Director's Half Cut, extract 00:00 to 02:00. youtube.com/watch?v=vxkwRNIZgdY.

Photographs (Wikimedia Commons)

A Creative Commons or public-domain licence for a photograph does not remove rights in the artwork that was photographed. Pest Control is Banksy's official contact and the body that issues certificates of authenticity for eligible works. It does not, according to its own FAQ, certify illegally executed works, and it does not treat the removal of a street mural as a normal way to obtain a certificate.
