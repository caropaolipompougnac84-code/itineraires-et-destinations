Anglais · Première bac pro (all streams) · B1 (CEFR)

Dolly Parton: An American Legacy

Nine lessons of 55 minutes, designed by Madame Carole Paoli Pompougnac. Programme de langues vivantes, BO spécial n° 5 du 11 avril 2019. One level for the whole class, with Help boxes and Going further boxes inside each lesson.

Why this sequence?

Dolly Rebecca Parton (19 January 1946 to 25 August 2026) was born in a one-room cabin in Locust Ridge, in the Great Smoky Mountains of East Tennessee. She became a singer, a songwriter, an actress, a businesswoman and one of the most generous donors in American culture. She was also the honorary godmother of Miley Cyrus. She died in Nashville on 25 August 2026, aged 80. In this sequence we talk about her life in the past.

When a famous person dies, two stories begin at the same time. The first is the story of what she really left: songs, jobs, books, values. The second is the story people invent: rumours, fake videos, "secret" inheritances. This sequence studies both, and asks you to tell the difference.

The big question of the sequence. Is a legacy what a person leaves behind, or what other people decide to make of it?

This is an open question. You can answer "what she leaves" (her songs, her library, her park exist whatever people say), or "what others make of it" (an icon is created by painters, fans, the media and even fake videos), or something in between. At the end of the sequence, you will defend your own answer.

The nine lessons

Each lesson asks a smaller, debatable question, and gives you the grammar you need for the task of the day.

LessonDebatable questionGrammar for the task
1. An icon in colourDoes a portrait show a person, or the image the public wants?present simple / present continuous; look, look like; comparatives and superlatives
2. Rags to rhinestonesCan you be proud of where you come from and still reinvent yourself completely?past simple; used to; order of adjectives

4. 9 to 5Can a pop song change the way people see work?frequency adverbs; have to, must, mustn't, don't have to, should

9. Final taskCan you present a legacy, play a professional scene and expose a rumour?review

Final task · Évaluation sommative

Backstage at the tribute concert. In groups, you prepare a tribute concert for Dolly Parton. You present her life, you play a backstage scene with a professional (dresser, hairdresser or make-up artist) and you tell the audience the truth about the inheritance rumour.

All three assessments have a student sheet, a teacher’s marking grid and a self-correction grid. Every grid includes a Language criterion (vocabulary reused, correct syntax).

Self-correction method (for all three assessments).

  1. Read the teacher’s grid and your mark.
  2. In the self-correction grid, write what you missed, criterion by criterion, using the words of the teacher’s grid.
  3. Write a new, improved version of your work that follows the grid.
  4. Underline in your new version the vocabulary and the structures you have added.

How every lesson works

Each lesson follows the same order so that students always know where they are.

  1. Recap and flashcards (the last lesson in three questions).
  2. The question of the day (a short debate starter).
  3. Documents (text, image, song or video, always with a task).
  4. Expanding your vocabulary (theme words + words to analyse and argue).
  5. The teacher’s point (one grammar point useful for the task of the day).
  6. Exercises (from recognition to production, with answer keys).
  7. Speaking task (every student speaks at least once).
  8. Written record (what goes into the notebook).
  9. Homework.

Each lesson ends with three results: a written record, a turn to speak for every student, homework for the next lesson.

The vocabulary heading is always Expanding your vocabulary. Students make their own flashcards in every lesson (the method is in the teacher's guide).

Real facts only

Every fact in this sequence has been checked against reliable sources (see the sources and fact-check notes). Dolly Parton's dates (born 19 January 1946, died 25 August 2026 in Nashville, aged 80) were verified on 13 November 2026 against the English Wikipedia page and against reports by AP News, Reuters, Billboard, USA Today and the announcement published on dollyparton.com. One famous claim, "Dolly left everything to Miley", is not verified: we study it as a rumour in Lesson 7, never as a fact.

Compréhension de l'écrit. Texte d'entraînement (le jour du CCF, prendre un document authentique inconnu de 15 lignes au plus) :

In 1995, Dolly Parton started a small project in Sevier County, Tennessee, where she grew up. Every month, each child under five received a book by post, at no cost to the family. Dolly did it for her father: he could not read or write, and she wanted other children to have a better chance. The project is called the Imagination Library. Today, it sends books to children in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Ireland and Australia. In 2023, it gave its two hundred millionth book. Dolly often says that she is proud to be called « the Book Lady ».

Source : texte d'entraînement écrit pour la classe, d'après les informations publiées par la Dollywood Foundation (imaginationlibrary.com).

Consigne : « Rendez compte en français du document. Aidez-vous des questions. »

  • Où et quand le projet a-t-il commencé ?
  • Que reçoivent les enfants, et à quel rythme ?
  • Pourquoi Dolly Parton a-t-elle lancé ce projet ?
  • Quelle est l'importance du projet aujourd'hui ?

Expression écrite. Choisissez un sujet. Écrivez 100 à 120 mots au moins.

  • Sujet 1 (vie quotidienne) : Which person (an artist, a sports player, a member of your family) is a model for you? Explain why.
  • Sujet 2 (vie professionnelle) : You work in a bookshop. A customer wants a book for a young child. Write an email to recommend one book and explain your choice.

Transcript (version prof seulement)

> When I look at Warhol's Dolly, I don't only see a star in colour. I see a woman who wrote songs about work, money and pride, and who built a park that employs people in her own county. People tell stories about her: the coat of many colours, the nine-to-five day, the rumour about a will. Some are true, some are tale. What I keep is this: a voice that came from a poor place and did not forget it, and a business that is also a way of giving work. A legacy is not only a museum. It is what still pays a wage, and what a younger artist can still sing.

Consigne : « Écoutez trois fois le document. Rendez compte en français de ce que vous avez compris : qui parle, de quoi, où, pourquoi, et ce que la personne pense. »

Corrigé (version prof). La locutrice, devant le portrait de Warhol, voit plus qu'une star : une femme qui a écrit sur le travail, l'argent et la fierté, et qui a créé un parc qui emploie des gens chez elle. Certaines histoires sont vraies, d'autres sont des légendes (le manteau de plusieurs couleurs, la journée de neuf à cinq, une rumeur de testament). Elle retient une voix venue de la pauvreté qui n'oublie pas d'où elle vient, et une entreprise qui donne encore du travail. Un héritage, ce n'est pas seulement un musée : c'est aussi un salaire, et une chanson qu'une plus jeune peut encore chanter.

Compréhension de l'écrit, CE

Texte d'entraînement (le jour du CCF, prendre un document authentique inconnu de 15 lignes au plus) :

> Dolly Parton is an American figure, and she is also a case study for a professional class. The Warhol portrait made her an icon. The songs told another story: a childhood without money, women at work, a voice that can be tender and sharp in the same verse. Dollywood is not only a theme park; it is an employer. When a rumour about an inheritance circulates, the professional question is the same as in any company: what is a fact, what is a tale, and how do you say so without trampling a person? A legacy, in this sequence, is what remains in culture and what remains in work.

Corrigé (version prof). Dolly est une figure américaine et un cas pour une classe professionnelle. Le portrait de Warhol en fait une icône ; les chansons racontent l'enfance, le travail des femmes, une voix tendre et tranchante. Dollywood est aussi un employeur. Face à une rumeur, il faut séparer le fait de la légende, sans écraser la personne. L'héritage est à la fois culturel et professionnel.

Expression écrite, PE

Choisissez un sujet. Écrivez 100 à 120 mots au moins.

  • Sujet 1, vie professionnelle : You work in the communication team of a heritage site or a park. Write a short text for visitors: who Dolly is, what people can see, and why the place also means jobs.
  • Sujet 2, vie quotidienne : Write about a song or an image from the sequence that struck you, and explain what it says about work or family.

Éléments de corrigé (version prof). Sujet 1 : présentation claire, un fait vrai, le lien emploi. Sujet 2 : un objet précis (Warhol, 9 to 5, Coat of Many Colors, Jolene), un avis. Attention à la rumeur : on ne la reprend pas comme un fait.
