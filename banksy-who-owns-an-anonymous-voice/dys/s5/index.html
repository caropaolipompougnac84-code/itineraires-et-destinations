Lesson 5. Does a mural lose its voice when it leaves its wall?

Activités langagières. Réception écrite, réception orale, médiation, production orale, interaction, production écrite.

The question today. If the place is part of the slogan, what remains when the image moves?

The gymnast in Irpin

Look at the girl with a ribbon. She seems to balance on a hole in a damaged building, in Irpin, in 2022. This is not the other gymnast, in Borodianka. In 2023 the wall was cut, in order to save the painting. The place and the image were then separated.

Worked example. Visible: the gymnast is painted above a dark hole. Interpretation: she may be using the hole as a ball. Not proven by the photograph alone: the exact cause of the hole, and the intention of the people who later moved the wall.

London, 2024

A goat stands on a real ledge, in Richmond. Pelicans interact with a real shop sign, in Walthamstow. If you move only the painted animal, you lose the ledge or the sign.

A child in Venice, painted in 2019 on a wall above the water. Later floods marked the work. The water is part of what the image says.

Grammar: if + present, will / may

Worked example. If the mural is removed, it may be safer. If the mural is removed, it will lose its place.

Exercise. Complete. 1. If the pelicans are taken off the shop, they _____ lose the fish on the sign. 2. The gymnast was cut out _____ protect her. (in order to / because of) 3. The image is striking _____ the hole beneath her. (in order to / because of)

Corrigé

1 will / may. 2 in order to. 3 because of.

Speak

Give a recommendation, 30 to 40 seconds:

If the mural is removed, it may... If it stays, it may... I think it should... in order to...

Homework

Choose one work. Complete a short card: place, what you can see, what the place adds, what would change if the work left its wall. Bring it to Lesson 6.
