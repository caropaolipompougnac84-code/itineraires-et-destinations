Lesson 1. Who will be the next 007?

I can say that no official actor has been announced.

Road map

Lesson of 55 minutes, timed for 52 minutes. Lesson 1 of 6. Minutes 52 to 55: no new task. Working at home and need longer? Split the lesson into two blocks: this is a classroom structure, not a speed test.

  1. Say one sentence from the previous lesson (Lesson 1: what you already know about spies or Bond) 4 min (minutes 0 to 4)
  2. Read the goal and the word box 4 min (minutes 4 to 8)
  3. Read or listen to your route’s text 8 min (minutes 8 to 16)
  4. Exercise 1, then check it 8 min (minutes 16 to 24)
  5. Pause: look away from the text or move 2 min (minutes 24 to 26)
  6. Exercise 2, then check it 8 min (minutes 26 to 34)
  7. Exercise 3, then check it 6 min (minutes 34 to 40)
  8. Say your final answer aloud with the speaking frame 4 min (minutes 40 to 44)
  9. Written record 4 min (minutes 44 to 48)
  10. Progress record 2 min (minutes 48 to 50)
  11. Read and write down the homework 2 min (minutes 50 to 52)
  • Written record. One sentence for your route (see below), copied or typed.
  • Participation. Say at least one answer aloud. In class, use a quiet voice; at home, say it to yourself. You do not need to record yourself.
  • Homework for next lesson. Make two flashcards: rumour and announce. Say your written record twice.

Stopping point. Minute 44: stop the main exercises. Keep the written record and the homework, even if an exercise is unfinished.

Emergency exit · 8 min. If only eight working minutes remain, this replaces the unfinished plan; it is not added to it.
  1. Minutes 0 to 2: say the emergency sentence of the lesson aloud.
  2. Minutes 2 to 5: complete the short written record.
  3. Minutes 5 to 7: write down the homework.
  4. Minute 7 to 8: check one answer with the key. Do not rush through all the unfinished exercises.

Word box

WordMeaning
rumoursomething people say, but it is not official
announcesay something officially
actora person who plays a role in a film
fictionalnot real; invented for a story

Choose your route: Route S · SEGPA · Route C · CAP · Route P · Bac pro · CFA. Do only your route.

Route S · SEGPA

Short text

The United Kingdom is waiting for a new James Bond.

James Bond is a film character. He is not a real spy.

The last Bond film came out in 2021.

The press names some actors.

On 11 November 2026, no official actor had been announced.

Exercise S1

Find information

Worked example. Question: Is James Bond a real spy? Answer: No. He is a film character.

Now you.
1. What is the United Kingdom waiting for?
2. When did the last Bond film come out?

Self-check

1. A new James Bond.
2. In 2021.

Exercise S2

Complete

Worked example. “James Bond is a film character.”

Now you.
Use: official, press.
1. The names some actors.
2. No actor had been announced.

Self-check

1. press.
2. official.

Exercise S3

Write and speak

Worked example. “The United Kingdom is waiting for a new Bond.”

Now you.
Complete: “No official actor has been .” Say your answer aloud.

Self-check

“No official actor has been announced.”

Route C · CAP

Short text

Daniel Craig played James Bond for the last time in 2021.

The United Kingdom is waiting for a seventh official James Bond.

The press often names Jack Lowden, Callum Turner and Josh O’Connor.

A rumour is not an official announcement.

On 11 November 2026, no actor had been officially announced.

Exercise C1

True or false

Worked example. “James Bond is a real agent.” False. He is a film character.

Now you.
1. Daniel Craig played Bond in 2021.
2. The press announcement is official.
3. On 11 November 2026, the new actor was already official.

Self-check

1. True.
2. False. The press names actors, but that is not official.
3. False.

Exercise C2

Fact or rumour?

Worked example. “The last Bond film came out in 2021.” This is a fact.

Now you.
1. “Jack Lowden is the next Bond.”
2. “No official actor had been announced on 11 November 2026.”

Self-check

1. A rumour, if no official announcement has been made.
2. A fact, according to the text.

Exercise C3

Write and speak

Worked example. “The press names some actors, but this is not official.”

Now you.
Write two sentences with: The press names… / However, no official…

Self-check

Possible answer: “The press names Jack Lowden, Callum Turner and Josh O’Connor. However, no official actor has been announced.” Other accurate answers are accepted.

Route P · Bac pro

Short text

Daniel Craig’s last Bond film, No Time to Die, came out in 2021.

The next official actor will be the seventh James Bond.

According to the BBC on 9 November 2026, an announcement is expected in November or December.

The three names most often cited as frontrunners remain Jack Lowden, Callum Turner and Josh O’Connor.

Other names also circulate. Nothing is confirmed until an official announcement is made.

A classroom vote can still take place: it is an opinion, not a result.

Exercise P1

Identify the main point

Worked example. “The press has already chosen the next Bond.” This is not the main point of the text.

Now you.
Choose the best answer.
A. The next Bond actor is officially known.
B. Reports name possible actors, but there is no official announcement yet.
C. The classroom vote decides the official actor.

Self-check

B.

Exercise P2

Qualify

Worked example. “Jack Lowden is the next Bond.” Improved: “The press often names Jack Lowden, but this is not official.”

Now you.
Improve:
1. “The BBC has chosen the actor.”
2. “Everyone knows who Bond will be.”

Self-check

Possible answers:
1. “The BBC reported that an announcement is expected.”
2. “Many names circulate, but no official actor has been announced.”

Exercise P3

Explain

Worked example. “A rumour is not the same thing as an official announcement.”

Now you.
Write 50 to 70 words. Use however. Say why a classroom vote is an opinion, not a result.

Self-check

Your answer should mention the press or the BBC, say that no official announcement had been made on 11 November 2026, and use however.

CFA pathway

CAP apprentice: use Route C. Bac pro apprentice: use Route P. Do Exercises 1 and 2 of your route, then do this task instead of Exercise 3. Keep the same written record and homework.

CFA task

Fact or rumour at work

Worked example. “The newspaper says a new manager will arrive.” This is a report. It is not official until the company announces it.

Now you.
Write one fact from the lesson and one rumour. Starter: “I know that . The press says , but this is not official.”

Self-check

The fact must be in the text (for example, no official actor on 11 November 2026). The rumour must be labelled as not official.

Written record

S: “No official actor has been announced.”

C: “The press names some actors, but this is not an official announcement.”

P: “Reports circulate, however no official Bond actor had been announced on 11 November 2026.”

Speaking frame. “The press names . However, no official actor has been .”

Emergency sentence. “No official actor has been announced.”

Homework

Make two flashcards: rumour and announce. Say your written record twice.

My progress record

  • I read or listened to the text.
  • I checked my answers.
  • I said at least one answer aloud.
  • I kept my written record.
  • I know the homework.
