Sales, distribution and logistics

Vocational field: VDL. Sixteen English words of the trade: tools, objects and everyday gestures. Open to everyone.

Four steps: from French to English, listen and choose, match, and complete a sentence at work. The Listen button reads the word in British English.

Teacher key: sentences at work
  1. Scan the ________ on the product. (barcode)
  2. Here is your ________, thank you. (receipt)
  3. Put the tins on the top ________. (shelf)
  4. Move the pallet with the ________. (pallet truck)
  5. The boxes are in the ________. (stockroom)
  6. Here is your ________: two euros. (change)

In steps 1, 2 and 3, the right answer is the line in the word list.
